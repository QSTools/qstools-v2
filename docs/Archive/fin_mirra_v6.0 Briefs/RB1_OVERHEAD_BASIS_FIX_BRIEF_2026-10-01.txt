RB-1 - RATE BUILDER OVERHEAD BASIS FIX
DATE: 2026-10-01
STATUS: BUILT, NOT YET LIVE-VERIFIED
SEQUENCING: done BEFORE smoke test Step E so the asset pass live-tests it.

1. THE DEFECT
Rate Builder's per-staff-type overhead recovery rows (build_rate_builder_
labour_recovery_rows) were wrong in two independent ways:
  (a) within-group distribution across staff types used labour COST share;
      Cost Allocation's within-group basis is HOURS.
  (b) 100% of a group's overhead was loaded onto labour, ignoring assets;
      Business Outcome splits labour/asset on cost share (F7).
Effect: the minimum_recoverable_charge_out_rate (the pricing FLOOR shown in
Rate Builder) was overstated for asset-bearing groups, and mis-weighted
between staff types of differing pay rates.

2. WHY NOT "JUST READ COST ALLOCATION'S NUMBER"
Considered and rejected. Cost Allocation produces a GROUP-level overhead
figure (assigned_overhead_amount, recovered-hours weighted). Nothing upstream
produces a per-staff-type figure, so Rate Builder must distribute within the
group. The fix is to make it distribute by the same rule, from one shared
definition - not to duplicate the rule a second time.

3. WHAT CHANGED
- NEW lib/calculations/shared/overheadSplit.js - split_group_overhead moved
  here verbatim from useBusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenue.js.
- hooks/useBusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenue.js - local copy deleted, imports
  the shared one. NO behaviour change intended.
- lib/selectors/cost-allocation/costAllocationRateBuilderSelectors.js -
  labour pool from the shared split; staff-type weight by hours, with cost
  weighting as fallback when a group reports no labour hours.
- lib/calculation-trace/pilot-map/rateBuilderTraceNodes.js - formula_text
  updated to describe the new rule.

4. NOT CHANGED (deliberate)
- The all_productive row (costAllocationRateBuilderSelectors.js) still
  derives its split from labour_share_percent, a DIFFERENT basis from the
  per-staff-type rows. Pre-existing inconsistency, logged as RB-1b, not
  folded in here so the diff stays verifiable.
- Business Outcome's numbers. This is the regression test.

5f. FINDINGS RAISED DURING THE ASSET PASS (2026-10-01, none fixed inline)
    LABOUR COVERAGE WARNING IS ONE-DIRECTIONAL.
    labour_coverage_warning fires when labour hours fall SHORT of the
    group's asset hours (seen at E.1: 1,414 labour vs 1,440 asset, warned
    at a 26 hour gap). It does not fire in the opposite direction. With
    both staff in one group - 2,828 labour hours against 1,440 asset hours,
    a surplus of about 1,388 hours - no warning fired and
    labour_coverage_gap_hours read 0. The surplus is visible in the numbers
    (Business Outcome achieved rates 156 and 187 per hour, roughly double
    the floors, because each person recovers only about half their paid
    hours) but nothing tells the user that is what they are looking at.

    WHY IT CANNOT SIMPLY BE MIRRORED (owner call, 2026-10-01).
    Surplus labour hours are only waste if the labour is tied to the asset.
    A factory machine operator is on that machine all day, so paid hours
    beyond the machine's running hours are genuine idle cost. Site labour
    is not: an operator may run a machine 20 hours a week and spend the
    rest on other recoverable work, where the same surplus is not waste at
    all. The tool cannot tell these apart from hours alone - it needs to
    know whether a labour assignment is machine-bound. That is a setting,
    not a calculation, and it overlaps the open asset-labour linking and
    unattended / dry-hire exemption items. Logged as a design question;
    do not write a mirrored warning until that input exists.

    TRACE PAGE HAD NO RATE BUILDER ROOT (fixed 2026-10-01).
    app/calculation-trace/page.jsx offered only Business Summary and
    Business Modelling roots, so the s5c capture route above could not be
    followed as written. Three options added:
    rate_builder.labour_rate.supervisor_team_lead / .operator_delivery_staff
    / .all_productive. Separately: the Download trace JSON button exports
    the WHOLE graph regardless of the selected root (buildTraceSnapshot is
    never passed the selection), so earlier captures were complete even
    without the option. One download per data state is enough. The three
    options are hard-coded to the current staff types; a new staff type
    needs another line until the list is built from the graph.

    RB-1b IS WIDENING.
    The all_productive row's labour overhead pool no longer matches the sum
    of the per-type rows: 4,061.64 against 3,989.90 at E.2 (gap 72.52),
    versus a gap of 56.43 at E.1. The gap grows as assets take a larger
    share, because all_productive still splits on cost mix while the
    per-type rows split on hours. Known issue, still open, but the
    headline weighted figure is the least trustworthy of the three.
