GENUINE DRIFT FOUND (both confirmed against live code, not assumed - see
the calc-trace brief's new Section 7 for full detail):
  1. Assets' old 7-piece itemized cost model (ownership/finance/lease/
     depreciation/insurance/registration/other) does not exist live.
     Today: total_asset_cost_annual = asset_interest_annual +
     running_cost_annual, and running_cost_annual is a hardcoded 0.
  2. What replaced it: those costs are now entered on General Overheads,
     auto-tagged into a shared pool, then assigned back to specific
     assets (allocated_asset_overhead_cost_annual). True per-asset cost
     = total_asset_cost_annual + allocated_asset_overhead_cost_annual.
     Confirmed with the owner this is the correct, expected mechanism.
  3. General Overheads' old "gross benchmark minus excluded amount"
     single-subtraction model does not exist live; each flagged row is
     reduced individually, then summed.
