1. THE PROBLEM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A machine hired out WITHOUT an operator (dry hire: Kennards, Hirepool) earns $0 in Business Outcome.
  - hooks\useBusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenue.js get_group_covered_hours (L276-284): for an asset-hours group,
    covered hours = MIN(group_recovery_hours, assigned labour hours). No labour -> 0 covered hours.
  - The asset row's revenue uses get_asset_revenue_hours(asset_hours, covered, required) (L198), so it is 0 too.
  - capacity_coverage_gap reports the full machine hours as unearned ("no_labour" type), and the summary prints
    "<unit> has no labour assigned ... nobody is there to run it" (BusinessOutcomeTruthSituationSummary.jsx ~L589).
  - The code comment above get_group_covered_hours already lists "unattended / dry-hire units need an exemption
    setting" as a known limit. VARIABLE_TRACKER s21 lists it as an open design item.
Seen live in Scenario B group G03 (80t crane 40 percent + telehandler): $0 revenue, "$285,600 a year of potential
revenue is not being counted". The aligned P&L therefore carries no dry-hire income.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. OWNER DECISIONS (2026-10-18)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  D-1  Dry hire must earn money. It is a normal product for a hire business, not a staffing gap.
  D-2  Labour: a dry-hire unit has no operator tied to the machine. Yardmen and mechanics are support staff and sit
       in support groups.
  D-3  Billing basis: HOURLY for now. Weekly and monthly hire come later (units mode, R4).
  D-4  Default OFF. Existing data and every current number stay exactly as they are until a user ticks it.
  D-5  Every new calculated figure gets a calculation trace node (definition of done, launch tracker A3). Essential.
  OPEN Placement of the switch. Owner considered Rate Builder (rate setup card), Cost Allocation's productive-asset
       section (next to assignment percent) and Selected Asset. Leaning Cost Allocation because it drives overhead
       distribution, and possibly tied to a particular asset rather than a whole unit.
       Claude's recommendation: a tick on the ASSET ASSIGNMENT row in Cost Allocation (next to assignment percent),
       e.g. "Hired out without our operator (dry hire / unattended)".
         - Not on the asset itself: Scenario B's 80t crane is operated hire in G01 and dry hire in G03 at the same time.
         - Not Rate Builder: it prices hours; it does not decide how many hours a machine earns on or how overhead is
           spread.
         - Not the whole unit: an assignment-level flag still lets one unit hold an operated machine and an
           unattended one (for example a pump left running next to an operated excavator).
       Confirm at the start of the build.

3. PROPOSED BEHAVIOUR WHEN TICKED (assignment level)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Storage   asset_group_assignments[] gains an optional boolean (name to agree, e.g. earns_without_operator).
            Absent = false (D-4). Check costAllocationStorage's normaliser spreads the row so the field survives
            (bug class b) - it is said to spread every group; verify for assignment rows.
  Cost Allocation (costAllocationGroupCostBuilder.js)
            required_asset_hours and the labour cap (D1 3c) are worked out over OPERATED assignments only.
            Exempt assignments add their own hours to group_recovery_hours without the labour cap, so overhead
            distribution follows the machine hours. labour_coverage_gap and idle labour ignore exempt hours.
            Read first: how a no-labour asset group's recovery hours are chosen today (A-2: MAX of asset hours,
            confirmed live in Scenario B) and whether the run pattern (together / sequential) applies.
  Business Outcome (useBusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenue.js)
            Exempt asset row revenue = its rate x its own hours (no covered-hours share). Operated rows unchanged.
            capacity_coverage_gap skips exempt assignments; a unit whose assets are all exempt gets no "no_labour" line.
            Cascades (View A, View B) unchanged in formula; they simply receive the new revenue.
  Wording   The "nobody is there to run it" sentence must not fire for exempt assets. Consider one plain line in Cost
            Allocation explaining the tick.
  Rate Builder  No change expected (hourly calculator per asset already exists, Path B). Confirm the asset calculator
            rate is what the exempt row bills at.
  Business Modelling  Levers price on covered hours via group_recovery_hours; check exempt hours flow through.
