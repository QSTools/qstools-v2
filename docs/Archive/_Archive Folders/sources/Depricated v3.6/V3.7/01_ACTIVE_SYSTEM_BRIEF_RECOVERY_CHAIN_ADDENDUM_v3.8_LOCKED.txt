# QS Tools — Active System Brief Addendum

## v3.8 — RECOVERY CHAIN CONTRACT ADDENDUM

**STATUS:** ADDENDUM TO 01_ACTIVE_SYSTEM_BRIEF_v3.8_LOCKED.txt
**VERSION:** v3.8
**PURPOSE:** Add explicit Recovery Summary → Cost Allocation → Business Outcome contract language to the existing Active System Brief.

This is not a full replacement for `01_ACTIVE_SYSTEM_BRIEF_v3.8_LOCKED.txt`.

Keep the existing Active System Brief and add this as a supporting addendum.

---

# 1. WHY THIS ADDENDUM EXISTS

The Active System Brief v3.8 already includes the correct system flow:

```text
Business Summary
↓
Recovery Summary
↓
Cost Allocation
↓
Business Outcome
```

This addendum locks the more detailed separation required for the Recovery Chain build.

---

# 2. LOCKED RECOVERY CHAIN PRINCIPLE

The recovery chain must follow this separation:

```text
Business Summary = current reality
Recovery Summary = chosen strategy + visible consequence
Cost Allocation = delivery structure + dependency
Business Outcome = verdict
```

Core product rule:

```text
You can choose anything, but you cannot hide from what it causes.
```

This rule applies across Recovery Summary, Cost Allocation, and Business Outcome.

---

# 3. BUSINESS SUMMARY HANDOFF

Business Summary provides the current-reality baseline.

It must expose:

```js
business_type
activity_driver_type
activity_driver_value
required_recovery_per_driver
current_margin_per_driver
recovery_gap_per_driver
margin_pool
total_cost_burden
net_position
model_trust_state
business_summary_status
business_summary_warnings
```

Business Summary does not decide recovery strategy.

Business Summary does not calculate allocation status.

Business Summary does not produce the final verdict.

---

# 4. RECOVERY SUMMARY ROLE

Recovery Summary owns the selected recovery strategy and visible consequence.

It consumes Business Summary outputs.

It must expose:

```js
recovery_plan_target_per_driver
recovery_plan_split
component_required_recovery
active_recovery_model
labour_share_percent
asset_share_percent
overhead_share_percent
labour_recovery_cost
asset_recovery_cost
overhead_absorbed_cost
required_labour_recovery_rate
required_asset_recovery
```

Recovery Summary must not:

```text
rebuild Business Summary
rebuild Cost Summary
rebuild Revenue / COGS
calculate allocation_status
calculate business_outcome_status
optimise recovery mix automatically
```

---

# 5. COST ALLOCATION ROLE

Cost Allocation owns delivery structure, capacity visibility, and dependency status.

It consumes Recovery Summary outputs.

It must expose:

```js
allocation_status
allocation_dependency_type
allocation_warnings
active_recovery_model
recovery_plan_target_per_driver
recovery_plan_split
component_required_recovery
structure_valid
staff_coverage_percent
asset_coverage_percent
group_coverage_percent
external_delivery_enabled
external_delivery_required
internal_capacity_shortfall
```

Allowed `allocation_status` values:

```js
"ready"
"ready_with_dependency"
"strained"
"not_supported"
```

Critical rule:

```text
Not enough internal capacity is not automatic failure. It is a dependency signal.
```

Cost Allocation must distinguish:

```text
internal constrained capacity
external scalable delivery
```

Cost Allocation must not:

```text
change recovery strategy
recalculate Recovery Summary
produce the final business verdict
optimise the model
price quotes
```

---

# 6. BUSINESS OUTCOME ROLE

Business Outcome owns the verdict.

It consumes Recovery Summary and Cost Allocation outputs.

It must expose:

```js
business_outcome_status
outcome_status
outcome_title
outcome_message
primary_constraint_key
primary_constraint_title
primary_constraint_message
recommended_action
allocation_status
allocation_dependency_type
net_position
recovery_gap_per_driver
dependency_level
business_model_health
decision_warnings
```

Allowed `business_outcome_status` values:

```js
"viable"
"viable_with_dependency"
"at_risk"
"not_viable"
```

Business Outcome must not:

```text
recalculate Recovery Summary
recalculate Cost Allocation
change recovery strategy
change allocation structure
optimise scenarios
price quotes
perform feedback-loop analysis
```

---

# 7. TRUST INHERITANCE

Recovery Summary, Cost Allocation, and Business Outcome must inherit the upstream trust state.

If Model Readiness or Business Summary is blocked:

```text
Recovery Summary may display preview only.
Cost Allocation may display preview only.
Business Outcome must not present a final trusted verdict.
```

If Model Readiness is warning:

```text
Warnings must remain visible through the recovery chain.
```

---

# 9. FINAL ADDENDUM DEFINITION

The Active System Brief remains the central architecture file.

This addendum locks the Recovery Chain handoff:

```text
Current reality → Strategy and consequence → Delivery dependency → Verdict
```

No downstream module may calculate what an upstream module already owns.

No hidden assumptions.

No fantasy modelling.

Every choice must show its consequence.
