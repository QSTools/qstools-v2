# QS Tools — Business Summary v3.8 Recovery Chain Addendum

## STATUS: ADDENDUM TO 20_BUSINESS_SUMMARY_v3.8_LOCKED

**Purpose:** Add a clear downstream handoff section so Recovery Summary, Cost Allocation, and Business Outcome consume Business Summary correctly without duplicating logic or drifting into hidden assumptions.

This addendum does not replace the Business Summary v3.8 brief.

It extends it for the Recovery Chain build.

---

# 1. WHY THIS ADDENDUM IS NEEDED

The current Business Summary v3.8 brief is already correctly positioned as the factual current-business mirror.

It already defines the key activity-driver outputs needed downstream:

```js
activity_driver_type
activity_driver_value
required_recovery_per_driver
current_margin_per_driver
recovery_gap_per_driver
margin_pool
total_cost_burden
net_position
```

The Recovery Chain now needs these outputs to be treated as the locked handoff from Business Summary.

Business Summary must remain the final current-reality layer before strategy begins.

---

# 2. LOCKED RECOVERY CHAIN PRINCIPLE

The recovery chain must follow this separation:

```text
Business Summary = current reality
Recovery Summary = chosen strategy + visible consequence
Cost Allocation = delivery structure + dependency
Business Outcome = verdict
```

Core rule:

```text
You can choose anything, but you cannot hide from what it causes.
```

Business Summary provides the reality side of that rule.

Recovery Summary provides the choice and consequence side.

---

# 3. BUSINESS SUMMARY MUST NOT BECOME RECOVERY SUMMARY

Business Summary must not:

```text
allow recovery strategy inputs
store recovery model choices
calculate recovery shares
optimise recovery mix
recommend recovery strategy
calculate allocation status
calculate dependency status
calculate business outcome status
```

Business Summary only shows:

```text
where the business is now
what the current pressure is
what the current gap is
```

---

---

# 5. REALITY BASELINE RULE

Business Summary is the baseline reality layer.

The baseline is not a historical P&L ratio.

The baseline is:

```text
required recovery per driver
current margin per driver
recovery gap per driver
margin pool
total cost burden
net position
```

This distinction is critical.

P&L history is processed upstream into Revenue / COGS and Cost Summary.

Business Summary receives those processed outputs and converts them into current commercial reality.

Recovery Summary must not go back to raw history.

---

# 6. NO HISTORY DIRECTLY INTO RECOVERY SUMMARY

Business Summary may be informed by historical P&L through upstream modules.

Recovery Summary must not consume raw history directly.

Correct flow:

```text
P&L history
↓
Revenue / COGS + Cost Summary
↓
Business Summary current reality
↓
Recovery Summary strategy and consequence
```

Incorrect flow:

```text
Raw P&L history
↓
Recovery Summary strategy
```

That would create duplicated logic and hidden assumptions.

---

# 7. CURRENT REALITY DISPLAY REQUIREMENT

Business Summary should make these three values obvious enough that Recovery Summary can use them as the current-reality anchor:

```text
Required recovery per hour/unit
Current margin per hour/unit
Recovery gap per hour/unit
```

Simple test:

```text
Can the user clearly say:
"I need $X per hour/unit, I am currently getting $Y, and the gap is $Z"?
```

If not, Business Summary needs to be fixed before Recovery Summary is built.

---

# 8. RECOVERY SUMMARY HANDOFF STATEMENT

Add this wording to the Business Summary brief under Relationship To Recovery Summary:

```text
Business Summary is the current-reality baseline for Recovery Summary.

It does not decide how recovery should happen.

It exposes the required recovery per driver, current margin per driver, and recovery gap per driver so Recovery Summary can show the consequence of recovery strategy choices.

Recovery Summary must consume these outputs and must not rebuild Business Summary calculations.
```

---

# 9. DOWNSTREAM CONTRACT PROTECTION

Recovery Summary must not recalculate these Business Summary-owned values:

```js
activity_driver_type
activity_driver_value
required_recovery_per_driver
current_margin_per_driver
recovery_gap_per_driver
margin_pool
total_cost_burden
net_position
```

If Recovery Summary needs them, Business Summary must expose them.

If they are missing, fix Business Summary first.

Do not rebuild them downstream.

---

# 10. PRODUCT MODE NOTE

In product-based mode, Business Summary must use:

```js
units_sold_annual
```

as the primary activity driver.

Legacy hour fields may remain exposed for Business Modelling compatibility, but Recovery Summary must not use them as the primary driver in product-based mode.

---

# 11. LABOUR MODE NOTE

In labour-based mode, Business Summary must use:

```js
total_productive_output
```

as the primary activity driver.

This is the denominator for:

```js
required_recovery_per_driver
current_margin_per_driver
recovery_gap_per_driver
```

---

Business Summary is ready for Recovery Summary when it exposes:

```js
recovery_gap_per_driver
activity_driver_type
activity_driver_value
margin_pool
total_cost_burden

business_summary_status
model_trust_state
```

And the page clearly displays:

```text
required per driver
current per driver
gap per driver
```

If any of those are missing, do not build Recovery Summary yet.

---

# 13. FINAL ADDENDUM DEFINITION

Business Summary is the locked current-reality baseline for the Recovery Chain.

It shows what the business currently produces, what the business costs, and the recovery pressure through the selected activity driver.

It does not decide strategy.

It does not optimise recovery.

It does not test delivery structure.

It does not produce the verdict.

It hands a clean reality contract to Recovery Summary so the next page can show:

```text
Reality → Strategy → Consequence
```

without rebuilding upstream maths.
