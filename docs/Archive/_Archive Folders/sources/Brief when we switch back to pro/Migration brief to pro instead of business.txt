### C. Labour Recovery & Margin Pool Standard added

Created:

```text
docs/V3.6 Source Files/17_LABOUR_RECOVERY_AND_MARGIN_POOL_STANDARD_v3.6_LOCKED.txt
```

This locks one of the most important product insights discovered in the session.

Key insight:

```text
A fixed-price quote can show gross margin and still fail to recover internal labour, overheads, assets, and target profit.
```

Core fixed-price recovery test:

```text
Revenue
- external/direct costs
= margin / recovery pool
```

Then:

```text
margin / recovery pool
- required internal labour recovery
- required general overhead recovery
- required asset ownership recovery
- target profit
= recovery surplus / shortfall
```

If the recovery surplus / shortfall is negative, the quote is commercially loss-making against the model, even if a simple gross-margin view looks acceptable.

This was identified as likely one of the main hidden leaks in quote-based businesses.

## 4. WIP treatment clarified and implemented

WIP was a major conceptual issue.

Conclusion:

```text
WIP is not automatically General Overheads.
WIP is not automatically COGS.
WIP is not automatically excluded.
WIP is an accounting/timing adjustment until explicitly resolved.
```

WIP must default to:

```text
category: review_required
review_subcategory: wip_accounting_adjustment
```

Allowed WIP treatments:

```text
Leave as Review Required
Exclude from QS Cost Model
Include as COGS / Direct Job Cost
Treat as Income / Revenue Timing Adjustment
```

A WIP decision guide was added to the P&L UI to explain this.

Important business reasoning:

WIP in Xero/accounting can be used to move profit/loss across periods for tax/accounting timing. It cannot be blindly used as a direct QS cost without understanding the underlying source detail.

## 15. Important technical rules currently locked

### P&L

```text
P&L is raw financial input and classification.
P&L benchmark values flow to Module Reconciliation only.
P&L values must not feed Cost Summary calculations directly.
```

### General Overheads

```text
General Overheads owns total_general_overheads.
Only total_general_overheads may flow downstream.
Staff overheads are a category inside General Overheads, not a separate module.
Vehicle running costs belong in General Overheads, not Assets.
```

### Labour

```text
Labour owns salary/wages, employer KiwiSaver, ESCT, employer ACC, entitlements, productive hours.
Labour does not own staff overheads.
```

### Assets

```text
Assets owns ownership-only asset costs:
finance
lease
depreciation
ownership / replacement recovery if explicitly enabled

Assets does not own:
fuel
servicing
repairs
maintenance
registration
licensing
tyres
vehicle running costs
```

### WIP

```text
WIP defaults to Review Required.
WIP must not auto-feed COGS, General Overheads, Assets, Labour, Income, or Excluded.
```

### Fixed-price recovery

```text
Revenue
- external/direct costs
= margin / recovery pool
```

Then:

```text
margin / recovery pool
- required internal labour recovery
- required general overhead recovery
- required asset ownership recovery
- target profit
= recovery surplus / shortfall
```
