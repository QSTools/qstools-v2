---

# 1. PURPOSE

Revenue Summary is a real implemented QS Tools module.

It exists as a standalone route and supporting calculation layer.

Its purpose is to summarise revenue, direct cost, material cost, labour-related COG, operating expenses, target profit assumptions, and target mix assumptions before those values are consumed by downstream review, modelling, dashboard, quote checking, and AI export layers.

Revenue Summary is part of the revenue truth side of the system.

It is a peer support layer alongside Cost Summary and Business Summary.
