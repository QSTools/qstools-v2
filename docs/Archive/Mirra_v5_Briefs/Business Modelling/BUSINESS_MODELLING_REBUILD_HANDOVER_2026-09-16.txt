2. THE REBUILD - WHY, AND WHAT'S DECIDED
===============================================================================

**The problem:** Business Modelling's lever card has always opened with
"Your business is currently making $X in net profit" - but $X was built
from the INDEPENDENT view (every source judged alone, no cross-subsidy),
not the REAL reconciled figure. Before the materials fix, this was
disguised because Materials silently acted as a residual that forced the
total to land close to reality. After the fix, at real $0 breakeven, the
independent view showed $767,271 - the size of the cross-subsidy
currently hidden in the rate structure, NOT a real profit number.

**The decision (confirmed 2026-09-16, user's own words):** Business
Modelling needs TWO clearly separate sections:
  - **Section A - real current state.** "Where are we right now" - must
    match Business Outcome exactly. NOT YET BUILT.
  - **Section B - model a fix.** The existing independent lever engine,
    kept as-is, used for "what if I change this rate" - correctly
    independent, that's the whole point of modelling. ALREADY BUILT
    (BusinessModellingLeverCard.jsx, unchanged by this decision).

**[DECIDED] Which view is "real" for Section A:** View A, "How the
Business Runs" - per_source.headline_real_capacity. NOT "Each Part On
Its Own" (same total, different per-source credit/blame - a different
lens) and NOT View B (a separate materials-only fairness question).
headline_real_capacity is built via apply_real_capacity()'s two-phase
cascade and is the exact figure Business Outcome's own CrossCheck
confirms matches real P&L cost to the cent.

**[DECIDED] Baseline (in the Upside/Downside redesign) switches to the
same real figure** - per_source.headline_real_capacity - instead of its
current source (buildLiveLeverHeadline with empty levers, i.e. the
independent view). User's rationale: "the baseline is Business Outcome -
that's what the actual business is doing, which is the entire point of
the tool." THIS CHANGES ALREADY-SHIPPED, ALREADY-VERIFIED BEHAVIOUR -
the current $630,568 baseline was built from the independent view and
WILL change once this is implemented. Must be re-verified against real
data when built, not assumed to still read $630,568.

**Nothing in Business Modelling's own files is "leave untouched"
anymore** - specifically BusinessModellingLeverCard.jsx and
businessModellingIndependentCalculations.js, previously on that list per
BUSINESS_OUTCOME_FILE_MAP_v5_1.txt Section 7. That status is REVOKED for
these two files only. The other three files on that list
(SidebarNavigation.jsx, the S22 brief, generalOverheadCategorySelectors.js)
are unrelated and remain untouched.
