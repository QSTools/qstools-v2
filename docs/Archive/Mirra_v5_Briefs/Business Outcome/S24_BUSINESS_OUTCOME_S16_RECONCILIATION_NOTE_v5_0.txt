# Mirra v5.0 - Business Outcome: S16 Reconciliation Note

STATUS: RECONCILIATION NOTE - AMENDS S16_BUSINESS_OUTCOME_BUILD_BRIEF_v5.0
VERSION: v5.0
MODULE TYPE: Business Outcome / Commercial Truth Layer
DATE: 2026-08-15
AUTHORITY: Supersedes Section 2, Section 3, and Section 4 of
S16_BUSINESS_OUTCOME_BUILD_BRIEF_v5.0 (dated 2026-07-15) where they conflict
with this note. All other sections of S16 remain in force.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This note formally updates S16's source chain decision. S16 is not being
discarded, it is being amended in the one place it conflicts with the design
locked down in S23_BUSINESS_OUTCOME_NET_PROFIT_STREAM_UNPACKING_BUILD_BRIEF_v5_0.

---

# 2. WHAT S16 SAID

S16 Section 3 ("Legacy Conflict Resolution") classified Cost Allocation,
Recovery Summary, Rate Builder, and Recovery Outcome as legacy Recovery Chain
routes, and instructed Business Outcome not to depend on them primarily
unless a future compatibility layer explicitly mapped them into the v5.0
contracts.

---

# 3. WHAT CHANGES

Cost Allocation and Rate Builder are hereby reactivated as required, primary,
named inputs to Business Outcome. They are no longer treated as legacy
Recovery Chain routes for the purposes of Business Outcome.

Reason: Business Outcome's core function - unpacking the blended P&L Gross
Profit and Net Profit figures into true material, labour, and asset streams
- is not possible without them. Cost Allocation supplies the overhead
distribution into labour and asset pools (and the residual pool that lands
on material). Rate Builder supplies the labour and asset charge-out rates
that make up revenue. Routing around them, as S16 Section 3 instructed,
would make the feature defined in S23 unbuildable.

Recovery Summary and Recovery Outcome are NOT reactivated by this note. They
remain legacy Recovery Chain routes per S16 unless a separate note addresses
them. Only Cost Allocation and Rate Builder are affected.

---

# 4. UPDATED SOURCE CHAIN

S16 Section 4 ("System Role") and Section 5 ("Required Input Contracts") are
amended. Business Outcome's current v5.0 source chain is:

Input Modules
→ Cost Summary
→ Revenue / COG truth
→ Revenue Summary
→ Business Summary / Revenue Reality / Asset Reality where available
→ Cost Allocation (reactivated)
→ Rate Builder (reactivated)
→ Business Outcome
→ Business Modelling
→ AI-readable Business State Export
→ Business Overview Dashboard
→ Quote Checker

Full input field detail for Cost Allocation and Rate Builder is specified in
S23, Section 11, and should be treated as the authoritative contract for
those two dependencies going forward.

---

# 5. WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE

- S16's output_contract fields (Section 6) remain valid and are extended, not
  replaced, by S23 Section 12.
- S16's boundaries (Section 10) remain in force and are extended by S23
  Section 13.
- S16's relationship to Business Modelling, AI Export, and Dashboard
  (Sections 11-13) remain unchanged.
- S16's classification of Recovery Summary and Recovery Outcome as legacy is
  unchanged.

---

# 6. STATUS OF S16 GOING FORWARD

S16 remains a valid document. Its Section 2 ("Primary Decision") and Section
3 ("Legacy Conflict Resolution") should be read as amended by this note
wherever they name Cost Allocation or Rate Builder. Future readers of S16
should read it alongside this note and S23, not in isolation.

---

# 7. FINAL PRINCIPLE

Briefs are not permanently locked against evolution, they are locked against
silent drift. This note documents the change openly, states the reason, and
keeps both the old and new positions traceable, rather than overwriting S16
without a record.

END OF NOTE
