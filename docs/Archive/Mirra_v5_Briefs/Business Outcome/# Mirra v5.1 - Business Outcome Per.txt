# Mirra v5.1 - Business Outcome: Per-Source Revenue Attribution Brief

STATUS: BUILD BRIEF - FOR REVIEW
VERSION: v5.1
MODULE TYPE: Business Outcome / Commercial Truth Layer
DATE: 2026-08-27
AUTHORITY: Extends S23_BUSINESS_OUTCOME_NET_PROFIT_STREAM_UNPACKING_BUILD_
BRIEF_v5_0 to per-source granularity, following on directly from building
the waterfall (S23's three-stream view) and finding its "by operating
group" section unreliable.

---

# 1. WHY THIS EXISTS

The waterfall (S23, built and reconciled this session) proves the business
is viable in total. It does not show which part of the business is
producing that result and which part is being carried by the rest.

Testing the waterfall's "by operating group" section against the already-
verified Labour Recovery card (built and debugged earlier this session)
surfaced a real conflict: Foreman showed a $11,796 loss in the group split,
while every labour source showed as recovering in the Labour Recovery card.
Traced to source: the group split prices labour using Rate Builder's
"active model" rate (calculate_weighted_summary_charge_out_rate, keyed off
active_model) - the exact same stale-rate pattern already found and fixed
in the Labour Recovery card earlier this session (real rates are not
stored per-model; the true source of record is the flat per-source map,
readRateBuilderLabourSourceRates()). The group split's headline totals
were never fixed to use that correction - only the Labour Recovery card
was.

The real need underneath this: you cannot model how to fix the business if
you cannot see what is producing its revenue and what is not. This brief
defines that view properly, rather than patching the group split's rate
source in isolation.

---

# 2. THE PRINCIPLE

Every dollar of revenue traces to what earned it. Every dollar of cost,
including its fair share of overhead, lands against whichever revenue-
producing source it actually belongs to. The sum of every source's
individual net profit equals total net profit exactly - the reverse-proof
of the P&L, now at source granularity rather than three lump totals.

This is S23's Section 3 reconciliation anchor, applied one level deeper:
not just material/labour/asset, but Foreman/Site Crew/Owner-Director/
PC55/2inc Line/materials - whatever the real revenue-producing sources
actually are - each shown with its own revenue, true fully-loaded cost,
and verdict: paying its way, or being carried.

---

# 3. WHAT "SOURCE" MEANS HERE

A source is anything Cost Allocation already treats as a cost pool with a
real charge-out mechanism:

- A labour source type (Operator/Delivery Staff, Owner/Director, Senior
  Operator/Senior Staff, Site Crew, Foreman, etc.) - the same granularity
  already correctly used by the Labour Recovery card.
- A productive asset or asset group with a Rate Builder calculator linked
  (PC55, 2inc Line, etc.).
- Materials/COG as a whole (not per-source, since materials don't have
  Cost Allocation groups - see Section 5).

This is NOT the same as a Cost Allocation "operating group." An operating
group can currently mix multiple labour types and/or assets together
(confirmed in businessOutcomeAssetSplitCalculations.js - a group can have
hasLabour and hasAsset both true, with a labourAssignments array of
multiple staff types inside one group). The per-source view must attribute
revenue and cost at the SOURCE level (staff type / asset), not the group
level, then optionally roll sources back up to their group for display if
useful - not the other way around.

---

# 4. PER-SOURCE REVENUE AND COST

For each labour source:
- revenue = true_cost_per_hour... no - revenue = charge_out_rate (real,
  per-source, from readRateBuilderLabourSourceRates() - the CORRECTED
  source, same as Labour Recovery card) x that source's real recovery
  hours (from Cost Allocation, per source, not per group)
- true cost = real labour cost (Labour module, per staff type) + that
  source's allocated overhead (Cost Allocation, per source - already
  correctly computed, this is exactly what the Labour Recovery card
  already shows as true_cost_per_hour)
- net profit = revenue - true cost
- verdict = paying its way (net profit >= 0) or being carried (net profit
  < 0)

This is almost entirely already-built, already-verified logic - it is the
Labour Recovery card's own true_cost_per_hour and charge_out_rate, just
multiplied out to annual dollars via real hours instead of shown per-hour.
The bug was never in the Labour Recovery card's numbers; it was in the
waterfall's separate, older, uncorrected group-split calculation duplicating
the same job with the wrong rate source.

For each productive asset/asset source:
- revenue = Rate Builder calculator's charged amount for that asset's
  actual usage (existing calculateRateBuilderQuotePreview logic, already
  used correctly in businessOutcomeAssetSplitCalculations.js for assets -
  this part was not found to be broken, only the labour side was)
- true cost = real asset cost (Assets module) + that asset's allocated
  overhead (Cost Allocation)
- net profit = revenue - true cost
- verdict = same as labour

For materials/COG (whole, not per-source):
- revenue = material sales revenue (Revenue/COG truth)
- true cost = true COGS + residual/unassigned overhead (whatever Cost
  Allocation did not distribute to labour or asset pools - S23 Section 7)
- net profit = revenue - true cost
- verdict = same as labour/asset

---

# 5. THE RECONCILIATION ANCHOR (MANDATORY)

Sum of every source's revenue = total_revenue (Business Summary).
Sum of every source's net profit = net_position (Business Summary).

If these do not reconcile exactly, the variance must be surfaced as an
explicit, visible remainder - never hidden, never rounded away, never
silently absorbed into one source to force a match. Same non-negotiable
rule as S23 Section 3 and the waterfall's existing reconciliation banner.

Known open question requiring a decision before build: unassigned labour/
asset cost (real cost that exists but is not attributed to any Cost
Allocation group) and residual/undistributed overhead both need a home in
this reconciliation. Options: (a) their own explicit "Unassigned" line,
never folded into a named source, or (b) residual overhead only folds into
materials per S23 Section 7, unassigned labour/asset cost gets its own
line regardless. Recommend (b) for consistency with the already-locked S23
decision on overhead, decision needed on unassigned labour/asset cost
specifically.

---

# 6. WHAT THIS REPLACES

The waterfall's current "by operating group" section (BusinessOutcomeWaterfallCard.jsx's SplitGroupRow rendering of labourSplitRows/assetSplitRows) is replaced by this per-source view. The waterfall's TOP-LEVEL
rows (Revenue through Net Profit) are unaffected and stay as built - they
never depended on the flat rate, only the by-group breakdown did.

The existing Labour Recovery card is NOT replaced. Its true_cost_per_hour/
charge_out_rate numbers are the verified foundation this view is built on.
Whether the Labour Recovery card and this new view end up as one combined
card or two separate ones showing the same underlying numbers at different
granularity (per-hour rate vs annual dollar contribution) is a display
decision for build time, not a data decision - they must never disagree,
since they will be built from the same corrected source.

---

# 7. WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER (OUT OF SCOPE, THIS BRIEF)

- Fixing Rate Builder's "active model" concept itself, or removing it -
  only ensuring Business Outcome never reads through it for real rates.
  The active-model rate may still be legitimate for whatever Rate Builder
  itself uses it for; this brief only concerns Business Outcome's sourcing.
- Asset per-source revenue attribution when no Rate Builder calculator is
  linked (existing "No Rate Builder calculator linked to this group"
  reason/unavailable state carries over unchanged for those cases).
- Live utilisation data - full utilisation assumption still applies,
  disclosed the same way as the waterfall (S23 Section 6).

---

# 8. DEFINITION OF DONE

- Every labour source, asset source, and materials/COG shown with real
  revenue, real fully-loaded true cost, and a paying-its-way verdict
- Every number traces to an already-verified source (Labour Recovery
  card's true_cost_per_hour/charge_out_rate for labour, existing Rate
  Builder calculator logic for assets, Business Summary/Revenue-COG truth
  for materials) - no re-introduction of the "active model" rate path
- Sum of all sources' revenue reconciles to total_revenue exactly, sum of
  all sources' net profit reconciles to net_position exactly, any variance
  surfaced explicitly, never hidden
- Unassigned labour/asset cost and residual overhead each have an explicit,
  visible home in the reconciliation, not folded silently into a named
  source
- Old "by operating group" flat-rate section removed from
  BusinessOutcomeWaterfallCard.jsx once this replaces it

---

# 9. FINAL PRINCIPLE

You cannot model how to fix the business if you cannot see what is
producing its revenue and what is not. This is the actual point of
Business Outcome - not a viability certificate, but a map of which parts
of the business are paying their way and which parts are being carried,
built entirely from numbers already proven correct elsewhere in this
session, never from a rate source already known to be unreliable.
