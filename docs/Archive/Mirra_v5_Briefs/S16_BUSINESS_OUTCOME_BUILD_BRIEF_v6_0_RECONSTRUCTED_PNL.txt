BUSINESS OUTCOME — BUILD BRIEF v6.0
Reconstructed P&L / Cross-Subsidy Statement
(Supersedes the "Recovery Pressure Map" framing in S16 v5.0)

=====================================================================
1. PURPOSE — WHY THIS MODULE EXISTS
=====================================================================

The accounting P&L is a historical record. Revenue minus expenses,
full stop. It cannot tell you WHY a number is what it is — only THAT
it is. The only advice an accounting P&L can ever produce is
"charge more, reduce overheads," because it has no internal structure
that separates who earned what.

Business Outcome exists to answer a different question:

  "Given what this business actually costs, and what it's actually
   charging (via Rate Builder), where does Net Profit really come
   from — and which part of the business is silently funding another
   part?"

It is a RECONSTRUCTED P&L, not a new set of numbers. Revenue, General
Overheads, and Net Profit must always tie back to the same figures as
the original P&L / Business Summary. What's new is the decomposition:
each cost stream (Productive Labour, Non-Productive Labour, Productive
Assets, Non-Productive Assets, General Overheads) is shown standing on
its own — proven to either fund itself or be carried by material
margin.

BASELINE ASSUMPTION (Phase 1): the business is assumed to charge 100%
of labour/asset cost via Rate Builder rates. This is a deliberate
baseline, not a claim about current reality — the same principle as
"if you're not charging 100% of what your staff cost, you are by
definition funding that gap out of material margin, whether you
intended to or not." Business Outcome makes that funding visible.

PHASE 2 (future, not yet built): once live timekeeping, invoicing, and
asset utilisation data exist, the same structure gets fed real
"charged" figures instead of the 100%-baseline assumption. The
calculation shape does not change — only the inputs upgrade from
modelled to actual. This is what makes it a CONTROL system rather
than a report: the structure exists before the data does.

DOWNSTREAM CONSUMER: Business Modelling (useBusinessModelling.js)
currently models against Business Summary (the accounting-framework
view). Once Business Outcome is stable, Business Modelling should
consume Business Outcome instead — so "what if I raise my labour rate
by $5/hr" can show whether that stops labour cross-subsidising
material, not just move an abstract P&L line.


=====================================================================
2. WHAT THIS MODULE IS / IS NOT
=====================================================================

IS:
- A reconstructed P&L showing where Net Profit actually comes from
- A cross-subsidy statement: which cost streams are self-funding vs
  carried by material margin
- A decision-support / diagnostic layer
- Honest about what it doesn't know yet (asset utilisation, live
  actuals) — shows "not available" rather than a fabricated number

IS NOT:
- Actual performance tracking (that's Phase 2, with real data)
- Job costing or invoicing
- A pricing engine
- Using Cost Allocation for financial totals (Cost Allocation is
  structure/coverage confidence only — see field map below)

  support_asset_assigned_overhead_cost_annual

--- useCostSummary(inputs) ---
Default export from hooks/useCostSummary.js
DOES NOT SELF-SOURCE DATA. Takes inputs.labour, inputs.assets,
inputs.general_overheads, inputs.opening_hours, inputs.model_readiness
and only SHAPES whatever it's given. Calling useCostSummary() with no
arguments returns all-zero cost fields — this caused a real bug
earlier in this build (labour/asset/overhead all showing $0). Do not
call this hook directly from Business Outcome; use Recovery Summary's
pass-through instead, which already wires it correctly.

4. CALCULATION LOGIC
=====================================================================

--- A. Pressure Map (supporting detail) ---

Labour:
  model_recovery_capacity = weighted_summary.weighted_modelled_labour_revenue
                             (only if summary_complete === true)
  gap = labour_recovery_cost - model_recovery_capacity
  status = gap > 0 ? 'pressure' : 'sufficient'
           (or 'rate_builder_incomplete' if capacity unavailable)

Assets:
  status = 'asset_recovery_pressure' (always — no Asset Rate Builder
           exists yet, so capacity can never be calculated. This is
           intentional, not a bug to "fix" until an asset charge-out
           mechanism exists.)

Overheads:
  gap = total_business_overheads - overhead_absorbed_cost
  status = gap > 0 ? 'unrecovered' : 'covered'

--- B. Reconstructed Waterfall (primary output) ---

  Revenue
  − Productive Labour Charged        (Rate Builder capacity)
  − COG (total_direct_costs)
  = Margin

  − Productive Labour Shortfall      (productive_labour_cost - charged
                                       — CAN BE NEGATIVE, meaning a
                                       SURPLUS. Label/colour this
                                       correctly: negative shortfall =
                                       green "Surplus (adds to
                                       profit)", not a red deduction.)
  − Non-Productive Labour Cost       (total_non_productive_labour_cost
                                       — never chargeable by
                                       definition, always fully
                                       carried by margin)
  − Non-Productive Asset Cost        (support_asset_cost +
                                       support_asset_assigned_overhead
                                       _cost_annual — MUST include the
                                       assigned overhead share, or the
                                       waterfall will not reconcile)
  − Productive Asset Cost            (productive_asset_cost +
                                       productive_asset_assigned_
                                       overhead_cost_annual — SAME
                                       overhead-inclusion requirement.
                                       Shown as full cost, not yet
                                       split into charged/shortfall,
                                       because no Asset Rate Builder
                                       exists to define "charged".)
  − General Overheads                (total_business_overheads — the
                                       unassigned remainder only. Cost
                                       Summary has already excluded
                                       anything assigned to a specific
                                       asset, so this is safe to
                                       subtract directly with no
                                       double-count.)
  = Net Profit

GATING: the entire waterfall is unavailable (not a partial/wrong
number) unless Rate Builder's summary_complete is true. Margin itself
depends on knowing what's Charged, so there is no safe partial state
to show.

MANDATORY SANITY CHECK: reconstructed Net Profit must equal Recovery
Summary's net_position within $1. If it doesn't, DO NOT trust or ship
the waterfall — treat the variance as a real bug in the reconstruction
maths, not a rounding footnote. This check caught a real ~$33,700 bug
during this build (missing productive_asset_assigned_overhead_cost_
annual) — it is working as intended and should never be removed or
silenced.


=====================================================================
5. THE "BLOCKED" TRUST-STATE CHAIN (fully traced this session —
   do not re-trace from scratch next time)
=====================================================================

model_trust_state flows through this exact chain:

  reconciliationRules.js (buildModuleReconciliationChecks)
    → checks: P&L ready, Labour ready, General Overheads ready,
      Assets readiness, Employee overheads still in P&L (blocks if
      true), Business cost variance vs P&L benchmark (blocks if >1%)
  → moduleReconciliation.js (calculateModuleReconciliation)
  → useModuleReconciliation.js
  → modelReadinessCalculations.js (calculateModelReadiness)
  → useModelReadiness.js
  → useCostSummary.js reads model_readiness.modules.* as ITS inputs,
    sets model_readiness_status on its own output_contract
  → useBusinessSummary.js sets its own model_trust_state from
    cost_summary_output_contract.model_readiness_status
  → useRecoverySummary.js spreads business_summary.output_contract
    (unmodified) into calculateRecoverySummary, which sets
    model_trust_state = input.model_trust_state || "blocked"
  → Business Outcome reads recovery.model_trust_state directly

CONSEQUENCE: "Blocked" on Business Outcome reflects the health of the
ENTIRE reconciliation chain (P&L import vs Labour/Assets/Overheads
variance), not anything specific to Business Outcome's own inputs. Do
not narrow this to a Business-Outcome-specific check — that would hide
real upstream drift instead of surfacing it. There is a live
diagnostics page (Model Readiness) that shows exactly which check is
failing with a human-readable message — check there first, not by
re-tracing this chain.

Confirmed common cause during this build: Labour and Asset-finance
figures drifting out of sync with the imported P&L during ordinary
testing/iteration (self-inflicted, not a system bug). Resolves itself
once Labour/Assets/P&L are brought back into alignment.

6. ENVIRONMENT / TOOLING NOTES (avoid repeating these incidents)
=====================================================================

- PowerShell file writes: ALWAYS use full here-string + Set-Content.
  NEVER use regex -replace on JSX/JS files — caused one full file wipe
  and one 4x duplicate-insertion incident earlier in this project.
- Turbopack dev cache can go stale after a source fix, throwing a
  runtime error that references a compiled chunk running code that no
  longer exists in the current source. If an error doesn't match the
  current file content, stop the dev server, delete .next, and
  restart clean before debugging further — don't chase a ghost.
- When asking for file contents from the user's machine: prefer
  `Get-Content -Raw` printed straight to console over
  `Tee-Object -FilePath`. Tee'd output has silently returned blank at
  least once this session for unclear reasons (possibly $PSScriptRoot
  ambiguity or console buffering) — direct console read is more
  reliable.

7. OPEN / DEFERRED ITEMS
=====================================================================

1. Asset Rate Builder does not exist yet. Until it does, Productive
   Asset Cost can only be shown in full (cost), never split into
   charged/shortfall like labour. This is the single biggest gap
   between this module and full Phase 2 readiness.
2. Cost Allocation showing 125% labour coverage — flagged, not yet
   investigated. Likely double-assignment somewhere in Cost
   Allocation's group assignments. Separate from Business Outcome.
3. Reference doc for ALL hooks' output_contract shapes (not just the
   ones Business Outcome touches) — agreed as valuable, not yet built.
   Would eliminate most future guess→wrong→trace→fix cycles across
   the whole system, not just this module.
4. Business Modelling migration from Business Summary to Business
   Outcome as its source — not started, flagged as the natural next
   consumer once this module is stable.
5. Phase 2 data sources (timekeeping, invoicing, asset utilisation) —
   not built. When they arrive, they should replace the Rate-Builder-
   baseline assumption as direct inputs to the same waterfall shape,
   not require a rebuild of the calculation logic itself.
