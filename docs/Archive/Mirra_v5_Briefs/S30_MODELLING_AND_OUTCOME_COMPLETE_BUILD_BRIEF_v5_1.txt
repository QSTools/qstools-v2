# 6. PHASE 1/PHASE 2 - PRECISE MECHANICS
Phase 1: every profitable group loses the SAME % of its own profit,
simultaneously. Phase 2: only if shortfall EXCEEDS total combined
profit - leftover spreads by REVENUE share.
PRECISE BREAKEVEN BOUNDARY: shortfall exactly equal to total profit =
genuine breakeven, sitting exactly on the Phase 1/Phase 2 line. The
moment Phase 2 has real work to do, the business is already in a LOSS -
Phase 2 distributes the remainder of that loss, it is not "on the way to
breakeven."

58.7% retained, both matched exactly).
NOT YET VERIFIED: Phase 2's revenue-share spread - no scenario tested

per-group display.
