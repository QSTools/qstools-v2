## WHY THIS IS BIGGER THAN IT LOOKS
Card 1 (headline attention list) and Card 2 (ranked drill), both inside
BusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenueCard.jsx, have verdict language ("Being carried" / "Paying its way")
that is DEFINED BY the smoothed cascade - "being carried" literally means "the cascade is
redistributing margin to prop this source up." There is no naive equivalent of that concept - in an
unsmoothed view, nothing is "carried," sources are simply profitable or not, on their own.

Confirmed locations needing a naive-mode equivalent (from grep, not yet fully audited):
- Line ~734-735: per-group verdict badge (Real Capacity mode)
- Line ~750-751: Materials verdict badge (Real Capacity mode)
- Line ~944: Materials verdict badge (Assumed Capacity mode - real_capacity_verdict)
- Line ~1115: Card 1 attention-row styling keyed off verdict === "paying_its_way"
- Headline sentence ("X of 6 sources isn't paying its way") - likely also verdict-derived, not yet located precisely
- The Ledger itself (RealCapacityLedger / AssumedCapacityLedger) - the ENTIRE Ledger content IS the
  smoothed cascade story. There is no naive equivalent to show. Needs a decision: hide the Ledger
  entirely in As-Priced mode, or collapse it to just its already-naive "Starting Point" table.

`capacity_mode` (Real vs Assumed) is currently internal state to BusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenueCard.jsx
(`useState("real")` at line ~986), threaded down as a prop through RankedGroupsDrill, GroupBlock,
MaterialsSection, and others. A page-wide smoothing toggle means adding a SECOND, independent piece of
state (`smoothing_mode`, e.g. "smoothed" | "naive"), lifted up to page.jsx (both /business-outcome AND
/recovery-outcome, since both render BusinessOutcomeNetProfitBuildUp), threaded down the same way.
