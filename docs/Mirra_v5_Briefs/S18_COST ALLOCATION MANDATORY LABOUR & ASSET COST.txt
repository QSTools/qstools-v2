1. RELATIONSHIP TO THE EXISTING COST ALLOCATION BRIEF
--------------------------------------------------------------------

This is not a new module and not a new system version. It is the
next version of Cost Allocation itself — the module keeps its
existing identity, routes, files, and output contract; this brief
adds to that contract.

17_MIRRA_COST_ALLOCATION_v4.0_LOCKED.txt remains the base brief for
everything not addressed here: working groups, the productive labour
pool, the productive asset pool, shared asset percentage splitting,
overhead distribution, pool reconciliation, the simple default setup
flow. None of that changes.

This brief extends it in one direction only: non-productive labour
and non-productive assets, which the base brief deliberately
excludes, must now also be assignable — as cost only, never as
recovery — so every dollar the business spends on people and assets
lands somewhere visible.

2. SUPERSESSION NOTE (required per Section 14/19 of the naming
   convention brief — locked contract names/rules do not change
   silently)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Two rules in the base brief are overridden by this brief. Everything
else in the base brief is unchanged and still governs.

RULE 1 — SUPERSEDED
Old rule: "Productive Labour Only Rule" (base brief, Section 13)
  "Only productive labour enters Cost Allocation labour assignment
  ... Non-productive labour must not enter the Productive Labour
  Pool ... Non-productive labour belongs in overheads / business
  support cost pathway."
New rule: Non-productive labour may now be assigned directly to a
  working group as a cost-only line (Section 4.1 below). It still
  does not enter the Productive Labour Pool — that pool and its
  reconciliation stay productive-only and untouched. A parallel,
  separate pool is added for non-productive labour instead of
  changing the existing one.
Owning module: Cost Allocation.
Affected consumers: Recovery Summary, Rate Builder, Business
  Outcome (see Section 7).
Migration plan: additive — see Section 7. No existing field is
  renamed, removed, or reinterpreted.
Compatibility period: not applicable — existing productive-only
  fields keep their exact current meaning indefinitely.

RULE 2 — SUPERSEDED
Old rule: "Productive Assets Only Rule" (base brief, Section 19)
  "Only productive assets enter Cost Allocation asset assignment
  ... Non-productive assets must not enter the Productive Asset
  Pool ... Non-productive assets remain visible in Assets but do not
  enter Cost Allocation unless reclassified as productive."
New rule: Non-productive assets may now be assigned directly to a
  working group as a cost-only line (Section 4.2 below), using the
  same assignment mechanism as productive assets. The Productive
  Asset Pool itself stays productive-only and untouched.
Owning module: Cost Allocation.
Affected consumers, migration plan, compatibility: as per Rule 1.

Nothing else in the base brief — working group ownership, the
labour/asset separation rule, shared asset splitting, overhead
distribution, or the productive pool reconciliation panels — is
affected.

4. WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

This is cost attribution only. It does not touch recovery:

- Non-productive labour and non-productive assets still have ZERO
  recovery hours and are NEVER charged out.
- The Productive Labour Pool and Productive Asset Pool, and their
  existing reconciliation footers, keep their current productive-
  only meaning and math exactly as-is.
- The topline blended figures (e.g. total labour cost vs model
  capacity in Business Outcome's "Where is the pressure?" view)
  stay blended — that is correct and intentional, not something this
  brief changes. A business owner needs to see total spend pressure
  before drilling into the split. Confirmed correct this session
  against a live screen.

What this brief adds is the drill-down layer underneath that
topline: the productive/non-productive split must be visible not
just at the whole-business level (already true today) but at every
group level too, the same way productive labour already breaks out
by group. Today only the non-productive rows are flat, whole-business
buckets while everything else is already per-group — this closes
that one gap, it does not introduce a new pattern.

5. DECISIONS SETTLED THIS SESSION (previously open questions —
   now resolved, build against these)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5.1 Non-productive asset assignment mechanism — DECIDED

Non-productive assets are assigned using the same mechanism as
productive assets: asset-specific, cost-only, no recovery fields.
Not a generic per-group overhead pool.

Reason: Section 4.6 (Business Modelling scenario testing, future
work) requires swapping a specific non-productive asset for a
specific allowance and showing the profit delta (e.g. remove the
ute, add a $X/week vehicle allowance). That only works if the asset
stays an identifiable, swappable line item. A generic pool would
bury it and make that future scenario harder to build, not easier.

### 5.2 Coverage metric — DECIDED

Two metrics, additive — the existing productive-only coverage field
is not touched:

- staff_coverage_percent / asset_coverage_percent (EXISTING, UNCHANGED)
  Stays productive-only. Keeps feeding Recovery Summary, Rate
  Builder, and Business Outcome exactly as it does today. Zero
  downstream risk, zero reinterpretation.
- full_cost_attribution_coverage_percent (NEW)
  Denominator = every staff member and every asset, productive and
  non-productive. Answers "have we assigned everything," not "can we
  trust the recovery rate." A new, separate field — nothing that
  already consumes the old field needs to change.

Reason for not blending into one number: the existing field is a
recovery-trust signal consumed downstream. Folding non-productive
headcount into that same denominator would make a business look
"under-covered" in a recovery-trust sense the moment an unassigned
manager exists, which is a real gap but a different question than
the one that field currently answers for its downstream consumers.

### 5.3 Split must survive to group level — CONFIRMED REQUIREMENT

Per Section 4 above: productive/non-productive split must be visible
at every level a business can drill into — topline (blended, already
correct), and now group level (currently flat, being fixed by this
brief). This is the actual shape of the "assign everything" mandate,
not a separate feature.

6. SCOPE — WHAT NEEDS TO BE BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 6.1 Cost Allocation — Non-Productive Labour Assignment
- Allow non-productive labour types to be assigned to a working
  group.
- New section on the group cost stack: "Non-Productive Labour" —
  cost only, no recovery hours, no recovery rate shown or implied.
- Adds into "Total group cost" alongside Labour recovery / Asset
  recovery / Overhead recovery.
- Does NOT enter the Productive Labour Pool or its reconciliation.

### 6.2 Cost Allocation — Non-Productive Asset Assignment
- Per Section 5.1: same assignment mechanism as productive assets —
  asset-specific, percentage-splittable if needed, cost only.
- New section on the group cost stack: "Non-Productive Asset Cost" —
  cost only, no recovery rate shown or implied.
- Does NOT enter the Productive Asset Pool or its reconciliation.

### 6.3 Coverage — Two Metrics, Additive
- Per Section 5.2: add full_cost_attribution_coverage_percent as a
  new field. Leave staff_coverage_percent / asset_coverage_percent
  untouched.
- Denominator for the new field = all staff + all assets (productive
  and non-productive). Numerator = all assigned, of either kind.
- Display both on the Cost Allocation status strip as two distinct
  numbers, clearly labelled by what each answers.

### 6.4 Business Outcome — Per-Group Non-Productive Breakdown
- Per Section 5.3: extend the existing Full Labour Reconciliation
  and Full Asset Cost Reconciliation footers so the "Non-Productive
  Labour" and "Non-Productive Asset Cost" rows break out by group,
  the same way productive labour-only and asset-backed groups
  already do.
- Add a per-group table for each, mirroring the existing Labour
  Split / Asset Split pattern already built and confirmed reconciled
  this session.
- The topline blended pressure figures do not change. Only the
  breakdown underneath gets the extra level of drill-down.
- CRITICAL: re-verify the existing Full Labour Reconciliation and
  Full Asset Cost Reconciliation footers still tie out to the penny
  after this change. Do not assume — check, per Section 8 below.

### 6.5 Business Outcome — New Warning: Unaccounted Labour/Asset
- Per the user: "Labour and asset must be accounted for in the
  business outcome, otherwise it should warn."
- Follows the exact pattern of the existing unassigned_labour_cost /
  unassigned_asset_cost warnings, extended to cover productive AND
  non-productive: any staff member or asset not assigned to a group
  triggers a warning naming the actual gap amount — never a vague
  message.

### 6.6 Future — Business Modelling Scenario Testing (not in scope
    for this brief's build, stated to protect the data model choice)
- Once non-productive labour/assets are attributed to groups,
  Business Modelling can support "remove this non-productive asset
  from Management, replace with a $X/week vehicle allowance" and
  show the profit impact.
- The asset-specific assignment decided in 5.1 exists specifically
  so this stays a clean swap of comparable line items rather than
  something buried inside an opaque group total.

8. STANDING PRINCIPLES CARRIED FORWARD (established in the prior
   reconciliation session, apply to this build too)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

- RECONCILIATION DISCIPLINE: every headline figure must be
  independently re-derivable and checked against a second,
  independently-sourced calculation of the same thing. Surface
  disagreements explicitly with actual numbers and variance.
- Full file overwrites for code changes, not regex/string patches.
  View the complete file before editing.
- Cross-module IDs are not reliable joins — join on normalised name
  where two modules generate IDs independently, as was required
  fixing labour_type_key vs staff_type_id previously. Check for this
  same class of bug proactively in the new non-productive assignment
  paths.
- Validate before persisting — apply the same required-field guard
  used in Labour's save_profile()/create_profile() to any new save
  paths built here.
- When a live-data question can't be answered from source code
  alone, check the actual browser localStorage state directly rather
  than guessing.

11. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------

This brief is accepted when:

1. Non-productive labour can be assigned to a working group as a
   cost-only line.
2. Non-productive assets can be assigned to a working group using
   the same mechanism as productive assets, cost-only.
3. Neither enters the Productive Labour Pool or Productive Asset
   Pool — those pools and their reconciliation are unchanged.
4. staff_coverage_percent and asset_coverage_percent are unchanged
   in meaning and calculation.
5. full_cost_attribution_coverage_percent exists as a new, separate
   field covering all labour and assets, productive and non-.
6. Group cost stacks show non-productive labour and non-productive
   asset cost as distinct, clearly-labelled sections with no
   recovery rate implied.
7. Business Outcome's topline pressure figures remain blended,
   unchanged.
8. Business Outcome's Non-Productive Labour and Non-Productive Asset
   Cost reconciliation rows break out by group.
9. Full Labour Reconciliation and Full Asset Cost Reconciliation
   still tie out to the penny after the change.
10. A new warning fires, naming the actual gap amount, when any
    staff member or asset (productive or non-productive) is
    unassigned.
11. No existing output contract field is renamed, removed, or
    reinterpreted.
