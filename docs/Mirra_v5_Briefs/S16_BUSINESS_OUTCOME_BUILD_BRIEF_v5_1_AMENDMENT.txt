---

# 1. PURPOSE

This amendment records a deliberate, confirmed decision to extend Business Outcome's
source contract beyond the v5.0 baseline, and to lock in what has actually been built.

The v5.0 brief excluded Cost Allocation and Rate Builder from Business Outcome's
primary source chain, treating them as legacy Recovery Chain routes. That exclusion
is now partially reversed for one specific purpose: labour recovery.

This is not a return to the legacy Recovery Chain. The legacy Recovery Outcome page
and its hook remain untouched, separate, and are not the source of this data.

---

# 2. THE DECISION

Business Outcome's whole-of-business viability layer (Stage 2 / Business Summary
truth chain) is unchanged and remains as locked in v5.0.

Added to it: a Labour Recovery sub-layer, sitting inside Business Outcome, that
answers a question the v5.0 baseline could not answer - "is each labour source
actually recovering its true cost, or is it a hidden loss-maker?"

This restores the original v4.0 intent (Business Outcome should show where profit
is leaking, not just the aggregate total) without reintroducing the legacy
Recovery Chain page, hook, or allocation logic.

Reason for the decision: Business Summary / Cost Summary only expose aggregate
totals. They cannot show that, for example, Site Crew is being charged out below
its true fully-loaded cost while Owner/Director is comfortably ahead. Without
per-source visibility, "currently viable" at the top of the page can hide a real
problem underneath. The owner explicitly wants Business Outcome to mirror the
business back, including where it is leaking, not just certify overall viability.

---

# 3. UPDATED SOURCE CHAIN

Input Modules
-> Cost Summary
-> Revenue / COG truth
-> Revenue Summary
-> Business Summary / Revenue Reality / Asset Reality where available
-> Cost Allocation (labour group overhead distribution - real, not legacy)
-> Rate Builder (real saved charge-out rates per labour source, via
   rateBuilderLabourSourceRatesStorage - not the legacy Recovery Outcome route)
-> Business Outcome
-> Business Modelling
-> AI-readable Business State Export
-> Business Overview Dashboard
-> Quote Checker

The legacy Recovery Outcome page and its useBusinessOutcome.js hook are not part
of this chain and must not be touched or reused. Business Outcome's labour
recovery layer reads Cost Allocation and Rate Builder directly through their own
current, real storage/selectors - not through the legacy recovery hook.

---

# 4. UPDATED INPUT CONTRACT - LABOUR RECOVERY

In addition to the v5.0 Section 5 inputs (Cost Summary, Revenue/COG truth,
Revenue Summary, Business Summary, Reality layers), Business Outcome's labour
recovery sub-layer consumes:

## Cost Allocation

- rate_builder_labour_recovery_rows (true cost per hour per labour source,
  including allocated overhead), sourced via useCostAllocation.js /
  costAllocationRateBuilderSelectors.js

## Rate Builder

- real saved charge-out rate per labour source, read directly from
  readRateBuilderLabourSourceRates() in
  lib/storage/rateBuilderLabourSourceRatesStorage.js
- this is a flat map keyed by labour_source_type_id, independent of which rate
  model is currently active in Rate Builder - it is the true source of record
  for "what we actually charge," not a scenario or model-specific figure

Both are read-only. Business Outcome does not calculate or store labour rates
or overhead distribution itself; it consumes the existing outputs of Cost
Allocation and Rate Builder as-is.

---

# 5. UPDATED OUTPUT CONTRACT - LABOUR RECOVERY BLOCK

In addition to the v5.0 Section 6 output_contract fields, Business Outcome now
exposes a labour_recovery block:

- labour_recovery.rows[] - one per labour source, each with:
  - labour_source_id
  - labour_source_label
  - true_cost_per_hour (labour + allocated overhead, from Cost Allocation)
  - charged_rate_per_hour (from Rate Builder's saved source rates)
  - profit_per_hour (charged - true cost)
  - recovery_status (recovering / shortfall / not_ready)
- labour_recovery.weighted_summary:
  - weighted_true_cost_per_hour
  - weighted_charged_rate_per_hour
  - weighted_profit_per_hour
  - rate_coverage_percent (share of total hours with a saved rate)
- labour_recovery.overall_status (e.g. "All labour sources recovering")

Where a labour source has no saved rate in Rate Builder, its row must report
recovery_status "not_ready" honestly rather than a fabricated $0 or hidden row.

This block does not replace or alter the existing top-level output_contract
fields defined in v5.0 Section 6 (total_revenue, gross_profit, recovery gap,
etc.). It sits alongside them.

---

# 6. UPDATED BOUNDARIES

All v5.0 Section 10 boundaries remain in force, with one clarified exception:

"use Recovery Chain routes as primary truth" (v5.0 Boundary 3) is amended to:

Business Outcome must not use the legacy Recovery Outcome page, its hook, or its
allocation logic as a source. It may read Cost Allocation's and Rate Builder's
own current, real outputs directly, for the specific, additive purpose of
per-labour-source recovery visibility. This is a narrow, named exception, not a
general reopening of the legacy Recovery Chain as a Business Outcome source.

All other v5.0 boundaries stand unchanged, including: Business Outcome must not
calculate source module values directly, must not overwrite Cost Summary /
Revenue Summary / Business Summary, must not act as a scenario engine, and must
not let AI determine authoritative totals.

---

# 7. CURRENT IMPLEMENTED STATE (AS OF THIS AMENDMENT)

The following is built, working, and committed:

- hooks/useBusinessOutcomeLabourRecovery.js - additive hook, reads Cost
  Allocation's rate_builder_labour_recovery_rows and Rate Builder's real saved
  source rates directly; computes recovery_status, rate_gap, and
  weighted_summary per Section 5 above.
- components/business-outcome-truth/BusinessOutcomeTruthLabourRecoveryCard.jsx -
  displays per-labour-source table (source / true cost / charged rate /
  profit-per-hr / status) with a weighted-average total row, styled to match
  the existing v5.0 Business Outcome page.
- Wired into app/business-outcome/page.jsx between the summary card and the
  warnings panel.
- Bug fixed and committed: lib/calculations/cost-allocation/
  costAllocationLabourAdapter.js was deriving labour type keys by slugifying
  the display label instead of using the real staff_type_id, silently breaking
  overhead recovery for any staff type whose slugified label did not match its
  real ID (Senior Operator/Senior Staff, Site Crew). Fixed to use the real
  staff_type_id first, falling back to slugified label only for legacy/
  unclassified staff with no real ID.

Confirmed working output (screenshot-verified): all four labour sources
(Operator/Delivery Staff, Owner/Director, Senior Operator/Senior Staff, Site
Crew) show real nonzero true cost, real charged rate, and "Recovering" status.
Weighted average: $73.64/hr true cost vs $83.00/hr charged, +$9.36/hr profit,
100% of hours covered.

---

# 8. KNOWN GAPS - NOT YET BUILT

- Business Outcome's top-banner "Reconciliation mismatch" warning (from the
  pre-existing Stage 2 useBusinessOutcomeTruth.js truth-chain reconciliation
  between Business Summary and Revenue/COG truth) has not been investigated in
  this work. It is unrelated to the labour recovery addition.
- Recovery visibility is labour-only. Extending it to assets and unit-priced
  work (m2, m3, per-job pricing) is not possible yet: Cost Allocation has real
  required rates for these, but there is no persisted "actual charged" record
  to compare against. Rate Builder's Customer Charge Calculator is a live
  one-off calculator, not persisted history. Job Costing / actual-billing
  history (S23) is an explicit future build, not yet started. This has been
  raised and explicitly deprioritised - not to be revisited unless requested.

---

# 9. DEFINITION OF DONE (UPDATED)

Business Outcome's labour recovery layer is done when:

- Cost Allocation and Rate Builder inputs are confirmed read-only and correct
  (confirmed)
- labour_recovery output block matches Section 5 shape (confirmed)
- all labour sources with a saved rate show correct true cost, charged rate,
  and status (confirmed)
- labour sources with no saved rate report "not_ready" honestly, not $0
  (confirmed by design; no current example in data)
- build passes clean and the fix is committed (confirmed)
- the legacy Recovery Outcome page remains untouched and unaffected (confirmed)

All other v5.0 Section 15 items (contribution outputs, driver outputs, Business
Modelling / AI Export / Dashboard consumption) remain open and are unchanged by
this amendment.

---

# 10. FINAL PRINCIPLE

Business Outcome is still the current commercial truth layer, not the legacy
Recovery Chain. Reading Cost Allocation and Rate Builder directly for labour
recovery is a narrow, named, additive exception made because the business
explicitly wanted to see where profit leaks by labour source - not a reopening
of the legacy route as a general source of truth.
