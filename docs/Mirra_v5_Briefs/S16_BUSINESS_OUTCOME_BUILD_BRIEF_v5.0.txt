# Mirra v5.0 - Business Outcome Build Brief

STATUS: BUILD BRIEF - CONTRACT RECONCILIATION
VERSION: v5.0
MODULE TYPE: Business Outcome / Commercial Truth Layer
DATE: 2026-07-15

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This brief defines the v5.0 role of Business Outcome.

It resolves the conflict between the older v4.0 Recovery Chain model and the current v5.0 Business Review flow.

Business Outcome must not be rebuilt from legacy assumptions.

It must be aligned to the current v5.0 architecture before further code tidy-up.

Post-rename implementation note:

The active code/module naming now uses Business Outcome and /business-outcome.
This rename did not modernise the source chain, formulas, or output contract.
The current implementation may still preserve the existing Recovery Summary -> Cost Allocation -> Business Outcome chain until the S16 contract/source reconciliation task is completed separately.

---

# 2. PRIMARY DECISION

Business Outcome in v5.0 is not owned by the legacy Recovery Chain.

Business Outcome must consume the current business truth layers.

The v5.0 source chain is:

Input Modules
→ Cost Summary
→ Revenue / COG truth
→ Revenue Summary
→ Business Summary / Revenue Reality / Asset Reality where available
→ Business Outcome
→ Business Modelling
→ AI-readable Business State Export
→ Business Overview Dashboard
→ Quote Checker

Legacy Recovery Chain routes may remain available, but they do not define the current Business Outcome contract.

---

# 3. LEGACY CONFLICT RESOLUTION

The v4.0 Business Outcome brief referenced:

- Cost Allocation
- Recovery Summary
- Rate Builder
- Recovery Outcome

These are now treated as legacy Recovery Chain routes in the v5.0 baseline.

They must not be used as the primary source chain for Business Outcome unless a future compatibility layer explicitly maps them into the v5.0 contracts.

Current v5.0 Business Outcome should be based on:

- Cost Summary
- Revenue / COG truth
- Revenue Summary
- Business Summary
- Revenue Reality where available
- Asset Reality where available
- Model Readiness status where relevant

---

# 4. SYSTEM ROLE

Business Outcome is the current commercial truth layer.

It should explain:

- whether the business is commercially viable
- total revenue
- total COG
- gross profit
- gross margin
- total cost burden
- required revenue
- required recovery
- recovery gap
- cost absorption
- contribution by revenue stream where available
- contribution by operating group where available
- primary commercial drivers
- material warnings
- data completeness

Business Outcome describes the selected actual/current business state.

It is not a scenario page.

---

# 5. REQUIRED INPUT CONTRACTS

Business Outcome may consume approved outputs from:

## Cost Summary

- total_people_cost_annual
- total_asset_cost_annual
- total_business_overheads
- total_cost_burden
- required_revenue
- required_recovery_rate
- total_productive_output
- cost summary warning status

## Revenue / COG Truth

- total_revenue
- total_COG
- revenue stream mappings
- COG mappings
- gross profit
- gross margin
- mapping completeness
- reconciliation status

## Revenue Summary

- sales_revenue
- direct labour / COG labour values
- subcontract labour cost
- subcontract general cost
- equipment hire cost
- trade delivery or other cost
- material cost total
- operating expenses total
- target profit assumptions
- target material and labour mix assumptions

## Business Summary

- current consolidated business position
- cost and revenue relationship
- required recovery values
- warnings
- readiness where exposed

## Reality Layers Where Available

- Revenue Reality
- Asset Reality
- future operational reality layers

These must be consumed through structured outputs or selectors.

- output_contract_version
- source_period
- data_status
- reconciliation_status
- total_revenue
- total_COG
- gross_profit
- gross_margin_percent
- total_cost_burden
- required_revenue
- revenue_surplus_or_gap
- required_recovery
- achieved_recovery
- recovery_surplus_or_gap
- operating_profit_before_tax
- net_operating_margin
- productive_output
- cost_absorption_status
- primary_driver_key
- primary_driver_title
- primary_driver_evidence
- strongest_contribution_area
- weakest_contribution_area
- warning_list
- data_quality_list
- downstream_ready

Where a field is not currently calculable, it must be returned as unavailable or deferred rather than invented.

---

# 7. CONTRIBUTION CONTRACT

Business Outcome should support contribution analysis by:

- revenue stream
- operating group
- service / product category
- customer or segment where reliable
- other approved business classification

Each contribution record should support:

- contribution_id
- contribution_label
- contribution_type
- revenue
- COG
- gross_profit
- gross_margin_percent
- attributed_cost_burden where supported
- contribution_after_attributed_cost where supported
- percentage_of_total_revenue
- percentage_of_total_gross_profit
- ranking
- warning_state
- data_quality_state

Contribution outputs must clearly state when attribution is incomplete.

---

# 8. DRIVER CONTRACT

Business Outcome should expose deterministic driver records.

Driver record shape:

- driver_key
- driver_type
- driver_title
- direction
- value
- comparison_value
- variance
- affected_output
- severity
- evidence_reference
- confidence_state

Suggested driver types:

- revenue
- COG
- labour
- productivity
- asset
- overhead
- price
- volume
- mix
- recovery
- capacity
- data_quality

AI may explain driver records.

AI must not create authoritative driver values.

---

# 9. DOWNSTREAM CONSUMERS

Business Outcome should become a recognised upstream source for:

- Business Modelling
- AI-readable Business State Export
- Business Overview Dashboard
- historical reporting snapshots
- future recommendation logic
- future Quote Checker context where relevant

Current downstream wiring may not yet fully consume Business Outcome.

That is a known implementation gap to resolve after this contract is locked.

---

# 10. BOUNDARIES

Business Outcome must not:

- calculate source module values directly
- overwrite Cost Summary
- overwrite Revenue Summary
- overwrite Business Summary
- use Recovery Chain routes as primary truth
- act as a scenario engine
- create Dashboard-only calculations
- allow AI to determine authoritative totals
- hide incomplete data
- rank contribution using inconsistent measures

---

# 11. RELATIONSHIP TO BUSINESS MODELLING

Business Outcome should feed Business Modelling.

Business Modelling should use Business Outcome as the current commercial state where available.

Business Modelling owns:

- scenario assumptions
- target assumptions
- scenario outputs
- comparisons
- feasibility checks

Business Outcome owns:

- current commercial outcome
- current contribution
- deterministic current-state drivers
- data-quality status

---

# 12. RELATIONSHIP TO AI EXPORT

AI-readable Business State Export should eventually include Business Outcome as a named section.

Suggested future structure:

- business_summary
- business_outcome
- business_modelling
- selected_model
- readiness
- warnings
- traceability

Until this is wired, S06 should be treated as first-layer AI export, not final AI export.

---

# 13. RELATIONSHIP TO DASHBOARD

Business Overview Dashboard should eventually consume Business Outcome for:

- current commercial outcome
- gross profit
- contribution
- recovery gap
- primary drivers
- data-quality warnings

Dashboard must display Business Outcome values.

Dashboard must not recalculate them.

---

# 14. MIGRATION DECISION

Business Outcome remains part of the ultimate Mirra v5.0 architecture.

However, the current migration baseline may treat Business Outcome as contract-defined but not fully downstream-wired.

The next post-migration implementation task should be:

- inspect current Business Outcome code
- reconcile it to this v5.0 brief
- update downstream consumers where required

---

# 15. DEFINITION OF DONE

Business Outcome is ready when:

- source contracts are confirmed
- output_contract exists
- contribution outputs are defined
- driver outputs are deterministic
- data-quality warnings are exposed
- Business Modelling consumes it correctly
- AI Export includes it correctly
- Dashboard displays it correctly
- historical snapshot requirements are known
- no legacy Recovery Chain dependency is required for current v5.0 flow

---

# 16. FINAL PRINCIPLE

Business Outcome is the current commercial truth layer.

It must consume the current v5.0 business truth contracts, not the legacy Recovery Chain.
