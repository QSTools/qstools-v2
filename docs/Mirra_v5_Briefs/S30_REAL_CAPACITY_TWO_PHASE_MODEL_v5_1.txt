DATE: 2026-09-01 (same session as S30 Section 4.1/5.3 resolution)

# 1. WHAT THIS IS

A new, additive toggle on Business Outcome's per-source revenue page:
"Real capacity" vs "Assumed capacity". Assumed capacity is exactly
today's existing behaviour (the S30 ceiling/implied logic), completely
unchanged. Real capacity is a new, always-on model answering a
different, more honest question: given what the business has actually
billed and what it is actually committed to pay, who is genuinely
covering their own cost right now, and who is being propped up by
whom.

Confirmed with user this session: Real Capacity defaults on page load
(leads with the honest number). The headline banner goes visually
starker (red border/glow, new .business-outcome-headline-stark CSS
class) only when switching to Real Capacity reveals MORE failure than
Assumed Capacity already showed (compares being_carried_count between
the two headlines) - not simply whenever Real Capacity has any carried
sources at all.

---

# 2. THE MECHANISM - TWO-PHASE CASCADE, GROUP -> INDIVIDUAL HIERARCHY

Materials/COG has a hard floor: it can never show a net loss. Its
naive (unfloored) revenue share - total_revenue_reference minus
labour+asset's real modelled revenue - is compared against its real
true cost. Any shortfall this floor creates must come from somewhere
else, since real committed cost is real regardless of how it gets
allocated.

STEP 1 (outer, group-level): the 5 real operating groups + Materials
go through the cascade first, using each GROUP'S OWN combined
modelled_revenue/true_cost (not any one individual member) to
determine naive profitability:
  - Phase 1: the shortfall is absorbed only by groups currently
    naively profitable, weighted by CURRENT net_profit - proven
    (hand-verified in a spreadsheet before this was coded, matched to
    the cent against a 200,000-step numerical simulation) to mean
    every eligible group's margin shrinks by the exact same
    percentage simultaneously.
  - Phase 2 (fallback): once every eligible group's margin is
    exhausted, the leftover spreads across ALL FIVE groups
    proportional to modelled_revenue share.

STEP 2 (inner, individual-source level): whatever total dollar
adjustment a group received in Step 1 (naive minus final) becomes a
SECOND, smaller shortfall, pushed down across just that group's own
individual labour/asset sources via the IDENTICAL two-phase logic -
a member still individually healthy absorbs more of their group's own
adjustment, a member already underwater takes less. This guarantees a
group's children always sum exactly to that group's own Step 1 total,
at every revenue level, by the same invariant-preserving math proven
at the outer level.

CONFIRMED WHY TWO LEVELS ARE NEEDED (not optional): the first version
built tonight ran the cascade at INDIVIDUAL SOURCE level only (flat,
no group step). This produced numbers that diverged sharply from the
hand-verified spreadsheet - e.g. Foreman (Owner/Director deeply
negative + Senior Operator still positive, netting to a group total
that behaves differently as ONE unit than as two independent
participants) showed completely different figures depending on
granularity. User confirmed: run the cascade as a hierarchy, group
first, then individual - matching how the page's own headline/ranked
list already presents the story (one line per group).

# 3. CRITICAL - WHY LIVE NUMBERS DIFFER FROM THE SPREADSHEET (NOT A BUG)

The Section 4.1/5.3 spreadsheet built earlier this session used an
explicit "Proportional Scale Factor = Total Revenue Test / Real Total
Revenue" - i.e. it compared today's test revenue against a REMEMBERED
BASELINE ($2,500,000), and scaled EVERY source's naive figure
(including labour/asset groups' own net profit) by that ratio before
running Phase 1/2. This is a genuine "what if revenue were X instead
of the real baseline" model - Option 2, described below.

The LIVE APP deliberately does NOT do this (confirmed explicitly with
user this session: "we arent doing what ifs... that is the only
number [current real P&L revenue]... We can calc new numbers if we
need them" - no separate remembered baseline, no scale factor applied
to labour/asset sources' own naive net_profit). A source's own
modelled_revenue and true_cost are real, rate/hours-based, and
genuinely independent of today's total company revenue - they do NOT
shrink just because total revenue is down this period. This is Option
1 (confirmed correct and final for this page - see Section 4 below).

CONSEQUENCE: in the live app, ALL of the "revenue is down" pressure
flows through exactly one channel - Materials' shrinking leftover
share. Labour/asset groups' own naive profitability never moves. This
means Real Capacity's live numbers are legitimately, structurally
different from the spreadsheet's numbers - not an error to keep
chasing. Confirmed hand-verified example: at $1,225,000 test revenue,
Foreman's live naive_net_profit is +$30,368 (full, unscaled, real
baseline value) vs the spreadsheet's -$116,380 (baseline-scaled
value) - both are "correct" for what they each model, they are simply
answering different questions.

Do NOT re-open this as a bug in a future session without reading this
note first.

# 4. OPTION 1 (LIVE, CONFIRMED FINAL) vs OPTION 2 (DEFERRED TO BUSINESS
   MODELLING - NOT THIS PAGE, NOT BUILT)

Option 1 (this page, live now): no baseline, ever. A source's own
naive figure is always its real, current, unscaled value. Only
Materials' actual shortfall drives the cascade. Answers: "given what
we've actually billed and actually committed to pay, who is genuinely
covering their own cost right now."

Option 2 (explicitly deferred, NOT built anywhere yet): reintroduce
baseline-relative scaling - "if total company revenue fell from our
normal baseline to X, how would that proportionally affect every
group's own naive figure too." User confirmed this belongs in
Business Modelling as a deliberate, labelled scenario tool where the
person testing it consciously chooses a baseline and knows they are
exploring a hypothesis - NOT as silent behaviour on this page's
regular reporting view. If Business Modelling ever builds this, the
verified spreadsheet's Phase 0 scale-factor mechanic
(TestRevenue/RealBaselineRevenue applied to every source before the
Materials-floor cascade) is the reference implementation - see
S30_REVENUE_CEILING_SECTION_4_1_RESOLUTION_v5_1.txt's linked workbook.

# 5. REVENUE-VIEW / VERDICT-BADGE INCONSISTENCY - RESOLVED

Originally flagged: the "Ranked by revenue contribution" view showed a
real UX inconsistency - Revenue view always displays the naive,
unadjusted modelled_revenue figure (by design - Phase 1/2 only ever
touch net profit, never revenue, to avoid inventing a fake "implied
revenue" number), but the verdict badge next to it DID reflect
whichever capacity model was selected. Result: under Real Capacity, a
source could show a large, healthy-looking modelled revenue figure
sitting right next to a red "Being carried" badge - individually
correct, but reading as contradictory together.

RESOLVED (follow-up session, same date): verdict is fundamentally a
profitability claim, not a revenue-share claim - it never really
belonged attached to a dollar figure that isn't about profit in the
first place. Fix: the verdict badge is now only rendered in Net Profit
Contribution view (components/business-outcome-truth/
BusinessOutcomePerSourceRevenueCard.jsx, RankedGroupsDrill's ranked-
list-item rendering - single conditional added: `view_mode === "profit"
&& item.verdict && <VerdictTag .../>`). Revenue view now shows only the
MODELLED tag and the dollar/percentage figure, with no profitability
claim attached, regardless of which capacity model is selected.
Confirmed working in both Real and Assumed capacity.
