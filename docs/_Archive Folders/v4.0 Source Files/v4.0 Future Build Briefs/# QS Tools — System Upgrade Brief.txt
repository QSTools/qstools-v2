# QS Tools — System Upgrade Brief

## Opening Hours Macro Baseline, Cost Summary Hourly Basis, Business Summary Open-Hour View, and Recovery Layer Boundary

## 1. Upgrade Summary

This upgrade introduced a major system-wide clarification between the **macro business operating layer** and the **micro recovery strategy layer**.

The key system change is:

```txt
Macro layer uses net annual business open hours.
Micro recovery layer uses selected recovery drivers.
```

Before this upgrade, several macro pages were still using old Labour/recovery-hour concepts as the denominator for business-wide hourly metrics. That caused confusion because productive Labour hours were being treated like business operating hours.

This upgrade separates those concepts cleanly:

```txt
Opening Hours
→ defines when the business is available to operate.

Cost Summary
→ shows what the business costs to operate annually and per open business hour.

Business Summary
→ compares actual trading margin against the operating cost baseline using open business hours.

Recovery Summary
→ remains the micro strategy layer that tests how the business will recover the burden operationally.
```

The result is a much clearer system model:

```txt
Business open hours = macro availability / operating calendar.
Productive labour hours = micro recovery / labour capacity.
Selected recovery hours = strategy driver used downstream in Recovery Summary and Cost Allocation.
```

# 2. New Opening Hours Module

## 2.1 Purpose

The new Opening Hours module defines the business operating calendar.

It answers:

```txt
When is the business normally open?
How many open hours exist in a standard week?
How many annual open hours remain after shutdowns and public holidays?
```

It does **not** calculate productive Labour hours or Asset utilisation.

## 2.2 Key outputs

Opening Hours now exposes:

```txt
standard_weekly_open_hours
annual_open_weeks
annual_business_open_hours
public_holiday_closed_hours
additional_closed_hours
net_annual_business_open_hours
```

Current example:

```txt
Standard weekly open hours:       44.0 hrs
Annual open weeks:                50.0
Gross annual open hours:          2,200.0 hrs
Public holiday closed hours:      17.6 hrs
Net annual business open hours:   2,182.4 hrs
```

## 2.3 Important rule

Opening Hours creates **calendar context only**.

It must not be treated as:

```txt
productive Labour hours
recoverable Labour hours
Asset utilisation hours
chargeable hours
```

It is the macro denominator for business open-hour reporting.

# 3. Cost Summary Upgrade

## 3.1 Previous issue

Cost Summary previously used old recovery-hour/Labour-hour language for the main hourly baseline. That made it look like the business-wide cost per hour was based on Labour productive/recovery hours.

This was wrong for the macro layer.

## 3.2 New Cost Summary meaning

Cost Summary now answers:

```txt
What does the business cost to operate?
What does that cost per open business hour?
Where does the cost burden come from?
```

## 3.3 Correct hourly formula

Cost Summary now uses:

```txt
Operating cost per open hour
= total operating cost burden / net annual business open hours
```

Current example:

```txt
Total operating cost burden:       $705,680
Net annual business open hours:    2,182.4 hrs

Operating cost per open hour:
$705,680 / 2,182.4 = $323.34/open hr
```

Displayed as:

```txt
$323 / open hr
```

## 3.4 Cost Summary UI changes

Cost Summary now shows:

```txt
Operating cost baseline
$323 / open hr

Based on 2,182.4 net annual business open hours.
```

The main Cost Summary card now displays:

```txt
Hour selected:
Operating cost per open hour

Day / Week / Month / Quarter / Year selected:
Total cost burden scaled to the selected period
```

Cost composition in hour view now uses the same open-hour denominator:

```txt
People Cost per open hr
Assets per open hr
General Overheads per open hr
Total operating cost per open hr
```

## 3.5 Removed from Cost Summary visible UI

The following old macro wording was removed from Cost Summary:

```txt
Required Recovery Rate
Operating Recovery Hours
Labour burden pressure
productive labour hr
productive labour hours
```

Those concepts may still exist downstream, but they no longer drive the Cost Summary macro display.

## 3.6 Compatibility retained

Some compatibility fields remain in the output contract for downstream modules, including old recovery-hour fields. These were not removed because downstream modules still rely on them.

The important rule is:

```txt
Compatibility fields may exist.
They must not drive the macro Cost Summary headline.
```

# 4. Business Summary Upgrade

## 4.1 Previous issue

Business Summary was still showing hourly results using old recovery-hour logic and wording such as:

```txt
Required Recovery Rate
Actual Recovery Rate
Recovery Hours Used
```

That was not correct for the macro business result view.

## 4.2 New Business Summary meaning

Business Summary now answers:

```txt
Is the trading margin pool enough to cover the operating cost baseline?
```

It compares:

```txt
Margin Pool
vs
Total Operating Cost
```

And in hourly view it spreads that comparison over:

```txt
net annual business open hours
```

## 4.3 Correct hourly formulas

```txt
Required operating cost per open hour
= total operating cost burden / net annual business open hours

Actual margin per open hour
= margin pool / net annual business open hours

Deficit/surplus per open hour
= actual margin per open hour - required operating cost per open hour
```

Current example:

```txt
Revenue:                         $1,572,473
COGS / Direct Costs:             $1,240,091
Margin Pool:                     $332,382
Total Operating Cost:            $705,680
Net Position:                    -$373,298
Net annual business open hours:  2,182.4 hrs
```

Hourly:

```txt
Revenue per open hr:
$1,572,473 / 2,182.4 = $721/open hr

COGS per open hr:
$1,240,091 / 2,182.4 = $568/open hr

Margin Pool per open hr:
$332,382 / 2,182.4 = $152/open hr

Operating Cost per open hr:
$705,680 / 2,182.4 = $323/open hr

Deficit per open hr:
$152 - $323 = -$171/open hr
```

So Business Summary now correctly displays:

```txt
Deficit per Open Hour
$171 / open hr
```

## 4.4 Business Summary wording updates

The Business Summary page now uses macro open-hour wording:

```txt
Required operating cost per open hour
Actual margin per open hour
Deficit per open hour
Net annual business open hours
```

The following old visible wording was removed or targeted for cleanup:

```txt
Required Recovery Rate
Actual Recovery Rate
Recovery Hours Used
Operating Recovery Hours
productive labour hours
```

## 4.5 Data wiring fix

A key wiring issue was fixed:

```txt
Business Summary was not receiving the open-hour values correctly.
```

The missing pieces were:

```txt
useCostSummary needed Opening Hours in its memo dependency path.
useBusinessSummary needed to pass through Cost Summary macro fields.
```

The required values now flow through:

```txt
Opening Hours
→ Cost Summary output contract
→ Business Summary hook
→ Business Summary selector/card
→ Business Summary display
```

The important values are:

```txt
total_cost_burden
macro_required_operating_hour_rate
net_annual_business_open_hours
margin_pool
```

# 5. Recovery Summary Boundary

## 5.1 Decision

Recovery Summary is being left alone for now.

This is intentional.

## 5.2 Reason

Recovery Summary belongs to the **micro recovery strategy layer**, not the macro open-hour layer.

It answers:

```txt
How will the business recover the burden operationally?
```

That may legitimately use:

```txt
selected recovery hours
productive labour recovery hours
asset utilisation drivers
material margin
unit margin
group/division allocation drivers
```

## 5.3 Current Recovery Summary interpretation

The Recovery Summary top block currently compares the selected recovery strategy against the cost burden using selected recovery hours.

Example from current screen:

```txt
Actual recovery per recovery hour:     $150.17/hr
Required recovery per recovery hour:   $318.82/hr
Recovery gap per recovery hour:       -$168.65/hr
```

This is approximately:

```txt
$705,680 / 2,213 selected recovery hours = $319/hr
```

That is valid if the block means:

```txt
Whole-business burden through selected recovery hours
```

It should not be described as the macro Cost Summary open-hour baseline.

## 5.4 Future Recovery Summary cleanup

When Recovery Summary is rebuilt, labels should make the layer distinction clearer:

```txt
Macro baseline:
Operating cost per open hour

Micro recovery test:
Required recovery per selected recovery hour
```

This makes the logic clear:

```txt
The business costs $323 for every hour it is open.

But if only 2,213 selected recovery hours are available to recover the burden,
each selected recovery hour must recover about $319.
```

That is not the same metric, and both can exist.

# 6. Model Readiness Impact

## 6.1 Current status

Model Readiness is currently blocked.

This is correct.

It is not blocked because of Opening Hours. It is blocked because the current module totals do not reconcile to the P&L benchmark.

Current example:

```txt
P&L business cost benchmark:     $428,946
QS Tools module total:           $705,680
Variance:                        $276,734
Variance %:                      64.6%
```

Main drivers:

```txt
Labour P&L benchmark:            $331,343
Labour module total:             $593,693
Labour variance:                 $262,350

Asset finance P&L benchmark:     $0
Asset finance / interest cost:   $14,384
Asset variance:                  $14,384
```

## 6.2 Why this is acceptable

Extra Labour was added for testing other system items, so the current Labour variance is expected.

Therefore:

```txt
Model Readiness should remain blocked.
Cost Summary can be used as a preview.
Business Summary can be used as a preview.
Downstream decisions should not be trusted until the variance is resolved or accepted.
```

## 6.3 Model Readiness wording still to clean

Model Readiness still contains old wording in places:

```txt
Operating Recovery Hours
Required Recovery Rate
```

For macro baseline display, that should eventually become:

```txt
Net annual business open hours
Operating cost per open hour
```

But the pass/fail logic should not be changed.

# 7. System Layer Rules After Upgrade

## 7.1 Macro layer

Pages:

```txt
Opening Hours
Cost Summary
Business Summary
Model Readiness
```

Primary denominator:

```txt
net annual business open hours
```

Used for:

```txt
operating cost per open hour
actual margin per open hour
deficit/surplus per open hour
business availability pressure
macro business baseline
```

Do not use:

```txt
productive labour hours
selected recovery hours
operating recovery hours
```

as the macro denominator.

## 7.2 Micro recovery layer

Pages:

```txt
Recovery Summary
Cost Allocation
Recovery Outcome
Quote Engine
Business Modelling
Rate Builder later
```

May use:

```txt
selected recovery hours
productive labour hours
asset utilisation hours
unit drivers
material margin drivers
group allocation drivers
```

Used for:

```txt
how the business recovers the burden
which stream carries cost
whether labour/assets/materials can support recovery
quote/rate strategy
cost allocation by division/group
```

## 7.3 Labour layer

Labour keeps:

```txt
productive hours
productive labour hours
recovery-hour contribution flags
productive staff rates
non-productive staff classification
```

Those are still correct inside Labour and downstream recovery strategy logic.

They are not the macro business open-hour denominator.

# 8. Critical Formula Split

## 8.1 Opening Hours

```txt
net_annual_business_open_hours
= annual_business_open_hours
  - public_holiday_closed_hours
  - additional_closed_hours
```

## 8.2 Cost Summary

```txt
total_cost_burden
= total_people_cost_annual
  + total_asset_cost_annual
  + total_business_overheads
```

```txt
operating_cost_per_open_hour
= total_cost_burden / net_annual_business_open_hours
```

## 8.3 Business Summary

```txt
margin_pool
= revenue - COGS / direct costs
```

```txt
net_position
= margin_pool - total_cost_burden
```

```txt
actual_margin_per_open_hour
= margin_pool / net_annual_business_open_hours
```

```txt
deficit_or_surplus_per_open_hour
= actual_margin_per_open_hour - operating_cost_per_open_hour
```

## 8.4 Recovery Summary

For selected recovery hours:

```txt
required_recovery_per_selected_recovery_hour
= selected_cost_burden / selected_recovery_hours
```

This is micro-layer strategy logic, not the macro open-hour baseline.

# 9. Flow-On Effects

## 9.1 Cost Summary

Cost Summary is now a cleaner macro baseline.

It should no longer be interpreted as:

```txt
how much Labour must recover per productive hour
```

It now means:

```txt
what the business costs per open business hour
```

## 9.2 Business Summary

Business Summary now produces a much more intuitive open-hour result.

Instead of asking:

```txt
Are we recovering enough per Labour/recovery hour?
```

It asks:

```txt
For every hour the business is open, does trading margin cover the business cost baseline?
```

## 9.3 Recovery Summary

Recovery Summary becomes more clearly downstream.

It should not be forced to use open hours everywhere.

Its job is to test:

```txt
how the burden will be recovered
```

not:

```txt
what the business costs per open hour
```

## 9.4 Cost Allocation

Cost Allocation remains downstream of Recovery Summary.

The upgrade clarifies that Cost Allocation should allocate the recovery burden according to operational drivers, not redefine the macro business open-hour baseline.

## 9.5 Quote Engine

Quote Engine still likely contains old required recovery rate wording.

This should be reviewed later because Quote Engine may need both:

```txt
macro open-hour pressure
selected recovery driver pressure
```

depending on quote type.

No Quote Engine changes should be made until its role is deliberately reviewed.

## 9.6 Business Modelling

Business Modelling still likely uses old `required_recovery_rate` and productive-hour assumptions.

That should be reviewed later as part of a modelling-layer upgrade.

No blind replacement should happen.

## 9.7 Model Readiness

Model Readiness still correctly blocks when module totals do not reconcile to the P&L benchmark.

The wording should eventually align with the macro open-hour baseline, but the readiness logic should remain strict.

# 14. Final System Rule

The upgrade establishes the following locked principle:

```txt
Opening Hours defines the business operating calendar.
Cost Summary converts annual operating cost into cost per open business hour.
Business Summary compares trading margin against that open-hour cost baseline.
Recovery Summary and Cost Allocation decide how the burden is recovered operationally.
```

This prevents the system from confusing:

```txt
business availability
productive labour capacity
selected recovery strategy
```

and creates a cleaner foundation for downstream allocation, modelling, quote checking, and recovery strategy.
