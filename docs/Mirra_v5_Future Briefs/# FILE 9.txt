# QS Tools — Core Business Question Catalogue

## Version: v1.0 — DRAFT FOR LOCK

## PURPOSE

This brief defines the core business questions QS Tools must be capable of answering through:

* deterministic calculations
* current structured business outputs
* historical reporting
* baseline, actual, and target comparisons
* scenario modelling
* AI-supported explanation

These questions are not intended to become hardcoded prompts.

They define the range of business decisions the database, calculation engine, reporting layer, Dashboard, Business Outcome, Modelling, and AI architecture must support.

The questions must remain industry-neutral.

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLE

QS Tools must not build one database field for every question.

The question catalogue must instead be used to identify:

* required measures
* required dimensions
* required periods
* required identities
* required module outputs
* required historical facts
* required scenario variables
* required calculation ownership
* required AI explanation context

---

## QUESTION OWNERSHIP RULE

Each question must be classified as one of:

* deterministic current-state answer
* historical reporting answer
* reconstructed historical-model answer
* scenario-model answer
* AI explanation of deterministic evidence
* unsupported until more source data exists

---

## NOT ALLOWED

* claiming all questions can be answered without verifying source coverage
* building fixed AI responses for every question
* allowing AI to invent missing measures
* treating questions as database entities
* creating duplicate measures under different names
* assuming internal financial data proves customer price sensitivity
* treating correlation as confirmed causation

---

## DEFINITION OF DONE

The catalogue is ready when:

* all questions are mapped to a category
* each category has required measures and dimensions
* unsupported questions are identified
* required Business Outcome outputs are known
* required Modelling outputs are known
* required reporting facts are known
* required AI context is known

---

## FINAL DEFINITION

This catalogue defines the business decisions QS Tools is expected to support. It is the demand-side specification against which the database, modules, Business Outcome, Modelling, Dashboard, reporting layer, and AI system must be tested.

## IMPORTANT COVERAGE DISTINCTIONS

### Internal Financial Evidence

Can support:

* actual revenue
* actual cost
* margin
* recovery
* utilisation
* historical trends
* concentration
* cost structure

### Operational Evidence

May be required for:

* capacity
* productivity
* asset usage
* time use
* delivery efficiency
* backlog

### Commercial Behaviour Evidence

Required for stronger pricing conclusions:

* quote value
* quoted rate
* accepted or rejected status
* customer or segment
* work or offering type
* date
* stated loss reason where available
* actual delivery result
* repeat purchase behaviour

Internal margin alone must not be treated as proof of price sensitivity.

### External Evidence

May be required for:

* competitor pricing
* market size
* market demand
* economic conditions
* industry benchmarks

Questions requiring external evidence must be marked accordingly.

---

## QUESTION MAPPING EXAMPLE

Question:

Where is the safest place to increase price?

Required measures:

* current price
* cost
* contribution margin
* quote win rate
* demand or backlog
* available capacity
* actual delivery margin
* repeat business

Required dimensions:

* customer
* segment
* offering
* operating group
* time

Required layers:

* reporting
* structured model
* commercial activity facts
* recommendation engine

Result:

This question is only partially supportable from internal cost and margin data until quote outcome and demand data exist.

Questions should be placed into:

### Phase 1 — Core Financial Truth

* revenue
* COG
* gross profit
* net profit
* total cost
* recovery
* baseline
* actual
* target

### Phase 2 — Operational Performance

* labour productivity
* capacity
* asset utilisation
* operating groups

### Phase 3 — Commercial Optimisation

* quote conversion

* price sensitivity

### Phase 4 — Strategic and External Context

---

## READINESS TEST

The database design must not be considered complete until:

* every Phase 1 question is fully mapped
* every Phase 2 question has an identified data owner
* Phase 3 data requirements are designed even where not yet implemented
* unsupported conclusions are clearly identified
* Business Outcome and Modelling gaps are known

---

## NOT ALLOWED

* marking a question supported because AI can discuss it generally
* treating missing dimensions as calculation problems
* treating missing source data as an AI problem
* creating a new module variable where a reporting dimension is sufficient
* creating a reporting fact where a deterministic output is sufficient
* claiming price sensitivity without behavioural evidence
* storing AI opinions as financial facts

---

## DEFINITION OF DONE

The matrix is complete when every catalogue question has:

* a decision type
* required measures
* required dimensions
* a source layer
* an owner
* a support status
* a known gap
* an implementation phase

---

## FINAL DEFINITION

The Question-to-Data Coverage Matrix is the proof that QS Tools has been designed around the business decisions it is expected to support, rather than around disconnected database tables or individual AI prompts.

# FILE 11

# QS Tools — Business Outcome Output Contract Brief

## Version: v1.0 — DRAFT FOR LOCK

## PURPOSE

This brief defines the required role, inputs, calculations, and outputs of Business Outcome.

Business Outcome must combine cost truth and revenue truth to show:

* whether the business is commercially viable
* where gross profit is generated
* whether total costs are being recovered
* which areas contribute most and least
* what major factors are driving the result

Business Outcome is not a scenario page.

It describes the current or selected business state.

---

## POSITION IN THE SYSTEM

The intended flow is:

Input modules
→ Cost Summary
→ Revenue and COG truth
→ Business Outcome
→ Modelling
→ Dashboard
→ AI explanation

---

## REQUIRED INPUT CONTRACTS

Business Outcome may consume approved outputs from:

### Cost Summary

* total_people_cost
* total_labour_cost
* total_employee_overhead_cost
* total_asset_cost
* total_general_overhead_cost
* total_business_cost
* total_cost_burden
* productive_output
* required_recovery
* required_revenue
* recovery_model
* warning status

### Revenue and COG Layer

* total_revenue
* total_COG
* gross_profit
* gross_margin
* revenue by stream
* COG by stream or allocation
* revenue by operating group where available
* financial period
* mapping completeness
* reconciliation status

### Rate Builder and Operating Structure

Where available:

* actual rates
* minimum rates
* operating groups
* working units
* contribution groups
* linked productive capacity

---

## REQUIRED HEADLINE OUTPUTS

Business Outcome must expose:

* total_revenue
* total_COG
* gross_profit
* gross_margin_percent
* operating_cost_after_COG
* total_cost_burden
* operating_profit_before_tax where supported
* net_operating_margin where supported
* required_revenue
* revenue_surplus_or_gap
* required_recovery
* achieved_recovery where supported
* recovery_surplus_or_gap
* data_period
* data_completeness
* reconciliation_status

---

## CONTRIBUTION OUTPUTS

The contract should support contribution analysis by:

* revenue stream
* product or service
* operating group
* customer or segment where reliable
* other approved business classification

Each contribution record should be capable of returning:

* identity
* revenue
* COG
* gross profit
* gross margin
* allocated or attributed operating cost
* contribution after operating cost where supported
* percentage of total revenue
* percentage of total gross profit
* percentage of total cost burden
* ranking
* warning state
* data quality state

---

## GROSS-PROFIT SPLIT

Business Outcome must clearly show:

* where gross profit is generated
* how gross profit is distributed across the business
* whether one area is carrying the rest of the business
* whether any area consumes resources without adequate contribution

The gross-profit split must not imply causation where cost attribution is incomplete.

---

## DRIVER OUTPUTS

Business Outcome should expose deterministic driver records such as:

* driver_key
* driver_type
* driver_title
* direction
* value
* comparison_value
* variance
* affected_output
* severity
* evidence_reference
* confidence_state

Suggested driver types:

* revenue
* COG
* labour
* productivity
* asset
* overhead
* price
* volume
* mix
* recovery
* capacity
* data_quality

---

## STRONGEST AND WEAKEST CONTRIBUTION

These outputs must be based on approved measures.

Possible measures include:

* gross profit
* gross margin
* contribution after attributable cost
* revenue-to-cost ratio
* recovery surplus

The selected ranking measure must be visible and consistent.

The system must not label the highest-revenue area as the strongest contributor unless the approved contribution measure supports it.

---

## BUSINESS HEALTH OUTPUTS

Business Outcome should return deterministic status for:

* profitability
* gross margin
* recovery
* cost absorption
* concentration
* productivity
* data completeness

These should be based on explicit thresholds or comparison rules, not AI opinion.

---

## DATA-QUALITY OUTPUTS

Business Outcome must expose:

* revenue reconciliation
* COG reconciliation
* module reconciliation
* unmapped account value
* unallocated cost value
* incomplete operating-group allocation
* incomplete revenue-stream mapping
* provisional or approved period status

---

## DOWNSTREAM CONTRACT

Business Outcome outputs will be consumed by:

* Business Modelling
* Dashboard
* historical snapshots
* AI structured context
* reporting exports
* recommendation logic

Internal variable names must remain stable.

---

## NOT ALLOWED

* manually entered Business Outcome totals
* duplicate recalculation of source-module formulas
* hiding unmapped or unallocated values
* presenting attributed profitability as exact where attribution is incomplete
* mixing actual and target values in the same unlabeled output
* allowing AI to determine authoritative health status
* ranking contribution using inconsistent measures

---

## DEFINITION OF DONE

Business Outcome is ready when:

* inputs reconcile
* headline outputs are complete
* contribution outputs are defined
* gross-profit split is traceable
* driver records are deterministic
* data-quality warnings are exposed
* Modelling can consume the contract
* Dashboard can consume the contract
* AI can explain the outputs without recalculating them

---

## FINAL DEFINITION

Business Outcome is the current commercial truth of the business. It combines revenue, COG, cost burden, recovery, and contribution into a traceable output contract that explains how the business is performing before future scenarios are applied.
