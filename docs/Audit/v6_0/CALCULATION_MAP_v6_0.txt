0. HOW TO READ AND MAINTAIN THIS FILE
===============================================================================
- One line per money-moving figure: formula in words, then
  `function` Lnnn (file). Formulas are as the code computes them today,
  not as they should be. Where the code is wrong or inconsistent, the line
  carries a [CM-n] tag pointing to Section 12.
- NO DOLLAR FIGURES (same rule as the tracker). Numbers go in dated
  [VERIFIED] brief entries only.
- Line numbers are as of the 2026-09-24 extract and WILL drift. Search by
  function name; the line number is only a hint.
- "x" means multiply, "/" divide. "sum of" means a reduce over the rows named.
- Items marked (tracker) were not in the extract (no arithmetic on that
  line) and are taken from VARIABLE_TRACKER_v6_0.txt.
- Regenerate the raw extract (read-only, ~1 min):
    python Py\audit-scripts\extract_calc_map.py
  Output: docs\Audit\v6_0\calc_map\ - 00_COVERAGE.txt first (should be
  short and explainable), 01_FIELD_INDEX.txt to find every place a field
  is calculated, then the per-module dumps.
- WHEN A CALCULATION CHANGES (required - kickoff s4 checklist):
  re-run the extract after the patch builds and live-tests, update the
  formula AND line numbers here from the new dump, close or amend any CM-n
  entry in s12, re-date the header, and commit with the code change.
  A calculation change is not done until this file matches the code.
- Coverage of the 2026-09-24 extract: 2,388 functions parsed, 2,182
  calculation statements. 84 "not parsed" declarations checked by hand:
  37 were captured as statements anyway, 47 are list/JSX values with no
  maths. 5 statements outside functions are module constants. Nothing
  material missing.

12. KNOWN INCONSISTENCIES (found by reading formulas side by side)
===============================================================================
CM-1 OPEN - HIGH. Asset-driven units stack labour on top of the calculator
  rate. Business Outcome: asset revenue = blended rate x covered hours
  (L196) AND labour revenue = charge rate x hours (L391), summed (L550,
  L1175-1179). The owner bills the calculator rate ALL-IN (e.g. machine
  rate includes the operator). Rate Builder's own result panel, the legacy
  asset split and Quick Start all treat it as all-in; only the current
  Business Outcome hook stacks. Effect: every asset-driven unit's entered /
  Best Case revenue is overstated by its crew's charge rate x hours; the
  coverage gap value (L484) likewise. Real net profit is NOT affected (the
  cascades reconcile to real revenue) but the split of who carries whom,
  View A materials residual, View B shortfall, Best Case, and Business
  Modelling ceilings all are. FIX PENDING - Business Outcome, own session
  step, separate confirmation + live test.
CM-2 NOTE. Two overhead-per-labour-hour bases coexist by design: Rate
  Builder puts 100% of group overhead on labour (RB-1); Business Outcome
  splits by cost share (F7). Only matters if a new display reads the
  Rate Builder rows.
CM-3 NOTE. Group minimum rate (Cost Allocation) excludes non-productive
  cost; Business Outcome true cost includes it (C6, BO-3 by design).
  Business Modelling floor uses the full figure.
CM-4 NOTE. assigned_asset_hours is the MAX of a group's asset hours while
  assigned_asset_burden is the SUM of their cost (Cost Allocation L54-59).
  Intended (co-deployed assets run at the same time); keep in mind for any
  per-asset rate.
CM-5 CLEANUP. The Phase 1 / Phase 2 cascade exists three times:
  apply_real_capacity (hook), apply_real_capacity_v2 (View B),
  run_group_level_cascade (Business Modelling). A change to one must be
  mirrored or the copies disagree.
CM-6 WATCH. ViewBGroupsDrill credits View B surplus to profitable groups
  inside the component (L948-961). The selector does not (BO-12). Any
  consumer other than that drill sees uncredited figures.
CM-7 NOTE. "required recovery rate" exists in several forms: Cost Summary
