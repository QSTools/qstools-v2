Raised while testing whether Mirra's own engine can model a subscription

2. ITEM 1 - COST-ONLY WORKING GROUPS CANNOT BE PRICED
===============================================================================

SOURCE OF CLAIM: variable tracker s18 (Rate Builder, code-traced 2026-09-23),
Rate Builder brief. NOT re-verified in code since.

WHAT THE TRACKER SAYS
- Customer Charge Calculator (Path B) reads Cost Allocation
  operational_group_cost_rows (group_cost_per_hour, labour/asset/overhead
  recovery rates, group_recovery_hours).
- "Only groups with assigned_asset_burden > 0 offered."
- Recovery hours = sum of "hr" line quantities, else the output-driver
  quantity (RB-13). Rate Builder is hours-native; business_type is not read.
- Path A (Labour Rates Builder) prices labour groups on their own. Saved
  charge rates key by staff_type_id; saved calculators key by
  linked_cost_allocation_group_id; both are read by Business Outcome.

HOW PRICING LAYERS TODAY (owner-confirmed 2026-09-24)
- Labour is priced first, as its own line (Path A).
- An asset operated by that labour is then priced as the combined
  labour-plus-asset rate; the asset's own incremental charge is the DIFFERENCE
  between the combined rate and the labour rate. Not the combined rate
  standing alone.
- Consequence: a pure labour group (people, no asset) is already covered by
  Path A. The asset-burden gate does not wrongly exclude hours-based labour
  groups.

THE GAP (reframed)
- A working group with real cost but neither billable labour hours nor an
  asset has no pricing path. Examples:
    - hours-based business: a fabrication shop, stores or yard function,
      or any cost-only support group that is meant to be recovered through
      pricing rather than spread purely as overhead;
    - units-based business: a metered-cost group (for example a per-document
      processing cost) with no person or machine attached.
- This is NOT a business_type / mode problem. It is "Path B assumes every
  priceable working group contains an asset". The same defect would appear in
  a purely hours-based business. Do not scope it as "add a units-mode Path B".

WHAT IS NOT YET KNOWN (must be answered by the code check)
- Q1. Is assigned_asset_burden > 0 a hard gate in the group picker, or an
  unexercised assumption that another path already handles?
- Q2. Where does the gate live (line calculator hook, selector, or Cost
  Allocation rate-builder selector)?
- Q3. If a cost-only group is offered, what does group_cost_per_hour mean for
  a group whose recovery driver is units, not hours? (group_cost_per_unit is
  hard-coded 0 in Cost Allocation - tracker s6, "hours-native".)
- Q4. Does Business Outcome (build_asset_sources, get_group_blended_rate,
  get_group_covered_hours) have any way to earn revenue from a group with no
  asset and no assigned labour? BO-6 currently says a asset-driven unit with
  no labour earns nothing; a cost-only group is neither asset-driven nor
  labour-driven. Check what it does today.
- Q5. Are there Rate Builder group pickers other than Path B with the same
  gate?

OPTIONS TO EVALUATE (not decided)
  A. Relax the Path B gate so any group with cost is offered, pricing on the
     group's recovery driver. Smallest change; depends on Q3 and Q4.
  B. New pricing path for cost-only / metered groups (per-driver-unit charge),
     stored separately, read by Business Outcome the same way calculators are.
     Larger; needed if A cannot express a per-unit charge.
  C. Workaround: give the group a nominal asset line to unlock Path B.
     REJECTED as a design - it distorts asset cost and utilisation and would
     pollute Cost Allocation. Listed only so it is not proposed again.
Expect the answer to be A or B depending on Q3/Q4; decide after the code read.

CONSTRAINTS FROM EXISTING CONVENTIONS
- Rate Builder has no output contract; only stored user inputs reach Business
  Outcome (tracker s18). Any new stored pricing input needs: has_loaded guard
  on the save effect, an explicit read in Business Outcome, and a check for
  bug class (b) (storage/selector layer dropping unlisted fields).
- Keep new files under ~600 lines; do not grow the large Business Outcome
  files (file map s7, conventions rule 7).
- The five untouchable files (file map s7) stay untouched.

4. ITEM 2 - UNITS-MODE VERIFICATION PASS (still owed)
===============================================================================

SOURCE OF CLAIM: kickoff s1 (parked business_type gap), tracker s8 (P2.1
findings), s9 (chain), s12-s21 hours/units notes.

WHAT IS KNOWN
- The setting is business_type (labour_based / product_based), storage key
  qs_tools_business_setup_v1. Default labour_based everywhere. The
  completeness check Boolean(business_type) can never fail (tracker s8).
- Four mode vocabularies exist and all derive one-way from business_type:
  business_type, commercial_driver_mode (Revenue/COGS), recovery_mode
  (Recovery Summary), activity_driver_type (Business Summary).
- Mode-aware today: Business Setup, Revenue/COGS, Business Summary (driver
  and hierarchy heading), Recovery Summary (driver card; product mode zeroes
  every stream except overhead).
- Passthrough only: Cost Allocation (business_type, activity_driver_type
  carried as data; driver label built but not rendered; group_cost_per_unit
  hard-coded 0).
- Hours-native, business_type NOT read: Labour, Assets, Cost Allocation
  branching, Business Outcome, Business Modelling, Rate Builder, Quote Engine.
- Known product-mode defects logged, not fixed:
    C3b  product mode overhead pool would double count (uses
         total_cost_burden instead of the net figure).
    Business Summary "Current business mirror" uses open hours in both modes.
    R2/R3 Recovery Summary stream logic in product mode.
    BO-6  product / throughput businesses parked as outside the hours-native
          model.
- Live checking so far was WIRING ONLY: the test dataset has no unit-driver
  data, so no product-mode number has ever been exercised.

WHAT THE PASS MUST ANSWER
- U1. What is the unit driver, where is it entered, and where does it live in
  storage? (Revenue/COGS holds commercial_driver_mode; confirm whether a real
  units quantity is stored anywhere.)
- U2. How does a unit driver reach Cost Allocation so that a group can be
  recovered per unit, not per hour? (group_cost_per_unit is 0 today.)
- U3. How does Rate Builder charge per unit (this is where Item 1 and Item 2
  meet - a metered group needs a per-unit charge with a real unit quantity).
- U4. How does Business Outcome convert units x price into modelled revenue
  and reconcile to real revenue, including the View A residual and View B peer
  cascades, covered-hours logic (BO-6) and the capacity coverage gap?
- U5. What happens to overhead and the non-productive cost policy when the
  recovery driver is units? (C3b, C6, distribute_non_productive_cost.)
- U6. Mixed businesses: an hours-based business that also sells fixed-price
  or per-unit lines beside its hourly rates (e.g. per-area or flat-fee jobs).
  commercial_driver_mode already has a mixed_unit_based value; confirm what,
  if anything, consumes it. Decide whether one business can carry both
  drivers at once.
- U7. Macro views (Cost Summary / Business Summary) use open hours; decide
  what the units-mode macro basis is.

===============================================================================
5. WHY THIS IS INFRASTRUCTURE, NOT A NICE-TO-HAVE
===============================================================================

- Without a working unit driver the tool only analyses hours-based businesses.
  Any product, throughput, subscription or metered-cost business would get
  numbers that look plausible but are hours-shaped.
- The first real units-based test case is planned to be the owner's own
  software business (one operating group; several working groups inside it;
  pricing tiers built in Rate Builder on top of the shared groups). That case
  is business planning and lives OUTSIDE the repo. This brief carries only the
  engineering requirement it exposes.
- A metered-cost group is the cleanest test of Item 1 because it has no
  labour and no asset by construction.

  Business Outcome currently breaks: it adds labour's charge rate ON TOP of
  the calculator rate in asset-driven units (calc map s12 CM-1). Rate Builder's

  comes only from assigned labour, assigned assets, non-productive members
  and its overhead share; overhead is weighted by labour cost whenever any
  productive labour exists (C5), so a group with no labour gets ZERO
  overhead. A group with neither labour nor asset may therefore carry no
  cost at all today - i.e. it cannot be CREATED as a cost-only group, not
  merely priced. If confirmed, Item 1 starts in Cost Allocation (a direct
  cost input or a different overhead method), then Rate Builder.
