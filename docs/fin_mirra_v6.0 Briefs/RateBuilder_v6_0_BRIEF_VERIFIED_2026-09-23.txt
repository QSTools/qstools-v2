===============================================================================
1. ROLE AND BOUNDARIES (intent carried forward - still believed correct)
===============================================================================

Rate Builder answers "what rate recovers it?". It turns Cost Allocation
structure + recovery requirement + component cost + margin target into
minimum recoverable rates and sell rates.

Owns: charge-out rates (labour per staff type), customer charge lines,
charge vs cost vs profit comparison, margin / markup application.

Does NOT own (consumes only): labour cost, asset cost, overhead cost, COG,
productive hours, asset utilisation, groups, assignments, overhead
distribution, recovery testing, final business verdict.

Locked decisions still in force (verify each against code):
  L1 One owner per calculation. Labour owns labour cost; Cost Allocation owns
     group cost and overhead split; Rate Builder compares charge to cost.
  L2 Minimum recoverable labour rate = productive labour cost + allocated
     overhead. Non-productive labour is NOT loaded directly into the rate.
  L3 All-productive weighted rate is read-only: sum(staff-type charge rate x
     productive hours) / total productive hours.
  L4 Overhead follows labour cost weighting, not equal split.
  L5 Productive asset running costs stay out of the labour overhead pool
     (now true upstream: pool transfer to Assets, verified P2.4/P2.5).
  L6 Rate Builder must not invent labour charge rates, market rates, or
     product rates from COG totals.
  L7 Margin and markup are distinct: margin sell = cost / (1 - m);
     markup sell = cost x (1 + k). Active method must be visible.
  L8 P&L implied labour benchmark does not belong in Labour Rates Builder
     (belongs to Business Outcome). See RB-Q5 - component still exists.

Candidate findings from the P2.5 dump (re-view fresh before acting):
  RB-1  build_rate_builder_labour_recovery_rows gives each staff type
        group_overhead x (its cost / group labour cost). Summed over the
        group, labour carries 100% of the group's overhead, including groups
        with productive assets. If Path B also charges the group overhead
        rate on machine work, overhead may be recovered twice when a job is
        priced with labour charge-out + a machine calculator. Design check.
  RB-2  A group with zero overhead or zero labour cost is skipped entirely
        (return before the assignment loop), so that group's labour hours
        and cost also drop out of the staff-type row. Latent while every
        labour group gets overhead (labour_cost_weighted method, C5).
  RB-3  all_productive falls back to summed staff rows only when the share
        product is zero - mixed basis (see C24).

V3 code read (dump rb_v3_pathB_2026-09-23.txt):
  SCOPE INSIGHT: Rate Builder publishes NO output contract. What leaves
    it is stored user input only: per-staff-type charge rates
    (qstools_rate_builder_labour_source_rates_v1) and calculators
    (qs_tools_rate_builder_calculators_v1). Its calculated figures
    (minimum rate, recovery cost, profit) are display-only; Business
    Outcome reads minimum recoverable rates from Cost Allocation and
    re-derives the rest itself. So Rate Builder display bugs do not
    propagate down the chain; storage keys and ids do. The v5 "contract
    fields" (group_rows, operational_group_cost_rows) are Cost Allocation
    inputs, not outputs. Rate Builder reads no readiness / trust field, so
    the blocked Model Readiness state (C16) and breakeven revenue do not
    affect it - live checks can run on current data.
  Mechanism: calculator linked to one Cost Allocation group; only groups with
    assigned_asset_burden > 0 are offered. Top "Total job" cost =
    group_cost_per_hour x recovery hours. has_loaded guard present.
  RB-12 Two different total costs on one card. "Total job" uses the group
        rate (labour + asset + overhead [+ non-productive, C6] per group
        recovery hour). The breakdown "Total / hr" cost = Labour-tab minimum
        rate (one person, selected source, carries overhead - RB-1/RB-5) +
        group asset rate. They will not agree; the group's own labour and
        overhead rates are shown above but not used in the breakdown.
        Asset charge in the breakdown is a residual (total charge - labour
        charge) - the same subtraction pattern S28 s4 flagged in Business
        Outcome.
  RB-13 recovery hours = sum of quantities on every "hr" line; if none, the
        output-driver quantity in its own unit (m3, each...) is used as
        hours. "day" lines are not counted. Two hour lines for the same job
        (pump hire + operator) double the hours.
  RB-14 Labour-only groups cannot be priced in the calculator (design).
  RB-15 Group rate includes non-productive cost; stream rates and the labour
        minimum rate do not (C6 visible on one screen).
  RB-16 DEFAULT_CALCULATOR seeds a named real-business calculator with
        priced lines for any new user (L6 / test-data leak). Latent.
  RB-17 A calculator whose group stops being asset-backed silently shows
        no cost group, zero cost, status not_ready.
  S28   NOT BUILT: calculators keyed by linked_cost_allocation_group_id only.
  Recovery Link tab is a placeholder; the charge / cost / profit view lives
    in the Customer Charge Calculator.
  RB-6  UPDATE: pnl_derived_recovered_labour_rate IS emitted by the Labour
