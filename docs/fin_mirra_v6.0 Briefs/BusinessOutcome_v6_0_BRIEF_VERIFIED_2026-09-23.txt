===============================================================================
1. ROLE AND BOUNDARIES
===============================================================================

Business Outcome answers "is the business actually working, and who is
carrying whom?" It compares modelled revenue per source against true cost per
source and reconciles the total to real P&L revenue.

Consumes (does not own): Cost Allocation group cost rows, recovery rows and
Rate Builder labour recovery rows; Labour productive_staff_type_rates; Rate
Builder STORED user inputs (per-staff-type charge rates, group calculators,
materials markup); Revenue/COGS totals; Opening Hours; Balance Sheet; Assets
interest / depreciation (EBIT display only).

Owns: per-source modelled revenue and true cost; non-productive cost
distribution to working units; View A and View B cascades; capacity coverage
gap; health gauge baseline; per_source, the hand-off object Business Modelling
reads; the Labour Recovery overhead-per-hour figure (as of 8b F7 - previously
this one figure was sourced from Rate Builder instead, see s5 BO-2).

3. MECHANISM (verified by code read + live data)
===============================================================================

Labour sources (build_labour_sources): one row per (group, staff type). Cost =
Cost Allocation assignment assigned_cost. Overhead = the group's overhead split
between labour and asset by cost share (split_group_overhead), then by each
row's hours share. Revenue = Rate Builder saved charge rate x revenue hours.
Revenue hours: labour-driven group = own assigned hours; asset-driven group =
COVERED hours x hours share (BO-6).

Asset sources (build_asset_sources): revenue = group calculator blended rate x
COVERED hours, shared across co-deployed assets by direct-cost share. Cost =
assigned_asset_cost + asset overhead pool share by asset hours.

Covered hours (get_group_covered_hours, BO-6): asset-driven group =
min(asset seat hours, sum of assigned labour hours); otherwise the group's
recovery hours. Also written to group_real_capacity.group_recovery_hours, so
Business Modelling's lever calculations price on covered hours without being
edited.

Non-productive cost (distribute_non_productive_cost): pure-support working
units always spread; mixed units follow the stored policy (default
charge_to_self). Injected into group_real_capacity.true_cost - AND, as of 8b
Part B, also stamped onto each row as row.non_productive_share, so every
display layer (parent, ranked-drill child, build-up table) can show a child's
cost inclusive of its own support share without re-deriving it (file map
gotcha #4; BO-1, fixed 8b - see s5).

Cascades: View A - materials revenue is the residual (real revenue less labour
and asset modelled revenue); a materials shortfall is absorbed first by
profitable groups by margin, then by revenue share. View B - materials is a peer
priced at the Rate Builder markup; one global ceiling. Both headlines equal
real revenue less total true cost less unassigned. As of 8b F8/BO-11, View B's
headline also adds back any surplus (real revenue above what modelled sources
explain) as unexplained_revenue - see s5 BO-11.

Capacity coverage gap (calculate_capacity_coverage_gap): asset-driven,
labour short = revenue_at_risk (key kept; now means revenue NOT earned, valued
at asset blended rate + assigned labour's weighted charge rate); labour over =
wasted_cost; labour-driven: opportunity / data_check.

Labour Recovery overhead-per-hour (build_overhead_rate_map, 8b F7): as of this
fix, aggregated directly from per_source.labour_groups[].staff[] - summing
each staff type's overhead_share and hours across every group it appears in,
then dividing (hours-weighted average). Previously sourced from Rate Builder's
rate_builder_labour_recovery_rows, which puts 100% of a group's overhead on
labour regardless of assets (RB-1) - a different basis from every other figure
on this page. For a staff type working only in asset-less groups the two
bases are mathematically identical (asset_cost = 0 in split_group_overhead
collapses to the same 100%-on-labour result), so those rows are provably
unchanged by this fix; only staff working a productive-asset group can shift.
useBusinessOutcomeOutputContract.js was updated the same way (it now computes
its own per_source and passes labour_groups through) so AI Export, Dashboard
and Business Modelling see the same basis as the on-page card - not a stale
Rate-Builder-based copy.

Readiness: the per-source hook reads no readiness field. useBusinessOutcomeTruth
gates on revenue_cogs_ready, cost_summary_ready, business_summary_ready - NOT on
Model Readiness, so the blocked reconciliation (C16) does not stop this page.

Hours/units: hours-native; business_type not read.
