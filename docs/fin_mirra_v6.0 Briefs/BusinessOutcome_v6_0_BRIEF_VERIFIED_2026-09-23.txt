===============================================================================
1. ROLE AND BOUNDARIES
===============================================================================

Business Outcome answers "is the business actually working, and who is
carrying whom?" It compares modelled revenue per source against true cost per
source and reconciles the total to real P&L revenue.

Consumes (does not own): Cost Allocation group cost rows, recovery rows and
Rate Builder labour recovery rows; Labour productive_staff_type_rates; Rate
Builder STORED user inputs (per-staff-type charge rates, group calculators,
materials markup); Revenue/COGS totals; Opening Hours; Balance Sheet; Assets
interest / depreciation (EBIT display only).

Owns: per-source modelled revenue and true cost; non-productive cost
distribution to working units; View A and View B cascades; capacity coverage
gap; health gauge baseline; per_source, the hand-off object Business Modelling
reads.

===============================================================================
3. MECHANISM (verified by code read + live data)
===============================================================================

Labour sources (build_labour_sources): one row per (group, staff type). Cost =
Cost Allocation assignment assigned_cost. Overhead = the group's overhead split
between labour and asset by cost share (split_group_overhead), then by each
row's hours share. Revenue = Rate Builder saved charge rate x revenue hours.
Revenue hours: labour-driven group = own assigned hours; asset-driven group =
COVERED hours x hours share (BO-6).

Asset sources (build_asset_sources): revenue = group calculator blended rate x
COVERED hours, shared across co-deployed assets by direct-cost share. Cost =
assigned_asset_cost + asset overhead pool share by asset hours.

Covered hours (get_group_covered_hours, BO-6): asset-driven group =
min(asset seat hours, sum of assigned labour hours); otherwise the group's
recovery hours. Also written to group_real_capacity.group_recovery_hours, so
Business Modelling's lever calculations price on covered hours without being
edited.

Non-productive cost (distribute_non_productive_cost): pure-support working
units always spread; mixed units follow the stored policy (default
charge_to_self). Injected into group_real_capacity.true_cost only - never
written back to source rows (file map gotcha #4; BO-1 is this).

Cascades: View A - materials revenue is the residual (real revenue less labour
and asset modelled revenue); a materials shortfall is absorbed first by
profitable groups by margin, then by revenue share. View B - materials is a peer
priced at the Rate Builder markup; one global ceiling. Both headlines equal
real revenue less total true cost less unassigned.

Capacity coverage gap (calculate_capacity_coverage_gap): asset-driven,
labour short = revenue_at_risk (key kept; now means revenue NOT earned, valued
at asset blended rate + assigned labour's weighted charge rate); labour over =
wasted_cost; labour-driven: opportunity / data_check.

Readiness: the per-source hook reads no readiness field. useBusinessOutcomeTruth
gates on revenue_cogs_ready, cost_summary_ready, business_summary_ready - NOT on
Model Readiness, so the blocked reconciliation (C16) does not stop this page.

Hours/units: hours-native; business_type not read.

5. FINDINGS REGISTER
===============================================================================

BO-1 DISPLAY (open, F2). Revenue / Net Profit table
  (businessOutcomeNetProfitBuildUpSelectors.js) shows direct + overhead cost
  without the non-productive share, so revenue less shown cost != shown net
  profit for any group carrying non-productive cost. Its "reconciles" check
  compares two uninjected figures and always passes. Same flaw in
  merge_groups_by_id_real_capacity children (achieved_revenue = raw true_cost +
  injected net profit). Net profit itself is correct everywhere.
  all_sources[] carries no total_cost, so Business Modelling cannot read the
  stale cost. Fix: read group_real_capacity[].true_cost / non_productive_cost.

BO-2 DESIGN / DISPLAY (open, F7). Labour Recovery card overhead per hour uses
  Rate Builder rows (100% of group overhead on labour, RB-1); per-source rows
  split group overhead labour/asset by cost share. They differ in any group
  with a productive asset. Decide which the card should show.

BO-3 DESIGN DECISION (label open, F3). Minimum recoverable rate shown on each
  staff row is the GROUP's staff-type weighted rate (Cost Allocation group cost
  / group hours), excluding non-productive cost (C6). User decision 2026-09-23:
  correct by design - people are pooled at working-unit level, not assigned
  individually to an asset. Follow-up: label it "Group minimum rate".

BO-4 CLEANUP (open, F4). page.jsx calls useBusinessOutcomeWaterfall and
  selectBusinessOutcomeWaterfall (incl. a useModelReadiness call) and never
  renders the result.

BO-5 EXPLAINED (closed). Per-source cost per hour differs from Labour's rate
  where revenue hours differ from the staff type's own hours (asset-driven
  groups). Cost itself matches Labour to within a few dollars. The over-claim
  case it exposed is fixed by BO-6.

BO-6 FIXED (ab7d721, b668bbd). Covered-hours cap. Before: asset-driven groups
  billed the asset's full seat hours for both labour and asset even where
  assigned labour covered fewer hours, or none (an earlier "seat-hours" rule,
  recorded in code as user-confirmed; relief-cover rationale). User decision
  2026-09-23: an asset cannot earn for hours nobody is there to run it; a group
  with no labour earns nothing and is flagged to add labour. Applies to both
  "How the Business Runs" and "Best Case" (both start from modelled revenue).
  Known limits, logged not built:
    - Unattended / dry-hire / automated units need a per-working-unit "runs
      without our labour" exemption (Cost Allocation storage change).
    - One person attending several SEPARATELY billed units at the same moment
      is not modelled; put those assets in one working unit (seat = longest
      asset, one operator covers all).
    - Product / throughput businesses (e.g. quick-service food) are outside the
      hours-native model - parked with the business_type gap (kickoff s1).

BO-7 DATA (explained). Assets' calculated interest exceeds the flagged P&L
  interest line; General Overheads floors the line at zero (no double count)
  and sets general_overheads_ready false via
  has_asset_finance_interest_duplication. Excess = synthetic assets' interest +
  a small overshoot of Assets' today-dated estimate (A4). Feeds the known C16
  benchmark block. non_asset_interest_annual has no reader (dead).

BO-8 LATENT. capacity_coverage_gap values use live rates only - Business
