1. WHAT COST ALLOCATION ACTUALLY DOES (code truth, 2026-09-23)
===============================================================================

The v4 brief describes Cost Allocation as a pure working-group builder that
feeds Recovery Summary. The code differs in three structural ways, recorded
here as the truth; Section 3 lists the gaps.
  (a) Recovery Summary is UPSTREAM of Cost Allocation, not downstream.
  (b) Recovery-rate logic v4 said to remove is still here and load-bearing.
  (c) Overhead distribution is automatic only; manual assignment is dead.

INPUTS (in order of construction inside useCostAllocation)
  Labour   useLabour().output_contract -> costAllocationLabourAdapter builds
           productive_labour_type_rows, support_labour_type_rows,
           all_labour_type_rows, keyed by the REAL staff_type_id (adapter fix
           comment: an earlier version slugified labels and broke every join).
  Assets   useAssets().output_contract.active_assets (productive AND support)
           -> useCostAllocationAssetOverlay. Asset cost used is the contract
           row's asset_recovery_cost_annual = own cost + effective GO pools.
           Hours: utilisation_hours_annual, else FALLBACK to Recovery Summary's
           recovery_hours_used (C10).
  Recovery Summary
           useCostAllocation calls useRecoverySummary() itself as a fallback
           (unless inputs.recovery_summary is passed) and
           build_cost_allocation_inputs copies ~40 fields from its output
           contract. The ones that change numbers:
             overhead_absorbed_cost -> the OVERHEAD POOL (C3)
             recovery_hours_used    -> asset-hours fallback, group recovery rows
             labour_recovery_cost + asset_recovery_cost -> working_unit rate
               (dead field, C1)
             labour_share_percent   -> Rate Builder all_productive row (C24)
             recovery_summary_ready, recovery_plan_target_per_driver,
             component_required_recovery -> allocation_status (C16)
           The rest (required_recovery_rate, margin_pool, business_type,
           activity_driver_type, model_trust_state...) pass straight through
           into Cost Allocation's contract.
  Storage  useCostAllocationStorage (qs_tools_cost_allocation_v2_1): divisions,
           operational_groups, labour_group_assignments, asset_group_assignments,
           overhead_group_assignments (always empty in practice), links.
           Save effect guarded by `hydrated`. Removals are soft (is_active false).

POOLS (costAllocationRuleBuildContext -> *PoolBuilders)
  Productive labour pool: productive staff-type rows. Assignment cost =
    type total cost x assignment_percent (assigned_staff_count IGNORED - C13).
    Filtered by resolve_is_productive_assignment, which trusts the flag STAMPED
    on the assignment at creation (C17).
  Non-productive labour pool (S18): support staff-type rows, cost only, no hours.
  Productive asset pool: asset rows with asset_type != support. Cost and hours
    x assignment_percent. Joined by asset_id.
  Non-productive asset pool (S18): support asset rows, cost only.
  Overhead pool: available = Recovery Summary overhead_absorbed_cost. Hours
    mode: total_business_overheads (net of the asset pool transfer - verified
    live, Section 4). Product mode: total_cost_burden (C3b).
  Over-allocation (> 100% of a type or asset) is DETECTED and blocks status,
  but is not capped - group costs carry the excess (C14).
  All pool objects and totals live NESTED in *_assignment_result on the
  calculation result, not at its top level (root cause of C19 and C9b).

GROUP COST ROW (costAllocationGroupCostBuilder, per active valid group)
  total_group_cost = assigned productive labour cost
                   + assigned productive asset burden
                   + assigned overhead (automatic)
                   + assigned non-productive labour cost
                   + assigned non-productive asset cost
  Recovery hours: group.group_recovery_hour_source if stored, else asset_hours
    if the group carries a productive asset, else labour_hours; or manual.
    asset_hours = the MAXIMUM of the group's asset assigned hours, not the sum.
  Per-stream rates (labour/asset/overhead) = stream cost / group recovery hours.
    Non-productive cost is in total_group_cost and group_cost_per_hour but in
    NO stream rate (C6). group_cost_per_unit hard-coded 0.
  labour_coverage_warning computed when asset hours > assigned labour hours;
    not surfaced anywhere (C22).

OVERHEAD DISTRIBUTION (costAllocationOverheadPoolBuilder)
  Automatic only. Method inferred, not chosen:
    any productive labour assigned -> labour_cost_weighted (group share of
      assigned productive labour cost)
    else any asset burden         -> asset_burden_weighted
    else                          -> equal_split
  Groups with no productive labour get $0 overhead under labour weighting (C5).
  User overhead_group_assignments are accepted as a parameter and never read.
  Weights always sum to 1, so the pool is always fully assigned once any group
  exists.

GROUP RECOVERY ROWS (useCostAllocationGroupRecoveryRows + hook correction)
  Built first with a whole-business "working unit" rate from Recovery Summary
  figures, then useCostAllocation.js (157-205) OVERWRITES labour/asset/overhead
  rates and minimum_recoverable_rate_per_hour from each group's cost row.
  Consumed by Business Outcome (minimum_recoverable_rate_per_hour) and Rate
  Builder. working_unit_recovery_rate_per_hour is left on the row, stale, and
  read by nothing (C1).

RATE BUILDER ROWS (costAllocationRateBuilderSelectors)
  Per staff type: overhead taken from each group's actual split, weighted by
  the assignment's share of group labour cost - consistent with Cost Allocation.
  "All productive labour weighted rate" row: overhead = total overhead x
  Recovery Summary labour_share_percent - NOT Cost Allocation's split (C24).

STATUS / WARNINGS
  allocation_status (costAllocationStatus.js): not_supported whenever Recovery
  Summary is not ready, no recovery plan target, no component required
  recovery, an asset structure issue, or structure invalid; strained on low
  coverage; ready otherwise; forced to blocked on any over-allocated pool.
  cost_allocation_ready = ready | ready_with_dependency.
  Warnings are {warning_key, message} objects (setup + structural). Four
  structural flags are hard-coded false (C8). No cost_allocation_model_trust_state
  of its own - Recovery Summary's model_trust_state is passed through.

INSTANCES
  useCostAllocation() is called independently by the page and by six consumers
  (two Rate Builder hooks, four Business Outcome hooks). Each instance has its
  own storage state and its own useRecoverySummary() call (C15).

THE REAL CONSUMER SURFACE
  operational_group_cost_rows[]   per group: assigned_labour_cost/hours,
      assigned_asset_burden/hours, assigned_overhead_amount,
      assigned_non_productive_labour_cost, assigned_non_productive_asset_cost,

      labour_coverage_gap_hours, labour_coverage_warning, and the ENRICHED
      labour/asset/overhead/non_productive assignment lists for that group.
      Business Outcome and Rate Builder read this, not the top-level lists.
  operational_group_recovery_rows[]  minimum_recoverable_rate_per_hour etc.
  rate_builder_labour_recovery_rows[] per staff type + all_productive row.

OTHER TOTALS
  total_grouped_labour/asset/overhead/operating_cost (operating includes

  unassigned_non_productive_labour/asset_cost, total_unassigned_cost (EXCLUDES
  non-productive - C8), labour_pool_over_allocated, asset_pool_over_allocated.
  staff/asset/group_coverage_percent (productive only, S18 5.2 unchanged).

!! NAMING TRAP (C2): productive_asset_cost / support_asset_cost in THIS
   contract mean own cost + assigned pools (the overlay's recovery cost). In the
   Assets contract the same names mean own cost only (Assets A11). Never mix
   the two sources under one name.
!! TRAP: top-level labour/asset/overhead_group_assignments are the RAW stored
   records (no assigned_cost; overhead list is the unused manual list). Read
   the enriched lists on operational_group_cost_rows instead.
!! TRAP: minimum_recoverable_rate_per_hour excludes non-productive cost (C6).
!! PASSTHROUGH: labour_recovery_cost, asset_recovery_cost, labour_share_percent,
   asset_share_percent, required_recovery_rate, recovery_hours_used,
   active_recovery_model, etc. are Recovery Summary's values republished, not
   Cost Allocation's own. asset_recovery_cost / asset_share_percent carry A11.

v5.0 "accepted contract" names (recovery_hours_used, base_calculation_inputs,
fallback_recovery_summary, recovery_outputs) are INPUT wiring names in
useCostAllocation.js and costAllocationInputBuilder.js, not output fields. Only
recovery_hours_used appears in the output, as a passthrough.
