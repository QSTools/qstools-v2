===============================================================================
1. WHAT THESE MODULES ARE FOR (decided this session)
===============================================================================

Two different "per hour" questions exist in the tool. They are now kept apart.

  MACRO VIEW - Cost Summary, Business Summary.
    "What does the business cost to keep open?" Per open hour, per open day,
    per open week. Denominators come from Opening Hours only.

  PRICING VIEW - Recovery Summary (and downstream Cost Allocation, Rate
    Builder; outside the core tool, Quote Engine and legacy Business
    Modelling).
    "What must each productive person-hour recover?" Denominator is the whole
    productive team's hours (person-hours).

Before this session both views were mixed on Business Summary, and the
pricing view used one average person's hours (Labour F18), which produced a
crew rate labelled as an hourly rate.

Chain (mechanism detail in the variable tracker, Sections 15-16):
  Opening Hours -> Cost Summary -> Business Summary -> Recovery Summary
    -> Cost Allocation
  Labour / Assets / General Overheads -> Cost Summary (via Model Readiness
    modules)

===============================================================================
1. WHAT WAS WRONG
===============================================================================

calculateRecoverySummary.js used productive_asset_cost as the full productive
asset burden. That field is OWN cost only (finance interest). It excludes the
General Overheads running-cost pools assigned to productive assets (fuel,
insurance, repairs, registration). In hours mode, General Overheads is already
net of those pools, so the pools were missing from every recovery stream.

Effect: required asset recovery rate, asset_recovery_cost, the suggested split
basis and the output shares (labour / asset / overhead) were all wrong.
Recovery streams summed to less than the cost burden by the whole pool amount.

===============================================================================
2. CORRECTION TO THE ASSETS BRIEF FIX SPEC
===============================================================================

The Assets brief said useRecoverySummary "already passes"
productive_asset_assigned_overhead_cost_annual in (~line 149). It does not.
Lines 148-153 of useRecoverySummary are the OUTPUT contract passthrough. The
calculation receives Business Summary outputs spread at top level plus the
Assets contract nested as input.assets. Business Summary does not publish the
pool figure. The fix therefore reads it from input.assets (the same source the
output passthrough uses), with input.* checked first.

===============================================================================

  P1 New constants: productive_asset_assigned_overhead_cost (from the Assets
