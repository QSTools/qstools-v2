1. WHAT BUSINESS MODELLING IS (principle - KEPT)
===============================================================================
A what-if tool: "what happens if I change a rate, a markup, or a cost?" Built on Business
Outcome's real per_source data. Rule: Business Modelling OWNS STATE, NOT BUSINESS
CALCULATIONS. It starts from the unchanged Business Outcome baseline, applies explicit
adjustments through the existing calculation owners, never mutates Outcome, and shows
current vs modelled. Revenue / net profit / cash are consequences, not levers.
Levers are real business decisions (charge-out rates, markup, overhead lines that are a
genuine choice, training/PPE/allowances, staffing/hours, financing choices).
Structurally fixed facts are NEVER levers: ACC rate, employer KiwiSaver, ESCT, insurance
premiums, market fuel prices. (acc_manual_override corrects a real rate; it is not a lever.)
Locked boundaries (S05, still valid): does not overwrite Labour/Assets/General Overheads/
Cost Summary/Revenue Summary/Business Summary; does not override Model Readiness; does not
infer or patch missing source truth.
Copilot briefs (09-16) are authoritative for CONCEPT only. Their 8-phase sequencing/effort
was reasoned without repo access and was NOT adopted.

4. WHY TWO ATTEMPTS FAILED (09-18) - KEEP, DO NOT REPEAT
===============================================================================
Attempt 1 - lever card headline from View B real-capacity cascade: the cascade is designed to
  reconcile to today's real revenue minus real cost, so the TOTAL cannot move whatever the
  lever; only WHICH sources are "carried" moves. Reverted.
Attempt 2 - buildLiveLeverHeadline (independent engine): sums every unit's own uncapped
  claim (rate x assumed hours, no cross-subsidy), so untouched units jump to their standalone
  ceiling and dominate; arithmetic correct, meaning useless for "what if I change one thing".
Both were found 09-18, BEFORE the 09-23 Business Outcome changes (BO-6 covered-hours cap,
  non-productive distribution, F8 unexplained revenue in View B headline). Re-test against
  current code before relying on either conclusion.
