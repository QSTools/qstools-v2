1. THE DESIGN (owner's flow, confirmed 2026-09-24)
===============================================================================
Business Modelling walks down the same chain the business runs on, top to bottom:
  0. TODAY - the real achieved position from Business Outcome. Read-only. Never mutated by
     modelling. (Real net profit on 2026-09-24 data: -$406,923.)
  1. OVERHEADS - what remains in Cost Summary after direct attribution (vehicle/fuel to assets,
     insurance, finance, and labour on-costs pulled to where they belong). Changing a remaining
     line changes every downstream minimum recoverable rate. First lever because it moves the
     floor for everything below. (Owner's "full circle", 09-18 handover s3.)
  2. LABOUR - per staff type: minimum recoverable rate and charge-out rate. Change the
     charge-out rate (or a genuine on-cost lever); flows into the asset rates that depend on it.
  3. ASSETS and COG MARGIN - asset blended rates and the materials markup, last, because they
     depend on steps 1-2.
  4. RESULT - after each change, net profit = today's real total + the effect of ONLY what was
     touched. Every other source stays exactly at today's achieved figure.
Every source is shown against three markers:
  FLOOR   = breakeven (minimum recoverable rate / breakeven markup)
  START   = current achieved rate from Business Outcome
  CEILING = Best Case: entered rate x the hours it can cover, uncapped by real revenue
            (Business Outcome's "Best Case (100% of Entered Rates & Hours)")
You are always moving a source from its START toward its CEILING and seeing how far above the
FLOOR it ends. FLOOR / START / CEILING as the frame for this page is the owner's decision
2026-09-24; it is not written in any earlier document.
LIVE NET PROFIT (owner requirement 2026-09-24): as any number changes, the effect on net profit
is visible. PROPOSED (open UI choice): keep TODAY fixed and show MODELLED and the CHANGE beside it
(e.g. Today -$407k -> Modelled -$291k, +$116k). Alternative: move the headline itself.
Method: use the EXISTING calculators through injection points (owner: that is why the injection
points were built). Business Modelling owns state, not business calculations.

5. THE BEHAVIOUR RULE AND EVIDENCE
===============================================================================
Rule (owner, agreed 09-18, reconfirmed 09-24): touch one lever -> recalculate ONLY that source
from its real cost and the new figure; every other source stays at today's real achieved figure;
total = today's real total + the effect of the change.
Why: (Attempt 1, 09-18) View B real-capacity cascade always reconciles to real revenue, so the
total cannot move whatever the lever - it only re-splits who is carried. (Attempt 2) The independent
engine summed every unit's own uncapped claim, so untouched units jumped to standalone ceilings.
Both conclusions pre-date the 09-23 Business Outcome changes (covered-hours cap, F8). Attempt 2's
dominant distortion (PC15 ~$295k) is gone - PC15 now has 0 covered hours.
Evidence 2026-09-24: the existing per-lever maths is exactly additive. With Materials at 5%
(from 15%) on COGS $1,240,091 = -$124,009 and Site Crew +$5/h x 1,414.12 h = +$7,071, the lever
card headline moved by -$116,938 = the sum of the two; nothing else moved. So "effect of one
change" can be computed reliably per source. Whether it should be computed by the existing
calculators via overrides (preferred, per owner) or by that lever maths is a build decision - to
be settled by the re-test in s4 (NOT YET PROVEN).
