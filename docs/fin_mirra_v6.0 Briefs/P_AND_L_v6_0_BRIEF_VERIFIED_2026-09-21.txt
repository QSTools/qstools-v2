alignment, carried forward.

2. HOW THE P&L WORKS (live code, [CODE-READ] + [VERIFIED])
===============================================================================
Storage: qs_tools_profit_and_loss_state (localStorage), hydrate-then-save guard
  (has_hydrated). Hook: hooks/useProfitAndLoss.js returns status, card,
  output_contract. Calculation: lib/calculations/profitAndLossCalculations.js
  (calculateProfitAndLoss). Selector: lib/selectors/profitAndLossSelectors.js
  (builds the card and display fields).
Line shape (fields that matter): pnl_line_id, line_name, amount (may be stored
  as text; calculation converts), section, category, direct_cost_category_id,
  review_subcategory, interest_treatment.
Live sections: trading_income, cost_of_sales, other_income, operating_expenses.
  (No other_expenses section exists.)
Live categories: revenue, cogs, labour, assets, general_overheads, excluded,
  unassigned, review_required.
THREE groupings are computed from the same lines:
  A. SECTION-BASED: total_trading_income, total_cost_of_sales,
     total_other_income, total_operating_expenses, gross_profit, net_profit
     (net = gross + other income - operating expenses).
  B. CATEGORY-BASED: total_revenue (category revenue, any section);
     total_direct_costs = total_cogs (cogs lines, summed by ACTIVE direct-cost
     groups in state.direct_cost_categories); labour / assets /
     general_overheads benchmarks (operating-expense lines by category);
     unassigned_balance; total_business_costs = labour + assets +
     general_overheads + unassigned.
  C. INTEREST: pnl_interest_total and related totals match "interest" in the
     line name; the marked / unreviewed / reviewed totals use operating
     expenses only, pnl_interest_total uses all sections.
Direct-cost groups are created by the importer from the imported cost-of-sales
  accounts (one per account) and are owned in P&L state; Revenue/COGS reads
  them. (v4.0 said P&L must not finalise COG categories; the code does group
  them, and Revenue/COGS depends on it. The brief now follows the code.)
Readiness: pnl_ready = no review lines, where a review line is unassigned /
  review_required, or cogs with a missing/review direct-cost group, and NOT a
  completely blank row (see Section 5, X2).

3. WHO READS WHAT (code read + live checks)
===============================================================================
Revenue/COGS (hooks/useRevenueCogs.js) reads: total_revenue, total_direct_costs,
  revenue_lines, cost_of_sales_lines, direct_cost_categories,
  direct_cost_category_totals. => downstream (Business Summary, Recovery,
  Business Outcome, Business Modelling) sees the CATEGORY-BASED pair.
The SECTION-BASED pair reaches only the P&L page, Model Readiness audit panel,
  the cross-import check and one Business Outcome card reference (unchecked).
lib/reconciliation/reconciliationRules.js reads: total_business_costs (as the
  P&L side of the business cost benchmark), unassigned_balance, pnl_ready (as
  "Profit & Loss readiness"), excluded_asset_finance_interest_total (capped
  redistribution from general overheads to asset finance, S22),
  employee_overheads_benchmark_total (always 0, see F14), labour lines (wages vs
  on-cost split by NAME pattern, see F13).
Business Summary page and Model Readiness read total_business_costs.
hooks/general-overheads/generalOverheadPnlSync.js consumes P&L lines [NOT
  TRACED in P2.2 - belongs to P2.5].
business_type: NOT read anywhere in the P&L core files (hook, calculation,
  selector, storage, importer, route). P&L is money-only and hours/units-neutral.
