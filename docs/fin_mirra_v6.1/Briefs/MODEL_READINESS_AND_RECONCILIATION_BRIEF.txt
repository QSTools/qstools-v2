1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Module Reconciliation answers: "do the operational modules (Labour, Assets, General Overheads) add up to what the
P&L says the business spent, and if not, why?" It compares each module with its P&L benchmark and says whether a
difference is a real problem or just the accountant not having journaled something yet.
Model Readiness answers: "is it safe to build on this model?" It turns the reconciliation checks into one status
that gates what comes after (Cost Summary trust, Business Summary trust, Recovery Summary, Cost Allocation, Quote
Engine, Business Modelling, the AI export).

Principle (S21): 100 percent reconciliation to the P&L will never happen and is not the goal. The question is "close
enough to trust, or a problem hiding in the numbers?" Variance is evidence, not an error. Only ONE check blocks on a
number (Section 3, R2).

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: the list of reconciliation checks and their ids, the tolerances and dollar floors, the timing-versus-error
  classification, the benchmark redistribution for asset finance interest, the manual-accept overlay, the Model
  Readiness groups and overall status (ready / warning / blocked), and the readiness flags
  (is_ready_for_modelling, is_ready_for_ai_export, is_ready_for_dashboard).
DOES NOT OWN: any module's own total (it reads Labour, Assets, General Overheads and the P&L and changes none of
  them), the P&L benchmark amounts, the Cost Summary and Business Summary trust fields that carry its verdict
  downstream (Summaries brief R8).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Reconciliation reads module output contracts and the P&L output contract only. It never edits, corrects or
    hides a variance. No other place may calculate Labour-versus-P&L variance (S21: the old duplicate in
    labourStatusSelectors.js, hard-coded benchmark 0 so it always read "Ready", was deleted).
R2  The ONLY numeric blocker is business_cost_variance: blocking when module total (Labour + Assets + General
    Overheads) differs from the P&L setup-module benchmark by more than 1 percent, OR from the full P&L
    total_business_costs by more than 1 percent. Everything else numeric is a warning at most.
R3  Other blocking checks are readiness facts, not variances: Profit & Loss ready, Labour ready, General Overheads
    ready, Assets ready (assets_ready passes when active assets are ready, or no assets are confirmed with zero asset
    cost), and employee_overheads (blocking if the P&L still carries employee overheads).
R4  Per-module variance checks (labour_variance with sub-checks labour_wages_variance and labour_on_costs_variance,
    asset_finance_variance, general_overheads_variance) use a 1 percent threshold and are NEVER blocking.
R5  Timing-aware classification (S21): only module > P&L can be "timing_expected" (not a warning, message says trust
    the module). Module < P&L is always a warning, even on a timing-aware check. Timing-aware checks: asset finance
    and labour on-costs. Labour wages is deliberately NOT timing-aware (genuine errors only).
R6  Dollar-floor fallback: when a timing-aware check's P&L benchmark is below a low-denominator threshold, materiality
    is judged against a flat dollar tolerance instead of the percentage. Asset finance: below 500, floor 250.
    Labour on-costs: below 300, floor 150. Placeholders, not tuned on multiple businesses (S21 s10).
R7  Asset finance interest benchmark redistribution (S22): covered = min(P&L interest flagged "contains asset
    finance", Assets' own calculated interest). The covered amount is moved out of the General Overheads benchmark
    and into the Assets benchmark before the checks run; the uncovered remainder stays in General Overheads. The
    P&L side of this mirrors what General Overheads does to its own total (General Overheads brief R4).
R8  Three asset-finance outcomes share the check id asset_finance_variance: (a) a P&L interest line is flagged ->
    a COVERAGE check (module <= P&L total interest + 1 is "covered", more than that is a warning); (b) a line was
    reviewed and marked "no asset finance interest" while Assets has real interest -> a MISCLASSIFICATION warning
    (not a timing lag); (c) nothing flagged or reviewed -> the plain timing-aware variance check.
R9  Manual accept (S20): only plain "warn" checks that are not blocking can be accepted, with a reason and the
    variance amount at acceptance. If the variance moves by more than 1 after acceptance the check reopens and keeps
    the stale record. Blocking, pass, covered and timing_expected checks ignore acceptances.
R10 Model Readiness status: blocked when any check is_blocking, else warning when any non-blocking check is a
    warning, else ready. Warnings never block. A blocked model means the downstream trust chain reads "not trusted".

4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================

calculateModuleReconciliation passes their status and output contracts to buildModuleReconciliationChecks.

===============================================================================
5. OUTPUTS AND CONSUMERS
===============================================================================
Reconciliation result: reconciliation_checks, blocking_checks, warning_checks, blocking_modules, warning_modules,
  reconciliation_ready (no blocking checks), module_total_business_costs, benchmarks and per-check variance amounts
  and percents, normalised_reconciliation_inputs (the inputs the page shows), plus passthrough totals.
Check ids (READ IN CODE): profit_&_loss_ready, labour_ready, general_overheads_ready, assets_ready,
  employee_overheads, running_cost_annual (legacy asset running cost, warning), business_cost_variance (module
  "Model Readiness"), labour_variance, labour_wages_variance, labour_on_costs_variance, asset_finance_variance,
  general_overheads_variance. Check statuses: pass, fail, warn, timing_expected, covered, accepted.
Model Readiness result adds: overall_status, model_ready, model_readiness_status, blocking_items, warning_items,
  passed_items, readiness_groups (source_inputs = Profit & Loss checks, module_outputs = Labour / Assets / General
  Overheads checks, reconciliation = the business_cost_variance check, traceability, modelling_readiness),
  is_ready_for_modelling (= no blockers), is_ready_for_ai_export (= is_ready_for_modelling),
  is_ready_for_dashboard (= at least one check exists), last_checked_at.
Consumers: Cost Summary (model_readiness_status), Business Summary (model_trust_state), Recovery Summary
  ("not_trusted"), Cost Allocation ("not_supported" when blocked upstream), Quote Engine and legacy Business
  Modelling (tracker s16 / Summaries brief). The AI export and dashboard flags are provided by this module; their
  readers were not re-traced for this brief. Business Outcome is NOT gated on Model Readiness (it uses revenue_cogs_ready,
  cost_summary_ready, business_summary_ready - Business Outcome brief R10).

8. FINDINGS REGISTER (keep numbers; update status in place)
===============================================================================
OPEN - DESIGN
MR-1  "General Overheads is not ready" is a BLOCKING check driven by status.is_ready, which is true only when the
      overhead profile has a name AND at least one profile has been saved (General Overheads brief GO-1). A business
      with reconciling numbers but no saved overhead profile is blocked. READ IN CODE; confirm on a fresh browser
      profile.
MR-2  The employee_overheads check can never fail (P&L employee_overheads_benchmark_total is hard-coded 0) - it is
      a dead blocker (tracker P2.2). Remove it or feed it.
MR-3  The "traceability" readiness group is static: status "warning" with one hard-coded passing check whose text
      says the pilot shell "is implemented and the first selected output chain is source-verified". That text is
      out of date (the live calculation trace now covers more), and the group never changes. It does not affect
      overall_status.
MR-4  business_cost_variance can block on the FULL P&L figure even when every module matches its own benchmark
      (any unassigned or uncategorised P&L cost above 1 percent). Intended as "unexplained"; the message does not say
      which of the two comparisons failed.
MR-5  Cosmetic: "timing_expected", "covered" and "accepted" checks show with the same Variance pill styling as a
      warning (S21 s10); statuses other than "pass" are not counted in passed_count of a group.
OPEN - CHECKS
MR-6  Scenario B (R2, 2026-10-18) showed "Cost Summary data isn't fully trusted" and "Partial - some upstream
      sources not yet ready"; Addendum 10 guessed the missing Balance Sheet. READ IN CODE: no reconciliation check
      reads the Balance Sheet, so a blocking check (most likely MR-1 or business_cost_variance) is the likelier
      cause. Not chased. Open Model Readiness on the stress data and record which check is blocking.
MR-7  Dollar floors (250 / 500 and 150 / 300) and the 1 percent labour wages tolerance are untuned (S21 s10).
MR-8  Labour vs P&L: about 160k variance unexplained after the synthetic staff (Summaries S-L1); owner remuneration
      setting (payroll / shareholder salary / drawings) is a design item for Labour and Reconciliation (Business
      Outcome 8b). The wages / on-cost split matches line names by substring ("acc" also matches accrued, account;
      Labour F3, P&L F13). Better key is review_subcategory.
MR-9  The P&L page's own reconciliation panel compares against model_total_costs, which nothing produces (P&L F4,
      looks orphaned). The only real P&L-to-macro bridge is Business Summary's Macro Position card (Summaries R7).
OPEN - NOT BUILT FROM THE v4.0 SPEC
MR-10 No six-state model trust object, no per-module reconciliation object (source_amount / tolerance_amount /
      reviewed fields exist only on the checks that were built), no variance_percent-based "leak_detected" status.
CLOSED
Duplicate Labour variance calculation (S21 s6); S20 manual accept built (rescoped to residual warn cases only).

10. OUT OF SCOPE FOR ANY RECONCILIATION OR READINESS CHANGE
===============================================================================
Do not recalculate Labour, Assets, General Overheads or the P&L here. Do not make a variance check blocking without an
explicit owner decision. Do not auto-correct a variance. Do not add a second place that compares a module with the
P&L. Do not change a check id without searching for readers (the trust chain reads them by id and by module name).
