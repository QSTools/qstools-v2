1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Both pages answer the quoting question: "does this job pay its way?" - but from different directions.

QUOTE ENGINE (saved quotes, full job build-up): type a quote (price, COG cost and markup, allowed labour hours,
  labour hourly cost). It works out the margin pool left after the job's own costs and whether that margin pays the
  business's required recovery for the hours allowed. It keeps quote jobs and versions, offers a what-if repair
  panel, and says whether the model it tests against can be trusted.
QUOTE CHECKER (quick test, nothing saved): type labour charge, labour hours and material cost / charge. It shows the
  implied labour charge rate and gross profit percent and compares them with the selected model, using the
  AI-readable business state export as its source of truth.

Both are READ-ONLY consumers of the model. They never change source business truth.
Outside this brief: the Quote Manager (separate repo, sidebar link), purchase order and invoice reconciliation
(Launch Build Order brief) - not built here.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: Quote Engine - the quote store (jobs, versions, current quote), quote-level maths, the result status
  (works / marginal / leaking / invalid), quote warnings, the repair what-if. Quote Checker - its own quote inputs
  (page state only) and its alignment status.
DOES NOT OWN: the required recovery rate (Cost Summary), the productive labour cost rate (Labour), the trust state
  (Model Readiness / Business Summary), the selected model and permissions (AI Business State export), anything
  about cost allocation or rates.

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Required recovery rate is the business-wide figure from Cost Summary (required_recovery_rate = total cost burden
    / whole productive team's hours, F18). The quote's hours are person-hours, so the two are on the same basis.
R2  The quote's labour_hourly_cost_rate is TYPED by the user. The model's productive_labour_cost_rate (Labour
    contract) is used only to compare (labour_cost_variance), never to replace the typed rate.
R3  Quote Engine formulas (READ IN CODE, quoteEngineCalculations.js; calc map s11):
      cog_sell_total       = cog_cost_total x (1 + cog_markup_percent / 100)
      labour_charge_total  = max(0, quote_price_total - cog_sell_total)
      labour_cost_total    = labour_hours_allowed x labour_hourly_cost_rate
      estimated_job_cost   = cog_cost_total + labour_cost_total
      quote_margin_pool    = quote_price_total - estimated_job_cost       (uses COG COST, not COG sell)
      business_recovery_required_total = labour_hours_allowed x required_recovery_rate
      quote_gap            = quote_margin_pool - business_recovery_required_total
      hourly_gap           = achieved margin per hour - required_recovery_rate
      max_viable_hours     = quote_margin_pool / required_recovery_rate
      required_price_at_allowed_hours = estimated_job_cost + business_recovery_required_total
      price_gap            = required_price_at_allowed_hours - quote_price_total
    Every divide is guarded (0 instead of an error).
R4  Result status (READ IN CODE, buildQuoteEngineStatus): invalid when quote price, labour hours or required
    recovery rate is 0, or the model is untrusted; else leaking when margin pool >= 0 and quote_gap < 0; else
    marginal when |quote_gap| <= max(1, 10 percent of required_recovery_rate) or the Business Summary has warnings;
    else works. See QE-1: this ordering has a hole.
R5  Model untrusted = quote_model_trust_state is not "ready" OR model_ready is not true. Trust state =
    Business Summary model_trust_state, else Cost Summary model_readiness_status, else ready/blocked from
    model_ready. A "warning" level trust state therefore makes every quote invalid (Summaries R8: warnings are
    meant not to block).
R6  Quote Checker maths (READ IN CODE, quoteCheckerCalculations.js): total_quote_charge = labour + material + other
    direct charge; total_direct_cost = material + other direct cost (labour is NOT a direct cost here);
    gross_profit = charge - direct cost; implied_labour_charge_rate = labour charge / hours;
    labour_recovery_gap = implied rate - required labour recovery (first non-zero of the selected model's
    required_recovery_per_driver, required_recovery_rate, then the Business Summary's). Status: blocked (no
    permission, no hours or no charge), warning (rate below recovery, or gross profit below target margin), aligned.
R7  Quote Checker input is page state only: nothing is saved, and the page opens on a built-in sample quote
    (40 hours, 4,000 labour, 2,500 material cost, 3,250 material charge).

4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
Quote Engine reads: Cost Summary output_contract (required_recovery_rate, total_productive_output,
total_cost_burden, people / asset / overhead totals, model_ready, model_readiness_status), Business Summary
(model_trust_state, warnings), Labour output_contract (productive_labour_cost_rate). It builds its own Cost Summary
instance (the same separate-instance pattern as tracker C15).
Quote Checker reads: useAiBusinessState (export_object.selected_model, business_summary, downstream_permissions,
trust_state, blockers, warnings).

Storage: qs_tools_quote_engine_store_v4 (lib/storage/quoteEngineStorage.js): quote_jobs, quote versions, the current
quote state. Loaded on mount, saved after has_loaded_quote_store (guard present). Listed in the S0 register as off
the hours-mode chain (copy baseline). Quote Checker stores nothing.

5. OUTPUTS AND CONSUMERS
===============================================================================
Quote Engine output_contract (buildQuoteEngineOutputContract plus status): quote price and cost breakdown, margin
pool and percent, business_recovery_required_total, achieved_margin_per_hour, required_recovery_rate, hourly_gap,
quote_gap, max_viable_hours, required_price_at_allowed_hours, price_gap, quote_result_status, quote_warnings,
quote_model_used ("balanced-cog-markup-plus-labour-hourly-cost"), quote_model_trust_state, job and client identity
fields. Legacy names are kept as aliases (material_*, labour_sell_total, true_labour_cost_total; the
direct_cost_package_* fields are hard-coded 0).
Consumers: none confirmed. No consumer of either output was found while reading the two hooks; a repo-wide search
for readers of the Quote Engine output_contract has NOT been done (do it before changing a field name). Quote
Checker's result is shown on its own page.
Warning ids (Quote Engine): missing_job_identity, missing_client_name, missing_quote_date, no_quote_price,
no_labour_hours, no_estimated_labour_cost, no_required_recovery_rate, estimated_labour_below_model,
negative_margin_pool, zero_estimated_job_cost, untrusted_model, model_warning_present.
Quote Checker ids: quote_checker_permission_blocked, labour_hours_missing, quote_charge_missing,
labour_recovery_below_model, gross_profit_below_target.

8. FINDINGS REGISTER (keep numbers; update status in place)
===============================================================================
OPEN - DESIGN
QE-1  Status hole: a quote that loses money before recovery (margin pool < 0) never reaches "leaking" (that branch
      needs margin pool >= 0) and its quote_gap is far larger than the marginal tolerance, so it falls through to
      "works" unless the Business Summary happens to have warnings (then "marginal"). The negative_margin_pool
      warning still appears. READ IN CODE; reproduce with a price below COG cost before changing anything.
QE-2  Marginal tolerance mixes units: a dollar gap on the whole job is compared with 10 percent of an HOURLY rate
      (about $8.50 on an $85 rate whatever the job size). Marginal is also unreachable on the short side, because a
      positive margin pool with a negative gap is classed "leaking" first.
QE-3  Trust: a "warning" trust state makes every quote "invalid" (R5), the opposite of the warnings-do-not-block rule
      in Summaries R8. Decide whether the quote pages should treat warning as usable.
QE-4  S-H3: the rate Quote Engine prices against (required_recovery_rate) changed basis with F18; no number check.
QE-5  Quote Checker uses a different recovery source (selected model / export) from Quote Engine (Cost Summary), and
      treats labour as margin rather than a direct cost. The two pages can disagree on the same job. Decide whether
      that is wanted or one should call the other's maths.
OPEN - CHECKS
QE-6  Quote Engine and Quote Checker are not exercised by any R2 harness scenario. Add an expected-values check for
      one worked quote before launch (Launch Build Order brief: regression suite).
QE-7  The v4.0 locked brief describes a "Quote Checker" with model-selection and permission outputs; the code's
      Quote Engine page is a different, later build (balanced COG-markup plus labour model). Do not carry v4.0
      Quote Checker field names onto the Quote Engine.
OPEN - NOT BUILT FROM THE v4.0 SPEC
QE-8  Structured quote warnings and trust objects as specified in v4.0 are not built; the ids above are the real
      set.
