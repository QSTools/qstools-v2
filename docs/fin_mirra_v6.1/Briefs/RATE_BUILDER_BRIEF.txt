===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Rate Builder answers "what rate recovers it?". The owner sets charge-out rates per staff type and builds customer
charge calculators (per asset, or legacy per working unit), and sees charge vs cost vs profit against the cost
floors that Labour and Cost Allocation produce.

KEY FACT (V3 scope insight): Rate Builder publishes NO output contract. What leaves it is STORED USER INPUT only -
per-staff-type charge-out rates and calculators. Its calculated figures (minimum rates, job cost, profit) are
display-only; Business Outcome re-derives what it needs. So Rate Builder display bugs do not propagate; storage
keys and ids do.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: charge-out rates per staff type; customer charge calculators and their lines (rate, quantity, unit, output
driver); charge vs cost vs profit display; materials markup (Path C).
DOES NOT OWN (consumes only): labour cost and staff-type rates (Labour); group cost, overhead split, recovery hours
and minimum recoverable rates (Cost Allocation); asset cost (Assets); revenue and verdicts (Business Outcome).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
L1  One owner per calculation: Labour owns labour cost; Cost Allocation owns group cost and the overhead split;
    Rate Builder compares charge to cost.
L2  Minimum recoverable labour rate = productive labour cost rate + allocated overhead rate. Non-productive labour is
    NOT loaded into the rate.
L3  All-productive weighted charge rate is read-only: sum(staff-type rate x productive hours) / productive hours.
L4  (UPDATED 2026-10-01, RB-1) Within a group, overhead is split labour / asset by the SHARED split_group_overhead
    (lib/calculations/shared/overheadSplit.js) and the labour pool is spread across staff types by HOURS (cost as
    fallback). Same rule as Business Outcome - one definition, never duplicated.
L5  Productive asset running costs stay out of the labour overhead pool (pool transfer to Assets upstream).
L6  Never invent labour charge rates, market rates or product rates. A staff type with no saved rate is
    INCOMPLETE - no fallback rate, never $0.
L7  Margin and markup are distinct (margin sell = cost / (1 - m); markup sell = cost x (1 + k)). NOT BUILT in
    Path A (RB-4).
L8  P&L implied labour benchmark belongs to Business Outcome, not Labour Rates Builder (RB-Q5).
L9  PER-ASSET PRICING (owner 2026-10-04 / 10-09): each asset gets its own calculator; the group price is the SUM of
    its assets' prices - a result, never an input. An asset-linked calculator prices the MACHINE ONLY; the operator
    is charged once, through the Labour tab rate, on the labour row. Legacy group calculators keep the all-in rate.
L10 Labour in a group's calculator comes from the group's Cost Allocation assignments and the saved per-staff-type
    rates; weighted by TOTAL ASSIGNED hours (same basis as Business Outcome's labour mix - D-IDLE-4). It is an
    hours-weighted average; a two-person crew does not show both rates added.
L11 Hard block (owner 2026-10-14): while a group's labour is incomplete (unpriced staff type -> points to Labour Rates
    Builder; no labour or no recovery hours -> points to Cost Allocation), adding / editing lines is locked; Delete
    stays; saved lines and totals are unchanged and still counted. A calculator with no group selected is never
    blocked. Messages name the staff type, never a person.
L12 The group dropdown placeholder is inert - it never unlinks a calculator (OPEN-D fix).

4. INPUTS AND STORAGE (what actually propagates)
===============================================================================
Reads: Labour contract (productive_staff_type_rates, weighted_all_productive_labour_rate,
  pnl_derived_recovered_labour_rate - carried, not rendered); Cost Allocation (rate_builder_labour_recovery_rows,
  operational_group_cost_rows, labour assignments); its own storage. Reads no readiness / trust field.
Storage (the real outputs):
  qstools_rate_builder_labour_source_rates_v1   per staff_type_id charge-out rates (NOTE: no underscore after "qs").
                                                One global map overlaid on every model (RB-11). Read by Business
                                                Outcome (readRateBuilderLabourSourceRates).
  qs_tools_rate_builder_calculators_v1          calculators: linked_cost_allocation_group_id, linked_asset_id
                                                ("" = legacy group calculator), lines, output driver.
  rateBuilderLabourRatesStorage.js, rateBuilderMaterialsMarkupStorage.js
Default seed: an unlinked "2-inch line pump calculator" is re-created whenever storage is empty (harmless; Business
Outcome ignores it). Returning to the calculator tab always reopens it - re-select group and asset each time.

===============================================================================
5. THE THREE PATHS
===============================================================================
PATH A  Labour Rates Builder - per-staff-type and all-productive minimum recoverable rates vs saved charge-out
        rates (useRateBuilderLabourRates, rateBuilderLabourSourceSelectors build_recovery_fields). Upstream rows from
        costAllocationRateBuilderSelectors.js.
PATH B  Customer Charge Calculator - per-asset (or legacy group) calculators. Asset-linked cost per hour =
        (assigned asset cost + hours-share of the group asset overhead pool) / asset hours. Labour breakdown by staff
        type (rateBuilderLabourByStaffType.js) with collapsed detail; pricing block (rateBuilderPricingBlock.js).
        Recovery hours = sum of "hr" line quantities, else output-driver quantity (RB-13).
PATH C  Materials markup (useRateBuilderMaterialsMarkup) - feeds Business Outcome View B materials (closed 8b, F6/H5).
Recovery Link tab is a placeholder.

      from the per-staff-type rows (C24 / R3). Fix candidate: all_productive = sum of staff-type rows. Add to the R2
      layer-2 expected values when fixed.

      staff-type row. Latent.
RB-12 (partly superseded by L9 / L10) Legacy group calculators: "Total job" uses the group rate while the breakdown
      uses labour + asset; asset charge is a residual.
RB-13 Recovery hours = sum of all "hr" lines; two hour lines for the same job double the hours; "day" lines not
      counted. "Per <unit>" row divides by output-driver quantity, not recovery hours ($125 vs $119.05).

10. OUT OF SCOPE FOR ANY RATE BUILDER CHANGE
===============================================================================
Do not compute group cost, overhead distribution or recovery hours here (Cost Allocation). Do not invent rates.
Do not duplicate split_group_overhead. Changing a storage key or id shape IS a cross-module change (Business Outcome
reads both stores).
