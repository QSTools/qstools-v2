===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
General Overheads answers: "what does the business cost to keep running, apart from the people (Labour) and the
machines (Assets)?" It reads the P&L operating-expense lines, classifies them, takes asset finance interest out of
the pool (so Assets owns it once), and publishes ONE number, total_general_overheads, plus the lines behind it.

It is a SOURCE module (v4.0 principle kept): it defines the pool; Cost Allocation decides how the pool is spread.
It does not distribute overhead and it does not decide the recovery model.

Chain: P&L operating expenses -> General Overheads -> Cost Summary -> (Business Summary -> Recovery Summary ->
Cost Allocation). General Overheads and Assets also feed Module Reconciliation (Model Readiness brief).

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: total_general_overheads (after the asset-finance interest adjustment), the overhead rows and their category,
  the category totals, the asset-related running-cost pools (what sits in each pool and how much is assigned to
  assets), the interest split (non_asset_interest_annual), user-created custom overhead lines, manual per-line
  overrides, and the saved overhead profile list.
DOES NOT OWN: wages and on-costs (Labour), asset ownership cost and asset interest (Assets), COGS and materials
  (P&L / Revenue-COGS), how overhead is spread over groups (Cost Allocation), the P&L itself. It reads the P&L and
  the Assets saved records; it never writes to either.

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  One source of overhead truth: total_general_overheads is the figure Cost Summary uses. Cost Summary must not
    rebuild categories or spread overhead (v4.0 rule, still the design).
R2  Which P&L lines sync in: every P&L operating-expense line EXCEPT categories revenue, cogs, direct_costs,
    labour, assets, excluded. Legacy category employee_overheads is mapped to general_overheads - there is no
    separate Employee Overheads module. READ IN CODE (generalOverheadPnlSync.js).
R3  Interest lines are recognised by the word "interest" in the P&L line name. interest_treatment on the line
    decides the rest: "asset_finance_exclude" = contains_asset_finance_interest; "general_overhead_keep" = no
    asset finance interest; anything else = not_reviewed.
R4  Asset finance interest is owned by Assets. A P&L interest row flagged "contains asset finance interest" has the
    ASSETS module's own interest total subtracted: active_amount = max(P&L amount - row share of Assets interest, 0).
    With more than one flagged row, the Assets total is split in proportion to each row's P&L amount. The remainder
    stays in General Overheads (a flagged line can be blended with overdraft or credit-card interest).
R5  The adjustment floors at zero (BO-7): if Assets claims more interest than the flagged P&L line, the row goes to
    0, never negative. When Assets interest is larger than the flagged P&L rows combined (this includes the case
    where NO P&L line is flagged but Assets has interest), the contract sets has_asset_finance_interest_duplication
    and output_contract.general_overheads_ready becomes false. READ IN CODE; the no-flagged-line case has not been
    seen live.
R6  Asset-related running costs (fuel, repairs and maintenance, registration and compliance, insurance, consumables)
    are held in General Overheads as POOLS until an asset takes them. What an asset takes moves out of General
    Overheads into Assets; Cost Summary adds the pool transfer to Assets and subtracts it from General Overheads,
    so the fuel is counted once and the total burden does not change (smoke test E.4).
R7  A partly assigned pool leaves the remainder in General Overheads (warning asset_pool_partially_allocated); a
    pool with more assigned than the P&L total is flagged asset_pool_over_allocated.
R8  The page is the override path, not the entry path: P&L lines auto-sync into synced_pnl_overhead_items. The
    ~30 named per-line fields (staff_overheads_cost etc.) exist only as a manual override if auto-categorisation is
    wrong (smoke test 1.5 finding). Principal repayments, GST, tax and drawings do not belong here.
R9  Variance to the P&L is evidence, not an error: it is shown by Module Reconciliation (general_overheads_variance,
    never blocking), not by this module.

===============================================================================
4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
Inputs: P&L operating-expense lines (read live from the P&L state through calculateProfitAndLoss and re-synced
whenever the sync signature changes), Assets saved records (read through useAssetStorage - NOT useAssets, to avoid a
circular hook dependency because useAssets imports useGeneralOverheads), custom lines, manual overrides.
Assets interest used = sum over non-retired saved assets of asset_interest_annual (falling back to interest_annual).
That is a stored calculated field (Assets A4: a today-dated straight-line estimate).

Storage (keys: STORAGE_KEY_REGISTER_S0_2026-10-17.txt):
  qs_tools_general_overhead_state    THE KEY THE CHAIN READS. 42 fields: named cost fields,
                                     synced_pnl_overhead_items, custom_overhead_items, category and allocation
                                     overrides. Loaded on mount, saved after hydration (is_hydrated guard present).
  qs_tools_saved_overheads           list of saved profiles (about 51 KB with one profile; each holds a snapshot of
                                     output_contract, total and rows). READ IN CODE: loaded into saved_overheads
                                     only - it drives the "profile saved" status, it is NOT a calculation input.
                                     Closes stress open item O-S0-1.

===============================================================================
5. OUTPUTS AND CONSUMERS
===============================================================================
output_contract (hooks/general-overheads/generalOverheadOutputContract.js):
  total_general_overheads            -> Cost Summary (people + assets + overheads), Module Reconciliation,
                                        Business Summary bridge, Quote Engine (through Cost Summary)
  category_totals                    -> summary card
  non_asset_interest_annual          -> not read by anything yet (excludes flagged rows)
  interest_in_overheads_annual       -> Business Outcome EBIT add-back (F-11, Addendum 1): every interest row's active_amount,
                                        flagged-row remainder included
  overhead_rows (allocation rows)    -> Cost Allocation overhead pool, Recovery Summary
  asset_overhead_pools, total_asset_overhead_pool_amount (and the asset_related_* names) -> Assets
        (asset_overhead_pool_assignments), Cost Summary pool transfer
  unallocated_overhead_lines / unallocated_overhead_amount, allocation_pool_warnings, allocation_pool_summaries
  general_overheads_ready            see Section 7 (two different readiness meanings exist)
  synced_pnl_overhead_items, imported_pnl_overhead_rows
status (separate object, hook return): is_ready, warning_count, warnings, grouped_summary, saved_profile_count.
  Module Reconciliation reads status.is_ready, not output_contract.general_overheads_ready.

Ten display categories: office_admin, financial_admin, finance_interest, insurance_compliance, staff_overheads,
vehicles_running, travel, facilities_premises, sales_growth, other_unallocated. A P&L line gets its category from
its review_subcategory first, then from keywords in its name (fuel, rent, insurance, software, legal and so on).

OPEN - DESIGN
GO-1  Two readiness meanings. output_contract.general_overheads_ready = finite total, at least one category total,
      and no asset-finance duplication. status.is_ready = profile name present AND at least one saved profile.
      Module Reconciliation and so Model Readiness use the SECOND, so a business with correct numbers but no saved
      overhead profile gets a BLOCKING "General Overheads is not ready" check. READ IN CODE
      (generalOverheadCardSelector.js, reconciliationRules.js getModuleReadyCheck); not seen live and the default
      profile name is unconfirmed. Check on a fresh browser profile before deciding.
GO-2  Clashing asset_* contract names (tracker A12/A13): asset_related_assigned_to_assets is a hard-coded 0, and
      asset_related_unassigned_cost, asset_related_unassigned_balance and
      total_unassigned_asset_related_overhead_cost_annual all equal the whole pool amount. asset_review_required is
      true whenever any pool amount exists. Pool "unassigned" figures here do not mean what the names say; the real
      assignment lives on the asset records. Consumers must not read them as assignment status.
GO-3  Over-assigned pools: the contract scales over-assigned pools pro rata, the Assets hook uses raw amounts, and
      the editor clamps (smoke test E.4). Latent while pools balance (tracker, Assets A3).
GO-4  [SHIPPED 2026-10-27, commit 0a4daac, Addendum 1] Non-asset interest add-back (R2 F-11): EBIT now adds back interest_in_overheads_annual.
      Owner decision 2026-10-27: all interest, overdraft and bank interest included. See the Business Outcome brief Addendum 6.
OPEN - CHECKS

GO-6  The P&L-sync "interest" test is a substring match on the line name; any line containing "interest" is treated
      as finance interest (same family as Labour F3 / P&L F13 substring matches).
OPEN - NOT BUILT FROM THE v4.0 SPEC (kept as design intent, not defects)
GO-7  No general_overheads_status values (ready_with_warnings, review_required and so on), no
      general_overheads_model_trust_state, no structured warning objects with ids (possible_duplicate_asset_cost,

ADDENDUM 1 (2026-10-27) - F-11: interest_in_overheads_annual [SHIPPED, commit 0a4daac; live-checked]
New output contract field interest_in_overheads_annual (generalOverheadCalculations.js L264-L277, passed through generalOverheadOutputContract.js L42):
the sum of active_amount over every row that is an interest row (is_interest_row: category finance_interest, or "interest" in the label or source line name) OR
a flagged asset-finance row. The flagged row's active_amount is already its P&L amount less the Assets interest, so the remainder counts and the asset part does
not (no double count with the asset interest EBIT already adds back). non_asset_interest_annual is untouched. Consumer: Business Outcome EBIT (Business Outcome
brief Addendum 6). Verified: Scenario A plus 18,000 "Overdraft Interest" gives pool 179,710.69 and interest_in_overheads 18,000 (page EBIT 179,662).
GO-6 now has a second consequence: a line with "interest" in its name that is not finance interest would be added back in EBIT too.
