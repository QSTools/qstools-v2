===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Revenue / COGS answers: "what does the business earn and what does it directly cost to earn it, before any people,
asset or overhead cost?" The answer is the MARGIN POOL (revenue - direct costs), the money that has to pay for
Labour, Assets and General Overheads and leave a profit.

It also carries the business's COMMERCIAL DRIVER MODE: hours-based (revenue earned per person-hour sold) or
unit-based (revenue earned per unit sold), and the unit rows for product businesses.

It holds NO data of its own for revenue or costs. It reads the P&L live, so P&L and Revenue/COGS agree by
construction (tracker s7).

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: margin_pool, gross_margin_percent, the revenue and direct-cost display lines (from P&L), the commercial
  driver mode in force, the unit driver rows (label, unit type, revenue share, average sale rate, estimated units),
  derived units and margin per unit, units_sold_annual (legacy field), the module's warnings and ready flag,
  business_type passthrough (from Business Setup).
DOES NOT OWN: the revenue and cost-of-sales numbers (P&L), the direct-cost category assignment (P&L:
  direct_cost_category_id on each line, 9 categories), labour, assets, overheads, revenue per source (Business
  Outcome), the recovery test (Recovery Summary), the quote (Quote Engine).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  margin_pool = total_revenue - total_direct_costs. gross_margin_percent = margin_pool / total_revenue as a
    FRACTION (0.35, not 35), 0 when revenue is 0. Display multiplies by 100.
R2  total_revenue and total_direct_costs are read from the P&L CATEGORY-based pair (total_revenue,
    total_direct_costs). The P&L also computes a section-based pair. They are equal only when every cost-of-sales
    line has an active direct-cost group and every revenue line has category revenue; nothing enforces that at
    runtime (tracker s10). The dev page /dev/cross-import-check tests it.
R3  Mode comes from Business Setup (business_type, default labour_based), never from Revenue/COGS itself:
    labour_based -> hours_based always; product_based -> unit_based, or mixed_unit_based if the stored mode says
    mixed. The stored mode only distinguishes single from mixed inside product mode (tracker s9).
R4  Hours mode: no unit rows are calculated; units_sold_annual and margin_per_unit stay as the legacy typed value
    and margin_pool / units_sold_annual; product_recovery_status = not_selected.
R5  Unit mode: a single unit row is forced to 100 percent revenue share. A row of unit_type "each" is a revenue
    bucket and uses the typed estimated_units_annual; every other type (m2, m3, lm, tonne, hour, custom) derives
    units = row revenue / average_sale_rate_per_unit (0 when the rate is 0). annual_revenue and annual_margin_pool
    = total x share / 100; margin_per_unit = annual margin pool / derived units.
R6  revenue_cogs_ready is true only when there are NO warnings at all (see Section 7). A negative margin pool is a
    warning, so it makes the module not ready. Business Outcome is gated on revenue_cogs_ready (Business Outcome
    brief R10).
R7  No sale value is assumed for COGS lines and nothing is allocated here: the module shows the margin pool, it does
    not spread it. Direct costs never enter General Overheads (General Overheads brief R2).
R8  Every hop that carries a field is an explicit list (bug class b). A new Revenue/COGS field must be added at the
    calculation return, the hook output_contract, the status and the card.

4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
Reads: P&L output_contract (total_revenue, total_direct_costs, revenue_lines, cost_of_sales_lines,
direct_cost_categories, direct_cost_category_totals) through useProfitAndLoss; Business Setup output_contract
(business_type).

Storage: qs_tools_revenue_cogs_state_v2  {commercial_driver_mode, units_sold_annual, unit_driver_rows[], ...}
  (lib/storage/revenueCogsStorage.js). Loaded on mount, saved after storage_loaded (guard present). Shape and seed
  role: STORAGE_KEY_REGISTER_S0_2026-10-17.txt. Unit types allowed: each, m2, m3, lm, tonne, hour, custom.

Xero COG import panel (components/revenue-cogs/RevenueCogXeroImportPanel.jsx, lib/imports/xeroCogImporter): reads
an Xero account-transactions workbook, builds a supplier / account summary and an account reconciliation of the
Xero net total against the P&L cost-of-sales total. READ IN CODE: the result is held in component state only. It
is not stored and it feeds no calculation, so closing the page loses it.

Four mode vocabularies exist and all derive one way from business_type: business_type (labour_based / product_based),
  commercial_driver_mode (hours_based / unit_based / mixed_unit_based), recovery_mode (hours_based / product_unit),
  activity_driver_type (hours / units). Do not add a fifth.

8. FINDINGS REGISTER (keep numbers; update status in place)
===============================================================================
OPEN - DESIGN
RC-1  A negative margin pool blocks readiness. Revenue/COGS counts negative_margin_pool as a warning, so
      revenue_cogs_ready is false for a business that is simply losing money at gross margin, and Business Outcome
      (gated on that flag) goes "not ready" rather than showing the loss. Decide: keep as a trust signal or
      separate "data missing" from "result is bad". READ IN CODE (revenueCogsSelectors.js); not seen live.
RC-2  Section pair vs category pair can diverge silently (R2). Nothing in the app warns on the main path; only the
      dev page checks.
RC-3  Product / unit mode has never been numerically verified. The R2 harness and the stress databases are hours
      mode. Units Mode future build brief holds the plan.
RC-4  Accountant-journal lines (for example opening work in progress) are ordinary direct costs with no review flag
      (P&L P2.2); downstream treatment is an open question here and in Business Outcome.
OPEN - CHECKS
RC-5  Xero COG import result is session-only (Section 4). Confirm that is intended before anyone relies on the
      reconciliation panel across sessions.
RC-6  Revenue Summary was retired as a manual-entry page (S19, 2026-08-06): removed from the sidebar (confirmed
      2026-10-21, SidebarNavigation.jsx has no /revenue-summary item). Its route, hook, calculations, selectors and
      storage files are still in the repo, unrouted. Decide whether to delete the dead code.
OPEN - NOT BUILT FROM THE v4.0 SPEC (kept as design intent, not defects)
RC-7  No revenue_cogs status values or trust state, no structured warning objects, no COG category / subcategory
      engine in this module (assignment lives on P&L lines), no supplier normalisation or supplier spend summary,
      no COG leak rule, no payable invoice detail drill. The supplier / product alias and reconciliation matching
      idea is carried by the future-build brief of 2026-10-21 (SUPPLIER_PRODUCT_ALIAS_AND_RECONCILIATION_MATCHING).
CLOSED
Business-type default drift (labour_based everywhere) logged, design decision, not patched (tracker s8).
