===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Business Outcome answers "is the business actually working, and who is carrying whom?" It compares MODELLED revenue
per source (each labour type, each asset, materials) against TRUE COST per source, and reconciles the total to REAL
P&L revenue. It is where final figures are presented (Cost Allocation only assigns). Its per_source object is the
hand-off Business Modelling reads.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: per-source modelled revenue and true cost; covered hours (BO-6 cap); non-productive cost distribution to
working units; View A and View B cascades; Best Case (each part on its own) vs How the Business Runs; capacity
coverage gap (shortfall revenue not earned, tied-unit idle labour); health gauge; EBIT / EBITDA display; Labour
Recovery card overhead-per-hour; per_source.
DOES NOT OWN (consumes): Cost Allocation group rows and recovery rows; Labour staff-type rates; Rate Builder STORED
inputs (charge-out rates per staff type, calculators, materials markup); Revenue / COGS totals; Opening Hours;
Balance Sheet; Assets interest / depreciation (EBIT display only).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Labour sources = one row per (group, staff type). Cost = Cost Allocation assigned cost. Revenue = saved charge-out
    rate x revenue hours. A staff type with no saved rate shows "No rate saved" / not_ready - never $0.
R2  COVERED HOURS (BO-6): an asset cannot earn for hours nobody is there to run it. Asset-driven group covered hours =
    MIN(asset seat hours, assigned labour hours). A group with no labour earns nothing and is flagged. Applies to
    Best Case and How the Business Runs.
R3  Asset revenue: per-asset calculators (Path B) price the MACHINE ONLY: asset rate x own hours x
    min(1, covered / required_asset_hours); the operator is charged once on the labour row. Legacy group calculators
    keep the all-in rate with the labour mix subtracted (CM-1 / CM-1b). An asset with no lines shows "Not priced".
R4  Overhead inside a group is split labour / asset by the SHARED split_group_overhead (one definition, also used by
    Rate Builder), then by hours.
R5  Non-productive cost: pure-support units always spread across working units by RECOVERED HOURS; mixed units follow
    the stored policy (default charge_to_self). Injected into true_cost and stamped as row.non_productive_share so
    every display adds up.
R6  View A: materials revenue = residual (real revenue less labour and asset modelled revenue); a shortfall is absorbed
    first by profitable groups by margin, then by revenue share. The materials ROW is floored at 0 (owner F-5:
    "materials can't go negative"); the cascade uses the UNFLOORED residual.
    View B: materials is a peer priced at the Rate Builder markup; one global ceiling; surplus revenue shown as
    unexplained_revenue (BO-11). Both headlines = real revenue - total true cost - unassigned.
R7  BEST CASE = each part on its own: each unit carries only its own costs (including its own non-productive cost);
    pure-support units appear once as their own rows. Nothing hidden, every line traceable (owner 2026-10-10).
R8  Idle labour is ALREADY inside net profit (revenue capped at covered hours, labour cost in full). Idle displays
    are explanation only - never added to or taken from a total. Valued at raw labour cost per assigned hour
    (Cost Allocation idle_labour_cost, D-IDLE-1). Shown only for units ticked "labour tied to the asset" (D-IDLE-2/3).
R9  Shortfall (labour short of asset hours) is "Revenue not earned" - a different measure, valued as revenue; never
    combined with idle. For per-asset-priced groups it is built from the same helpers as revenue; incomplete pricing
    shows "at least $X" plus what is missing (O-2).
R10 Readiness: gated on revenue_cogs_ready, cost_summary_ready, business_summary_ready - NOT on Model Readiness.
R11 Per day / per week use Opening Hours open days / weeks (S-H2 closed).
R12 Hours-native; business_type not read.

4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
No storage of its own except the non-productive policy key (default charge_to_self). Reads Rate Builder stores
(qstools_rate_builder_labour_source_rates_v1 - read once on mount, a new rate shows after navigating back;
qs_tools_rate_builder_calculators_v1; materials markup store), Cost Allocation contract, Labour contract, Revenue /
COGS, Opening Hours, Balance Sheet, Assets (EBIT).
