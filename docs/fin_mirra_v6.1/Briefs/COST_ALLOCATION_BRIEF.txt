===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Cost Allocation is where the business is organised into WORKING UNITS (operational groups inside divisions) and
every cost dollar is assigned to one: productive labour by staff type, productive assets, non-productive labour and
assets, and overhead. Its output - per-unit cost, recovery hours and minimum recoverable rate - is what Rate Builder
prices against and Business Outcome judges. It is the hinge between "what the business costs" and "what it earns".

Owner framing (D2, 2026-10-07): Cost Allocation is for ASSIGNING costs. Final figures belong in Business Outcome;
group totals and rates sit behind "Show checks". Pool progress (available / assigned / remaining) stays visible.
Governing principle (owner): the tool exists to reveal gaps. A gap that is calculated but not shown breaks that.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: divisions and working units; labour assignments (staff type x percent) and asset assignments; the five pools
(productive labour, non-productive labour, productive assets, non-productive assets, overhead); per-group cost rows;
group recovery hours and their basis; per-stream and minimum recoverable rates; labour coverage (shortfall) and
idle labour (surplus, tied units) measures; asset run pattern; allocation status and warnings.

DOES NOT OWN: labour cost or staff types (Labour); asset cost or hours (Assets); the overhead pool amount (comes
from Recovery Summary overhead_absorbed_cost); charge-out prices (Rate Builder); revenue and verdicts (Business
Outcome). Spreading non-productive cost across units for the outcome view is Business Outcome's step.

STRUCTURE NOTE (code truth vs v4 spec): Recovery Summary is UPSTREAM of Cost Allocation (overhead pool, status,
hours fallback). Recovery-rate logic the v4 spec said to remove is still here and load-bearing (C7).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Every source dollar is assigned exactly once: labour + productive assets + non-productive assets + overhead =
    grouped operating cost = Cost Summary total = Recovery Summary cost burden, with zero unassigned (proven live).
R2  Labour assignment cost = staff type total cost x assignment percent (assigned_staff_count is ignored - C13).
R3  Overhead is shared by RECOVERED hours everywhere (owner decision 2026-09-29): weight = group recovery hours /
    total. Labour cost, asset burden and equal split remain only as fallbacks when no group has recovered hours.
    A group with no recovered hours gets no overhead. (Business Outcome spreads pooled non-productive cost by the
    same rule.)
R4  Group recovery hours: stored basis if set, else asset hours if the group has a productive asset, else labour
    hours (or manual). Asset hours = the MAX of the group's assets (run together) - required_asset_hours is the SUM
    for "one after another" groups and is used for coverage measures.
R5  Labour cap (owner 2026-10-07): an asset-driven group with labour assigned recovers MIN(required_asset_hours,
    assigned labour hours). A labour shortfall is a shortfall whatever the run pattern. Cost per hour rises; total
    cost unchanged.
R6  Labour shortfall (labour hours < asset hours) shows a coverage notice in the builder pointing back to Assets.
    Surplus labour is NOT a warning unless the unit is ticked "labour tied to the asset only" (D4); then idle hours
    and idle cost are shown. Idle cost is already inside group cost per hour - it never moves a rate or a total.
R7  Over-allocation (>100% of a type or asset) is detected and BLOCKS status but is not capped (C14).
R8  Non-productive cost is in total_group_cost and group_cost_per_hour but in NO stream rate and NOT in
    minimum_recoverable_rate_per_hour (C6).
R9  NAMING TRAPS: productive_asset_cost here = own + pools (in the Assets contract = own only, C2). Top-level
    assignment lists are RAW stored records - read the enriched lists on operational_group_cost_rows. Fields like
    labour_share_percent, required_recovery_rate, recovery_hours_used are Recovery Summary passthroughs.
R10 Every asset must be assigned to a working unit (owner, F-4). An unassigned PRODUCTIVE asset (remaining cost over $1)
    BLOCKS: allocation_status = blocked, red banner, no spread. Unassigned SUPPORT-asset cost is spread EVENLY across all
    active working units (default, so totals are complete) with a red banner and warning. Spread rows are automatic
    non-productive asset assignments (asset_id unassigned_support_asset_spread).
R11 Moving a person between units never edits a record: the old assignment goes is_active false and a new one is
    created.
