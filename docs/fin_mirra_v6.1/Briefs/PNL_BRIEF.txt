===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
The P&L is the accounting source and the BENCHMARK the operational modules are reconciled against. It holds the
imported (or entered) profit and loss lines, classifies each line, and produces the revenue, cost-of-sales and
cost-bucket totals the rest of the tool starts from. It is money-only and hours / units neutral.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: P&L lines and their classification (section, category, direct-cost group, review subcategory, interest
treatment); section totals and net profit; category totals (revenue, direct costs, labour / assets / general
overheads benchmarks, unassigned); direct-cost groups (one per imported cost-of-sales account - the code owns them
here and Revenue / COGS depends on it); the asset-finance interest flag; P&L readiness.

DOES NOT OWN: operational labour cost (Labour - P&L labour is only the benchmark); asset cost (Assets); the
General Overheads cost model (General Overheads reads P&L lines and builds pools); reconciliation verdicts
(Module Reconciliation); revenue modelling (Business Outcome / Modelling).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Three groupings from the same lines:
    A. SECTION pair: trading income, cost of sales, other income, operating expenses, gross profit, net profit
       (net = gross + other income - operating expenses). Live sections are trading_income, cost_of_sales,
       other_income, operating_expenses. There is no other_expenses section.
    B. CATEGORY pair: total_revenue (category revenue, any section); total_direct_costs (= total_cogs, summed by
       ACTIVE direct-cost groups); labour / assets / general_overheads benchmarks (operating expenses by category);
       unassigned_balance; total_business_costs = labour + assets + general overheads + unassigned.
    C. INTEREST totals: detected by "interest" in the line name.
R2  Downstream reads the CATEGORY pair (via Revenue / COGS). The section pair reaches only the P&L page, Model
    Readiness audit panel and the cross-import check. Keep the category pair authoritative; divergence must become
    loud (F1, D2).
R3  pnl_ready = no review lines (unassigned, review_required, or cogs with a missing / review direct-cost group).
    A completely blank row (no name, zero amount, unassigned, no account code) is ignored (X2). Any name or amount
    makes it count.
R4  Asset finance interest: ticking a P&L interest line moves Assets' calculated interest out of General Overheads;
    the remainder (e.g. overdraft interest) stays in overheads. A line NAMED "asset finance" is auto-categorised
    assets / asset_finance and never reaches the cost model - test lines must use a neutral name.
R5  Amounts may be stored as text; the calculation coerces them (verified by R2 H5).
R6  P&L never reads business_type.

===============================================================================
4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
Import: Excel or PDF (app/api/pnl-import/route.js spawns Python in Py/API_for_PnL via
process.getBuiltinModule("child_process") - needs Node 20.16+ / 22.3+). Lines can also be edited by hand.
Line fields that matter: pnl_line_id, line_name, amount, section, category, direct_cost_category_id,
review_subcategory, interest_treatment.
Storage: qs_tools_profit_and_loss_state (has_hydrated guard). "Saved P&L Profiles" are period snapshots, not an
account-mapping store.

===============================================================================
5. OUTPUT CONTRACT AND CONSUMERS
===============================================================================
Hook hooks/useProfitAndLoss.js returns status, card, output_contract. Core verified fields:
  total_trading_income, total_cost_of_sales, total_other_income, total_operating_expenses, gross_profit,
  net_profit, total_revenue, total_direct_costs, direct_cost_category_totals, direct_cost_categories,
  revenue_lines, cost_of_sales_lines, labour_benchmark_total, general_overheads_benchmark_total,
  assets_benchmark_total, unassigned_balance, total_business_costs, pnl_ready,
  excluded_asset_finance_interest_total.
Aliases: total_cogs = total_direct_costs; excluded_asset_finance_interest_total =
  pnl_interest_marked_asset_finance_total; total_assigned_business_costs.
Display only, no readers: materials_cost, subcontract_cost, hired_equipment_cost. DEAD:
  employee_overheads_benchmark_total (hard-coded 0).
Struck v4.0 aspirations (not built): pnl_status, pnl_model_trust_state, period fields, structured warnings,
  imported-vs-calculated net profit variance, account-mapping store, pnl_* prefix (D1).

Consumers:
  Revenue / COGS        total_revenue, total_direct_costs, lines, direct-cost groups and totals
                        -> Business Summary, Recovery, Business Outcome, Business Modelling
  Module Reconciliation total_business_costs (P&L side), unassigned_balance, pnl_ready,
                        excluded_asset_finance_interest_total, labour lines (wages vs on-costs by name - F13)
  Business Summary,     total_business_costs
  Model Readiness
  General Overheads     P&L lines via hooks/general-overheads/generalOverheadPnlSync.js
