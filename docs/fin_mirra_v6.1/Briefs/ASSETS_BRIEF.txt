1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Assets is a SOURCE module. It holds the business's plant, vehicles and equipment and answers:
  - What does each asset cost the business a year (its own cost plus running costs assigned to it)?
  - How many hours a year is each productive asset available to earn?
  - What hourly rate must each asset, and the productive fleet, recover?
It does not pair assets with operators, group assets into working units, or price work.

IMPORTANT: the v4.0 spec describes a much richer module than the code. Where they differ, Sections 2-5 of this
brief describe the CODE (verified 2026-09-23); the v4.0 gaps are listed in Section 8 as findings.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: asset identity; productive vs support classification; own annual cost (today: finance interest only - A1);
finance schedule (principal and payment shown as cash-flow support, never as cost); utilisation hours; the
assignment of General Overheads running-cost pools to individual assets; per-asset and blended productive recovery
rates; pool guardrail status; asset warnings.

DOES NOT OWN: the running-cost pool amounts themselves (General Overheads builds them from the P&L); operator
pairing and working units (Cost Allocation); labour; revenue; Business Outcome verdicts; depreciation policy in the
P&L (General Overheads / P&L).

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Asset types are "productive" or "support" (non_productive maps to support; anything unrecognised maps to
    productive). Support assets have utilisation and rate forced to 0.
R2  Active = not retired (is_retired). is_active is stored but no filter reads it (A15).
R3  Own cost = finance interest while finance is active: purchase price x remaining fraction of term (to TODAY)
    x interest rate. Principal and payment are NOT cost.
R4  Running costs reach assets ONLY through the six General Overheads pools (fuel, insurance, repairs and
    maintenance, registration and compliance, consumables, finance interest). Cost Summary adds the assigned
    amount to Assets and removes it from General Overheads - a transfer, total unchanged.
R5  Interest double count is prevented: the finance-interest pool is built from the NET P&L interest after Assets'
    own calculated interest is removed (the "asset finance" tick on the P&L interest row).
R6  Annual weeks chain: asset override -> Opening Hours annual_open_weeks -> 48. (Stored rows always use 48 or the
    override - see A5.)
R7  NAMING TRAP (A11): productive_asset_cost and support_asset_cost are OWN cost and EXCLUDE pools. Consumers that
    need the full burden add *_assigned_overhead_cost_annual or read *_recovery_cost_annual. Do NOT change those
    fields to include pools - Business Outcome adds the overhead itself and would double count.
R8  Assets is hours-native and never reads business_type. Asset utilisation is independent of labour hours.
R9  Every asset should be assigned to a working group in Cost Allocation (owner decision F-4, 2026-10-18).

===============================================================================
4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
Per asset: name, type, purchase price, finance (rate, term, start, paid-off), scheduled hours per week, downtime,
annual weeks override, pool assignments {pool_key: amount}, register link, include_depreciation_in_cost (opt-in),
is_retired.
Storage (lib/storage/assetStorage.js):
  qs_tools_asset_state                    editor draft (one asset)
  qs_tools_saved_assets                   saved list - what everything downstream reads
  qs_tools_fixed_asset_register_state_v1  Fixed Asset Register import (separate)
Save effects are guarded by has_hydrated. sanitizeSavedAssetRecord re-runs calculateAssetOutputs on every read,
so interest refreshes on each page load.

===============================================================================
5. OUTPUT CONTRACT AND CONSUMERS
===============================================================================
Builder: hooks/assets/assetOutputContract.js (buildAssetsOutputContract). Key fields:
  assets / active_assets (per-asset rows with EFFECTIVE pool assignments, recovery cost, rate),
  assets_ready, no_active_assets_confirmed, asset_pool_guardrail_status,
  productive / support asset counts,
  total_asset_cost_annual (own, all live), productive_asset_cost (own), support_asset_cost (own),
  productive / support *_assigned_overhead_cost_annual, productive_asset_recovery_cost_annual,
  total_asset_recovery_cost_annual (= Cost Summary's Assets line),
  total_allocated_asset_overhead_cost_annual,
  total_productive_asset_utilisation_hours_annual, productive_asset_recovery_rate (blended, own + pools),
  asset_overhead_pools and assignment summary, asset_review_required, asset_overhead_assignment_warnings,
  total_asset_interest_annual, finance_cost_annual, cash_flow_support.
Not inside the contract (returned by useAssets alongside it): status, card, a second active_assets list built from
RAW assignments (A3), business_default_annual_weeks, asset_finance_pnl_match.

Consumers:
  Cost Summary       Assets line = total_asset_recovery_cost_annual; subtracts allocated pools from GO
  Recovery Summary   own + assigned overhead (full burden since the A11 fix)
  Business Summary   asset groups sum asset_recovery_cost_annual (A11 half fixed e517b39)
  Cost Allocation    active_assets rows; rebuilds productive cost WITH pools internally
  Business Outcome   own + assigned overhead (correct)
  Reconciliation /   total_asset_cost_annual, active_assets, no_active_assets_confirmed
  Model Readiness    (Assets blocks readiness until assets exist or "no active assets" is confirmed)
  General Overheads  reads saved assets directly for the interest subtraction (A13)
Nothing downstream reads asset_pool_guardrail_status yet.

ADDENDUM 2 (2026-10-28) - CASH AFTER PRINCIPAL REPAYMENTS: REQUIREMENT LOGGED [DECISION: design only, nothing built, no code touched]
Source: owner voice session 2026-10-28. A1 and R3 STAND: asset own cost stays finance interest only, because that is what keeps the tool tied to the P&L. Principal still is
not cost and still never enters recovery cost, rates or Cost Summary. This addendum adds a separate cash figure; it changes no existing calculation.
 1. [DECISION] The gap (owner): the model answers "what must I charge to cover cost" but not "where is my money going". Five or six financed assets can mean three or four
    thousand a month of principal that appears nowhere in the P&L, so a business can price at breakeven and run short of cash. Example from the owner: net profit 5,000, buy a
    vehicle, the model says it is affordable, and at year end the cash left is zero because the year's principal was 5,000. A large purchase just before year end is the common case.
 2. [DECISION] The figure: CASH AFTER PRINCIPAL REPAYMENTS = net profit minus the year's principal repaid across EVERY active asset with finance, whether or not the asset sits in
    a working unit (the bank takes it either way). Name chosen by the owner; no accounting jargon in the label. It is shown in Business Outcome beside net profit (Business
    Outcome Addendum 9) and in every scenario result (AI_LAYER Addendum 3 item 10).
 3. [DECISION] Principal per asset is already worked out here: finance payment minus interest, cash-flow support only (CALCULATION_MAP, Assets, about line 319 of the Assets calc
    file; VARIABLE_TRACKER). The new figure uses fields the asset already carries (purchase price, interest rate, term, start date). Boundary (owner): tax is never calculated;
    the tool stops at net profit.
 4. [DECISION] Protocols apply because this is a new number touching Assets and Business Outcome: its own stop-and-confirm, trace node that matches the screen, calculation map
    and variable tracker updated, a stress-test seed case, a dated addendum, git status before any commit. Filed here as an addendum, not as a new brief.
 5. [OPEN] To check in code, not decide: (a) whether a trace node for principal or for this figure already exists (the trace brief lists six Assets nodes and the excerpts seen
    mention neither; unconfirmed); (b) how the module stores principal; (c) the interest used is a straight-line estimate dated to TODAY, so principal derived from it drifts a
    little each day, which needs the same check as interest; (d) whether the sum across assets belongs in Assets or in Business Outcome; (e) retired and paid-off assets are
    excluded (assumed from "active asset with finance"; confirm).
 6. [OPEN] Stress-test seed: add a case with a large vehicle purchase (and the 100-asset case) with a known answer, so the figure is checked against expected numbers.

Real storage and the baseline never change. components\scenarios\ScenarioRemovalPanel.jsx on /assets gives Remove / Undo.
