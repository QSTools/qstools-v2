1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
These modules turn the source modules (Labour, Assets, General Overheads) into the business's total cost burden
and answer two DIFFERENT per-hour questions, which are kept apart (decided 2026-09-23):

  MACRO VIEW - Cost Summary, Business Summary
    "What does the business cost to keep open?" Per open hour / day / week. Denominators come from Opening Hours.
  PRICING VIEW - Recovery Summary (then Cost Allocation, Rate Builder; Quote Engine; legacy Business Modelling)
    "What must each productive person-hour recover?" Denominator = the whole productive team's hours.

Chain: Opening Hours -> Cost Summary -> Business Summary -> Recovery Summary -> Cost Allocation.
Labour / Assets / General Overheads -> Cost Summary (through the Model Readiness gate).

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OPENING HOURS owns: open days, hours per day, open weeks, public holidays, shutdowns -> net annual business open
  hours, net_annual_business_open_days, annual_open_weeks. (Also the default annual weeks for Assets.)
COST SUMMARY owns: total cost burden (people + assets + overheads, with the asset-pool transfer applied), per open
  hour / day / week / month / quarter / year, business_recovery_hours, required_recovery_rate,
  model_readiness_status passthrough.
BUSINESS SUMMARY owns: revenue vs cost burden, margin after labour, the cost hierarchy tree, the P&L bridge
  (proof that module totals reconcile to P&L net profit), model_trust_state.
RECOVERY SUMMARY owns: recovery streams (labour, asset, overhead, margin), required labour and asset recovery
  rates, stream shares, recovery readiness.
None of them owns source costs - they consume the Labour, Assets, General Overheads and P&L contracts.

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Macro views use the opening calendar only. Pricing views use productive person-hours only. Never mix them on
    one figure.
R2  business_recovery_hours = total_productive_output (whole productive team), NOT one person's average (F18).
R3  Per day = per hour x normal open day (day uses net open days from Opening Hours; shared scale helpers fall back
    to 260 / 52 only when days / weeks are not passed).
R4  Asset totals use asset_recovery_cost_annual (own + pools); productive + support = Assets total = Cost Summary
    Assets line (A11).
R5  Recovery Summary uses productive_asset_cost + productive_asset_assigned_overhead_cost_annual (full burden);
    the published productive_asset_cost stays OWN cost (Business Outcome adds pools itself).
R6  Support asset cost is in NO Recovery Summary stream - by design: Cost Allocation distributes support assets
    through its own non-productive asset pool; adding it to the overhead stream would double count (R3 reclass).
R7  The P&L bridge must always close: signed rows plus an "other classification differences" line, so a gap is
    surfaced, never hidden.
R8  Trust gate: any blocking Module Reconciliation check -> Model Readiness "blocked" -> Cost Summary status ->
    Business Summary model_trust_state -> Recovery Summary "not_trusted" -> Cost Allocation "not_supported".
    Warnings do not block. The gate is correct behaviour when test data does not match the P&L.
R9  Product mode (units): Recovery Summary zeroes every stream except overhead; Business Summary per day uses /365
    (parked units gap, S-D2). See Units Mode future build.

===============================================================================
5. OUTPUTS AND CONSUMERS
===============================================================================
Opening Hours  -> net annual open hours / days, annual_open_weeks -> Cost Summary, Business Summary, Assets weeks,
                  Business Outcome (scale helpers, since 8b)
Cost Summary   -> total cost burden, per-period figures, business_recovery_hours, required_recovery_rate,
                  required_labour_burden_rate (now numerically = required_recovery_rate, S-C3),
                  model_readiness_status -> Business Summary, Quote Engine, legacy Business Modelling baseline
Business Summary -> *_per_driver, *_per_recovery_hour, activity_driver_value (CALCULATION fields kept unchanged
                  because Recovery Summary reads them), model_trust_state -> Recovery Summary
Recovery Summary -> overhead_absorbed_cost (Cost Allocation's overhead pool), recovery hours, required rates,
                  stream shares (only consumer: Rate Builder all_productive row C24), asset_recovery_status,
                  recovery_summary_ready -> Cost Allocation
