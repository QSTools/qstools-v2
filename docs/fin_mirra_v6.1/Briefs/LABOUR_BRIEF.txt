1. PURPOSE
===============================================================================
Labour is a SOURCE module. It defines the people-cost base and productive capacity. It answers:
  - Who works in the business, and are they productive or non-productive?
  - What do they cost (wages plus employer obligations)?
  - How many productive hours do they create?
  - What is the weighted productive labour rate - overall and per staff type?
It does not decide recovery, allocate labour to groups, price jobs or judge the outcome.

===============================================================================
2. OWNS / DOES NOT OWN
===============================================================================
OWNS: staff identity; staff type identity; productive / non-productive classification; employment
cost (wages, salaries, owner wage where treated as labour); employer obligations (KiwiSaver, ESCT,
ACC); productive and non-productive hours (leave and statutory rest breaks); productive and
non-productive labour cost; weighted all-productive rate; weighted per-staff-type rates; labour
readiness, warnings, trust state.

DOES NOT OWN: subcontractor COGS, materials, revenue, COGS (Revenue / COGS); asset cost, utilisation,
asset/operator pairing (Assets, Cost Allocation); operating groups and overhead distribution (Cost
Allocation); staff-related costs that are not employment cost - training, PPE, phones (General
Overheads / Employee Overheads); recovery tests (Recovery Summary); rates (Rate Builder); verdicts
(Business Outcome).

FLOW POSITION: Business Setup -> P&L -> Revenue / COGS -> LABOUR -> Assets -> General Overheads ->
Module Reconciliation -> Model Readiness -> Cost Summary -> Business Summary -> Cost Allocation ->
Recovery Summary -> Rate Builder -> Business Outcome -> Business Modelling.

===============================================================================
3. RULES (do not break)
===============================================================================
R1  Labour is real cost and must be paid. Do not assume it is recovered because revenue exists.
R2  Labour cost comes from Labour module data only. P&L labour is a BENCHMARK for reconciliation,
    never the source. Charge-out rate and target rate are never labour cost.
R3  Stable IDs: staff_id and staff_type_id are stable; names are display only. Never infer staff
    type or productivity from a name.
R4  Staff types are user-controlled (create / rename). Starter examples allowed; no construction-only
    hard-coding. Missing staff type on a productive person -> review bucket, not a guess.
R5  Productive vs non-productive is explicit. Non-productive staff stay in Labour and keep their cost
    (it reaches Cost Summary and Business Outcome's true cost) but contribute NO productive hours
    and never enter productive rate outputs.
R6  Rates are WEIGHTED: type cost / type productive hours. Never a simple average of personal rates.
R7  Guardrails: no division by zero productive hours; zero cost with productive hours is flagged;
    unassigned productive staff are visible.
R8  Leave is paid INSIDE base cost (rate x paid hours); entitlements are a display split, not extra
    cost. Statutory paid rest breaks reduce productive hours, not cost (F19).
R9  Labour is hours-native and does not read business_type (Units Mode is downstream).
R10 Downstream modules consume the contract; none may recalculate Labour totals or rates.

4. INPUTS AND STORAGE
===============================================================================
Per staff profile (hand-entered): name, staff type, labour_class (productive / support /
non-productive), hours per week, days per week, labour_rate, charge_out_rate (optional),
productivity %, margin target %, leave (annual weeks; public holiday, sick, bereavement,
family-violence days), KiwiSaver on/off and rate, ACC rate, is_active.
Storage: lib/storage/labourProfileStorage.js; staff types key qs_tools_labour_staff_types_v1
(ids via generateStaffTypeId). Full key list: STORAGE_KEY_REGISTER_S0_2026-10-17.txt.
Future idea only (not designed): import staff from a payroll report (owner named Smartly), no tax
detail. Conventions rule 10 applies before building.
