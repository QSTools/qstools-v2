FOUND BY: R2 stress test, Scenario A (STRESS_TEST_DATABASE_AND_AGENT_DESIGN brief, Addendum 2, F-1).

1. WHAT THE APP DOES TODAY
   lib/calculations/labourCalculations.js calculateLabourOutputs (calc map s3):
     employer_kiwisaver_gross = gross_wages x 0.035
     esct_amount              = employer_kiwisaver_gross x ESCT rate (band on wages + contribution)
     total_employer_contribution_cost = employer_kiwisaver_gross + esct_amount + acc_work_levy
   So ESCT is added ON TOP of the 3.5 percent employer contribution.

2. WHAT IRD SAYS
   ESCT "is deducted from your employer contributions"; each pay period the employer works out "the amount of
   ESCT to deduct and your net employer contribution". ESCT goes to IRD, the net goes to the fund. The employer's
   cash cost is the gross contribution (3.5 percent), not 3.5 percent plus ESCT.
     https://www.ird.govt.nz/esct
     https://www.ird.govt.nz/employing-staff/deductions-from-income/employer-superannuation-contribution-tax/deduct-esct-from-each-employer-contribution
   Exception: an employer may agree to pay ESCT on top so the fund receives the full contribution (an employment
   agreement choice, not the IRD default).

3. SIZE (Scenario A, as of 2026-10-17)
   ESCT 8,089.54 on employer KiwiSaver 26,921.44; total labour cost 812,167.28 -> overstated by about 1.0 percent.
   Flows into every labour cost rate, Rate Builder minimum rates, Cost Summary burden and the recovery rate.

4. PROPOSED CHANGE (owner to confirm at build time)
   - Default: total_employer_contribution_cost = employer_kiwisaver_gross + acc_work_levy. ESCT still calculated
     and SHOWN as the split of the contribution (to IRD versus to the fund), not added to cost.
   - Option: "Employer pays ESCT on top" (per business, possibly per staff member). When on, ESCT is added as today.
   - P&L reconciliation: the KiwiSaver P&L line normally equals the gross contribution; check the labour
     benchmark reconciliation against both settings.

5. HOW TO BUILD AND VERIFY
   - Core calculation file: stop-and-confirm before patching (conventions rule 1). Own brief = this file.
   - Trace node for any new figure (definition of done, 2026-10-16).
   - Verify with the R2 harness: Py/stress_test, set kiwisaver_line_includes_esct to false in the recipe, extend
     mirra_expected.labour_person with the option, re-run Scenario A; every downstream figure shows the change.
   - Update CALCULATION_MAP s3 and the Labour brief in the same commit.

6. PLACEMENT
   Not part of R2. Suggested: after R2 layer 2, before R3 (Business Modelling redesign), so the modelling work
   starts on the corrected labour cost. Owner to confirm.
