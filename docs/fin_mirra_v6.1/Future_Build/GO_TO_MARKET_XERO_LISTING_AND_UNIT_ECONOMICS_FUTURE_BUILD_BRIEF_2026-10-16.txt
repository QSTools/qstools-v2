3. CHANNEL PLAN (zero cash) - research and reasoning
===============================================================================
Order of effort, highest leverage first:
  1. Bookkeeper relationships. A trusted referral skips the discovery
     problem entirely: nobody searches for a category they do not know
     exists ("true cost per job", "labour recovery rate"). Demo before the
     build is finished, framed as "here is the problem I am solving, I would
     like your take". Prospects named in the discussion: accounting and
     bookkeeping bodies and groups in NZ and AU (CPA Australia, Chartered
     Accountants ANZ, Institute of Certified Bookkeepers). Not researched.
  2. Xero App Store listing (Section 4). MYOB and QuickBooks have their own
     marketplaces; their listing requirements were NOT researched.
  3. Problem-framed content that ranks for the problem, not the product name
     ("why is my business busy but not profitable", "true cost per job").
     One body of content serves Google and the AI assistants (ChatGPT,
     Copilot) that answer from indexed web content.
  4. LinkedIn, posted from the owner's PERSONAL profile, not a company page.
     One 2026 summary puts organic company-page posts at about 2 percent of
     feed visibility. The algorithm tests a post on a small slice of the
     first-degree network and reads comments, likes, shares, saves and dwell
     time in the first 30 to 60 minutes. Practical: line up two or three
     genuine commenters in advance. Cadence: 3 to 5 good posts a week beats
     daily low-value posts; if daily, leave about 12 hours between posts,
     about 3 hashtags. A newer LinkedIn feed (March 2026) can surface topic-
     relevant posts to people outside the network.
  5. YouTube second, once there is something to demo: short screen
     recordings answering one question each. It is the second largest
     search engine. Feeds the same SEO and AI-answer layer.
  6. X: not required for the target audience. Skipped.
  7. Facebook: skipped, except trade-specific groups (owner's initial view).
Supporting items:
  - Coming-soon page: add an email capture and start the content now. Do
    not give launch dates; frame around the problem, so a slow build does
    not damage credibility.
  - One-upload cross-posting tools exist and have free tiers (search named
    Socialync, ShortSync, Upload-Post, Bytecap, Clip Dash). None tested or
    chosen. A per-platform caption override is the feature to look for.
  - Judge a channel only after about six months of consistent effort; the
    first two or three months usually look silent.
  - Reviews: Xero publishes a "Managing app reviews" guide (where, when and
    how to ask). Ask after a concrete result, such as a dollar figure Mirra
    surfaced for that business.

7. STORAGE SIZING AND STRESS-TEST SEEDING (answered in the same discussion)
===============================================================================
  - localStorage is per browser, per origin (whole site, not per page),
    local to the device, about 5 to 10 MB. Rough size for 100 staff, 25
    assets, 12 months of P&L at 150 lines, weekly timesheets for all staff,
    and 20 purchase orders a month: about 1.2 MB. The record shapes in that
    estimate were ASSUMED, not measured from the app. So storage size is not
    the binding limit; the database is needed for durability, multiple
    devices and live Xero history.
  - The database needs a stable ID on every record and references by ID, not
    array position. Several shapes were recalled as already having IDs
    (staff type, asset, cost allocation group link, calculator asset link,
    P&L line). That was from memory of the briefs, NOT audited. Audit ID
    coverage when the database step (build order step 5) starts.
  - Stress testing by seeding: a JSON file that a console script loads into
    localStorage keys is feasible, and 20 staff and 5 assets or 1,000 staff
    is only more of the same arrays. Care point: the shape must match what
    each loader expects, because a selector that rebuilds an object silently
    drops fields it does not list (conventions bug class 6b). Calculation
    cost with large staff counts re-running on every render is the likely
    first failure, not storage. Build order step 6 already says to stress
    test the server-migrated version, so the seed approach needs a second
    form once calculation moves server-side.
