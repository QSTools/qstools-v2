1. THE BUSINESS (what is OBSERVED and what is ASSUMED)
===============================================================================
OBSERVED by the owner: a young business (about six months trading) reselling ready-mix concrete bought from other plants (no plant overhead) with a delivery arm; the owner is his customer and compared prices: he sells about 25 to 30 dollars a cube under the incumbent supplier (a 30 MPa pump mix: incumbent about 280, his price 250; modelling number 27.50 under); 12 trucks; three people (him, his wife, a dispatcher); one price per cube covers concrete and delivery; a small six-wheeler carries about 4.6 cubes, a bigger truck up to 5.6 to 5.8. He used to work for the incumbent, so he knows the contract drivers.
ASSUMED (to be replaced by his real answers): he owns the 12 mixer bowls and has financed them (a bowl is bought and fitted to a chassis; no published bowl price was found; a used 2014 whole truck was listed at 109,990 ex GST, new truck prices are quote only; placeholder 100,000 a bowl, 8.5 percent, 60 months); drivers own the trucks (a Scania G480 6x4 was seen on site; used G480 listings ran about 85,000 to 242,000) and are hired in at 120 an hour or 240 a load for a two hour cycle (an hour each way plus loading about 20 minutes plus about 25 minutes on site plus washout); wait time on site is passed through to the customer; three people at 100,000 each, all non-productive; insurance, bowl cleaning, phones and admin in overheads; drivers pay fuel and truck maintenance; a driver rate locked for a term, renegotiated at renewal; buy price from the plant 200 a cube (owner doubts he gets 160 to 180; realistic 200 to 230).
