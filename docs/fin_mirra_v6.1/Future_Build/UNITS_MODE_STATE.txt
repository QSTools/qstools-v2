1. WHAT IT IS
Units mode prices and tests a business that sells a countable thing (cubes, tonnes, litres, cups, km, loads): how many units at what price pay for everything. Cubes (outcome) play the role hours (capacity) play; the working unit becomes a LOAD. First case: the concrete reseller (single product, single unit). Second: the coffee cart. A new field joins the core only if one of those two forces it.
Chain: Setup (mode switch only) > P&L (unchanged) > Assets (capacity) > Labour/Overheads/Opening Hours/Cost Summary/Business Summary (unchanged) > Revenue/COGS (unit rows + items list) > Cost Allocation (cost of sales as a fourth assignment, stream on the working group) > Rate Builder (one line: price per cube) > Business Outcome (cubes sold, cost per cube, price per cube, break-even cubes; display layer first) > Model mode (levers, later).

2. DECIDED, BY MODULE
SETUP: mode switch only (labour_based / product_based). Units are picked per row in Revenue/COGS. No new field. Business Outcome does not read business_type (CONFIRMED in code, V6.1cf).
REVENUE / COGS:
 - ONE combined items list: name, unit, buy price, optional sell price, each price stored once; reuse what is sound in the existing unit rows after reading them (2026-11-11).
 - RATE CARD framing (Q-U1 YES 2026-11-11): price x cubes sold = EXPECTED cost of sales shown beside the P&L figure with a difference line. The P&L still owns cost of sales (REVENUE_COGS R2, R7). This supersedes the 2026-11-07 "rate card dropped".
 - Cubes sold is DERIVED = P&L revenue / average sale rate, never typed (Q-U12: typed only where no revenue exists, so a business can be modelled before it trades). Q-U5: derived at setup, measured when live; the screen says which. The difference line is the in-tool test of the derived count.
 - Stream (second tag): unit rows hold the stream list; roll-ups add only within a stream; one stream per working group is normal. Revenue share per stream stays a typed, flagged-unmeasured estimate until the live layer.
 - Hours is a unit; a pure hours business stays on hours mode. Unit list gains kilometre, litre, load, cup (Q-U7); custom stays. Hours row inside units mode kept minimal (Q-U8); review the form on screen.
 - Optional hours per load on the item (blank or zero = not set; Q-U8).
ASSETS: capacity = ONE field, ONE unit (the unit says per trip or per hour; Q-U10), maximum load, fixed property of the machine, product mode only. No quantity field: twelve bowls = twelve records. NEW (both modes): duplicate asset with sequential naming (ASSETS Addendum 7).
COST ALLOCATION: assigns, holds no figures. Cost of sales link is a FOURTH assignment kind; a LOAD is the working unit (concrete per load = capacity x buy price). Hired-in truck and driver = cost of sales, not Labour. Stream on each working group (link only; none = incomplete). Each bowl gets its own working group; DUPLICATE GROUP COPY = shell: overhead, cost of sales and stream copied, labour and assets NOT copied, assigned afterwards (the unassigned figure is the check).
 - Overhead (Q-U3, Q-U4): pool = overheads after the asset transfer in BOTH modes (fix C3b). Within an operating group overhead follows the group's driver (volume in units mode); between operating groups it is shared on direct cost, not revenue; one unit and one stream per operating group. C3b first step is a READ-ONLY look at who reads overhead_absorbed_cost in product mode, then owner chooses B (Cost Allocation always uses overheads only) or B plus a Recovery Summary rebuild (CA Addendum 17).
RATE BUILDER: one line, price per cube (concrete AND delivery); no cost build-up. Price auto-fills item price first, then stream average; sell price typed as markup % or fixed dollar margin; override allowed with a flag to update the price list; breakdown view display only (Q-U9). Shared pricing across assets = SHARED_CALCULATOR_ASSIGNMENT brief (not units specific).
BUSINESS OUTCOME: keeps its shape. Working group drill-down shows bowl, truck, cubes, cost per cube, price per cube, overhead share; the materials row stays the whole-business total (BO Addendum 19). Every figure: trace node + calc map entry. Q-U13 = a CHECK in the seed run (do concrete and truck land in one materials row?).
MODEL MODE: lever = changes a number that exists; scenario = adds/replaces something (truck add or replace; replace must retire the hired line). Three levers agreed (volume, buy price, sell price), also together (Q-U6). COGS lever is built in the units chat, not before; moves share mirra_diffs_v1 (BM Addendum 53 item 4). WHEN TO GROW (headline, later, both modes): break-even, buffer today and buffer eaten, on a rolling twelve months (Q-U11).
SCOPE: first outside demo = the concrete reseller end to end on the real chain (cubes sold, cost per cube, price per cube, break-even cubes); a SLICE of the real build, never a shortcut; Model price levers may wait. Per-SKU retail is out of scope. First slice of R4 is read-only (section 5).
