COST ALLOCATION GUIDANCE

Build working units before pricing

Cost Allocation is an input page. It assigns labour and asset pools into divisions and operating groups. It does not set prices.

1

Create division

Create the major operating area first.

2

Create operating group

Create the crew, team, or working unit inside the division.

3

Assign labour

Assign the staff or labour group used by that working unit.

4

Assign assets

Assign the productive assets used by that working unit.

5

Review pool position

Check what is assigned and what is still left to assign.

Next step

Use Recovery Summary and Rate Builder after the operating structure is built.

Recovery testing, rate building, pricing, and business outcome decisions happen downstream.
