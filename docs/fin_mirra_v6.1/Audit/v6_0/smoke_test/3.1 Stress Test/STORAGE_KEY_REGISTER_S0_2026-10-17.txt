3. HAZARDS FOR THE GENERATOR (each one looks fine and gives a wrong answer)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  H1  STAFF ARE POOLED BY STAFF TYPE. costAllocationLabourAdapter groups every active
      staff profile into one row per staff_type_id (summed hours and cost, weighted cost
      rate). A labour assignment assigns a percent of the WHOLE type pool to a group:
      assigned_hours = type available_hours x assignment_percent / 100
      (costAllocationLabourPoolBuilders.js L66-69). assigned_staff_count is NOT used in
      that sum. Consequence: two operators of the same type cannot be put in different
      groups person by person, only by splitting the type percent, or by giving them
      different staff types. Business Outcome rows, trace nodes and charge-out rates are
      also per type. The generator must choose: many custom staff types (one per crew or
      role) or few types with percent splits. These stress different paths. OWNER DECISION.
  H2  assignment_percent is ROUNDED TO AN INTEGER (Math.round, L58-61 and L264-266). A
      three-way split of 33.33 becomes 33 + 33 + 33 = 99, so 1 percent of the type's hours
      and cost goes nowhere. Hypothesis to test in the split-group business, not observed.
  H3  STORED VALUES THAT WIN OVER RECOMPUTE:
        - an assignment row carrying assigned_hours or assigned_cost overrides the
          computed value (L66-82). The seed must NOT write these fields.
        - saved asset running_cost_annual is read from storage, not recomputed (assetStorage
          L313). Check what reads it before the first seed (O-S0-3).
  H4  SOFT-DELETED ROWS ACCUMULATE. labour_group_assignments has 34 rows, 3 active, 31
      inactive history rows from moving staff. They are filtered by is_active. At scale,
      every reassignment adds rows; the seed should write active rows only, and the
      UI performance pass (brief 3.6) should note the growth.
  H5  MIXED TYPES. labour_rate is stored as a string in all three profiles ("050", "60",
      "50"); P&L amounts are numbers for some lines and strings for others ("2400"). The
      app coerces. The generator writes numbers, with one deliberate string case kept to
      prove coercion still works.
  H6  ABSENT VERSUS DEFAULT. Two of four groups have no group_recovery_hour_source,
      asset_run_pattern or labour_tied_to_asset field at all (older records). Seed both
      the absent and explicit forms in the second business.
  H7  An asset can be assigned to two groups at 100 percent each (one row is inactive
      today). Whether the chain caps an asset at 100 percent across groups is NOT read.
      Test in the shared-asset business (brief 3.5).
  H8  has_loaded guards: costAllocationStorage and assetStorage hydrate in an effect and
      only save after hydration. Seed with the app closed or reload immediately after
      writing; never write while a page is open (its save effect can overwrite the seed).
