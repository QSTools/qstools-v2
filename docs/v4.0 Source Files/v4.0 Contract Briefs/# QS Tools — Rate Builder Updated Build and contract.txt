# QS Tools — Rate Builder Updated Build Brief + Contract Brief

## Build name

Rate Builder — Recovery Link / Charge Cost Profit Integration

## Purpose

Rate Builder has now moved from a rate-entry tool into a working pricing and profit test engine.

It compares:

* customer charge
* real Cost Allocation recovery cost
* profit / shortfall
* margin
* profit per selected unit

The main user-facing answer is:

Charge | Cost | Profit / Loss

## Current Rate Builder role

Rate Builder should remain focused on testing one price or rate at a time.

Correct split:

Cost Allocation = what the operating group costs
Rate Builder = what this price/rate earns against that cost
Business Outcome = what the whole business looks like

## Current working flow

### 1. Customer Charge Calculator

Purpose: build the customer-facing price.

It owns:

* charge lines
* quantity
* unit
* total charge
* output driver
* effective charge rate per selected unit

Example:

20 m³ × $46.75 = $935.00

### 2. Labour Rates Builder

Purpose: define customer-facing labour charge-out rates.

It owns:

* current charge-out rate
* minimum recoverable charge-out rate
* labour-only cost
* margin after full recovery

Example:

Labour charge: $70.00/hr
Labour cost: $58.91/hr
Profit/Loss: $11.09/hr

Important rule:

Rate Builder must not invent or infer labour charge rates. Labour charge rates must come from Labour Rates Builder.

### 3. Recovery Link

Purpose: compare customer charge against real Cost Allocation recovery cost.

It owns the review view:

* selected Cost Allocation group
* selected calculator
* selected output unit
* recovery basis
* recovery hours
* charge / cost / profit result
* labour / asset / overhead cost build-up

## Contract rules

### Rule 1 — Rate Builder must not calculate Cost Allocation

Rate Builder consumes Cost Allocation output.

It must not recalculate:

* labour recovery cost
* asset recovery cost
* overhead recovery cost
* group recovery rate

### Rule 2 — Cost Allocation owns cost

Cost Allocation is the source of truth for:

* group_cost_per_hour
* labour_recovery_rate
* asset_recovery_rate
* overhead_recovery_rate
* total_group_cost

### Rule 3 — Customer Charge Calculator owns charge

Customer Charge Calculator is the source of truth for:

* total_charge
* output unit
* output quantity
* effective customer charge rate

### Rule 4 — Labour Rates Builder owns labour charge

Labour Rates Builder is the source of truth for:

* labour charge-out rate
* minimum recoverable labour rate
* labour-only cost
* labour margin after recovery

### Rule 5 — Recovery Link combines, but does not own, source data

Recovery Link calculates:

charge - cost = profit / shortfall

It should not mutate upstream Cost Allocation, Labour Rates Builder, or Customer Charge Calculator data.
