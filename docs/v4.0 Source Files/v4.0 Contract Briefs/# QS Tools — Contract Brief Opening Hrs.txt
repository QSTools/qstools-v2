# 1. Contract Purpose

This contract defines the correct system ownership, data flow, and calculation rules for the macro operating-hour baseline introduced through the Opening Hours upgrade.

The contract applies to:

```txt
Opening Hours
Cost Summary
Business Summary
Model Readiness display alignment
```

It also defines the boundary between the macro business baseline and the downstream recovery strategy layer.

---

# 2. Core Contract Rule

```txt
Opening Hours defines business availability.
Cost Summary defines the operating cost baseline.
Business Summary compares trading margin against that baseline.
Recovery Summary and Cost Allocation decide how that burden is recovered operationally.
```

The macro layer must use:

```txt
net_annual_business_open_hours
```

as the denominator for business-wide hourly reporting.

The macro layer must not use:

```txt
productive labour hours
selected recovery hours
operating recovery hours
```

as the business-wide open-hour denominator.

---

# 3. Module Ownership

## 3.1 Opening Hours owns

Opening Hours owns the business operating calendar.

It owns:

```txt
standard_weekly_open_hours
annual_open_weeks
annual_business_open_hours
public_holiday_closed_hours
additional_closed_hours
net_annual_business_open_hours
opening_hours_status
opening_hours_warnings
```

Opening Hours does not own:

```txt
productive labour hours
asset utilisation hours
selected recovery hours
recovery rates
cost allocation
pricing
business outcome
```

---

## 3.2 Cost Summary owns

Cost Summary owns the annual operating cost burden and the macro cost-per-open-hour baseline.

It owns:

```txt
total_people_cost_annual
total_asset_cost_annual
total_business_overheads
total_cost_burden
macro_required_operating_hour_rate
```

Correct formula:

```txt
macro_required_operating_hour_rate
= total_cost_burden / net_annual_business_open_hours
```

User-facing label:

```txt
Operating cost per open hour
```

Cost Summary must not describe this as:

```txt
Required Recovery Rate
Labour burden pressure
Operating Recovery Hours
productive labour hour rate
```

---

## 3.3 Business Summary owns

Business Summary owns the comparison between actual trading margin and the operating cost baseline.

It owns:

```txt
revenue
direct_costs
margin_pool
total_cost_burden
net_position
actual_margin_per_open_hour
deficit_or_surplus_per_open_hour
```

Correct formulas:

```txt
margin_pool = revenue - direct_costs
net_position = margin_pool - total_cost_burden
actual_margin_per_open_hour = margin_pool / net_annual_business_open_hours
deficit_or_surplus_per_open_hour =
  actual_margin_per_open_hour - macro_required_operating_hour_rate
```

User-facing labels:

```txt
Required operating cost per open hour
Actual margin per open hour
Deficit per open hour
Surplus per open hour
Net annual business open hours
```

Business Summary must not use visible macro labels such as:

```txt
Required Recovery Rate
Actual Recovery Rate
Recovery Hours Used
Operating Recovery Hours
productive labour hours
```

---

# 4. Macro Layer Data Flow

The required data flow is:

```txt
Opening Hours
→ net_annual_business_open_hours

Cost Summary
→ total_cost_burden
→ macro_required_operating_hour_rate

Business Summary
→ margin_pool
→ actual_margin_per_open_hour
→ deficit_or_surplus_per_open_hour
```

The flow must be orchestrated through hooks/selectors.

Components must not calculate business formulas directly.

---

# 5. Correct Macro Formulas

## 5.1 Opening Hours

```txt
net_annual_business_open_hours
= annual_business_open_hours
  - public_holiday_closed_hours
  - additional_closed_hours
```

## 5.2 Cost Summary

```txt
total_cost_burden
= total_people_cost_annual
  + total_asset_cost_annual
  + total_business_overheads
```

```txt
operating_cost_per_open_hour
= total_cost_burden / net_annual_business_open_hours
```

## 5.3 Business Summary

```txt
margin_pool = revenue - direct_costs
```

```txt
net_position = margin_pool - total_cost_burden
```

```txt
actual_margin_per_open_hour
= margin_pool / net_annual_business_open_hours
```

```txt
deficit_or_surplus_per_open_hour
= actual_margin_per_open_hour - operating_cost_per_open_hour
```

# 7. Recovery Layer Boundary

Recovery Summary is not part of the macro operating-hour baseline.

Recovery Summary belongs to the micro recovery strategy layer.

It may use:

```txt
selected recovery hours
productive labour hours
asset utilisation hours
unit drivers
material margin
allocation groups
```

Recovery Summary asks:

```txt
How will the business recover the burden operationally?
```

It does not redefine:

```txt
what the business costs per open hour
```

That belongs to Cost Summary.

---

# 8. Cost Allocation Boundary

Cost Allocation is downstream of Recovery Summary.

Cost Allocation should allocate recovery burden according to selected operational drivers.

It should not redefine the macro open-hour baseline.

Cost Allocation may consume:

```txt
selected recovery strategy
group recovery drivers
division/group allocation structure
productive labour drivers
asset drivers
material/unit drivers
```

But it must not replace:

```txt
net_annual_business_open_hours
```

as the macro denominator for Cost Summary or Business Summary.

---

# 9. Compatibility Fields

Some legacy fields may remain in output contracts for downstream compatibility:

```txt
required_recovery_rate
total_recovery_hours
recovery_hours_used
total_productive_output
```

These fields may continue to exist.

However:

```txt
They must not be used as the macro Cost Summary or Business Summary open-hour denominator.
```

They are only valid where the page is explicitly testing a recovery strategy, Labour productivity, or downstream cost allocation.


But pass/fail logic must remain strict.

# 14. Acceptance Criteria

This contract is satisfied when:

```txt
1. Opening Hours calculates net annual business open hours.
2. Cost Summary uses net annual business open hours for operating cost per open hour.
3. Business Summary uses net annual business open hours for hourly margin comparison.
4. Cost Summary no longer presents Labour/recovery hours as its macro denominator.
5. Business Summary no longer presents Labour/recovery hours as its macro denominator.
6. Recovery Summary remains untouched as the micro recovery strategy layer.
7. Cost Allocation remains downstream of Recovery Summary.
8. Model Readiness still blocks when module totals do not reconcile to P&L.
9. No UI component contains new business formulas.
10. Build passes.
```
